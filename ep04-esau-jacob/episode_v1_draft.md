# Why did God "hate" Esau and love Jacob, the liar?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 105
answer: "Esau I hated" is said centuries later about Esau's nation, Edom, and in the Bible "hated" can mean "loved less"; Paul uses it to show Jacob was chosen before birth, not because he was better.

## Hook
VO: Why did God "hate" Esau and love Jacob, the liar?
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID GOD "HATE" ESAU AND LOVE JACOB, THE LIAR?

## Stakes
VO: Jacob tricked his father, who could not see, into blessing him. Esau cried, "he has cheated me these two times." [Genesis 27:1, 27:18-29, 27:34-36]
VO: But "Esau I hated" isn't in Genesis. [Malachi 1:2-3; Romans 9:13]

## Story
VO: It's in Malachi, centuries later, about two nations: Jacob's people, Israel, and Esau's, Edom. "I have laid waste his hill country." [Malachi 1:1-4]

## Turn
VO: And in the Bible, "hated" can mean "loved less." Jacob "loved Rachel more than Leah." The next verse says Leah "was hated." [Genesis 29:30-31]
VERSE: "he loved Rachel more than Leah" — Genesis 29:30 (ESV)
VO: Paul quotes the line for one reason. God chose Jacob when the twins "had done nothing either good or bad," "not because of works." [Romans 9:10-13]
VO: Jacob wasn't chosen because he was better. Christians still debate whether Paul means salvation, or which family carries the promise. [Romans 9:6-13]

## Landing
VO: Esau wasn't blameless either. Edom sounds like "red," the stew he traded his birthright for. Genesis says he "despised his birthright." [Genesis 25:29-34]
VO: But God told Israel, "You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother." He even gave them land Israel could not take. [Deuteronomy 23:7; Deuteronomy 2:4-5]
VO: When Jacob came home afraid, Esau ran to meet him, kissed him, and they wept. [Genesis 32:7; 33:4]
VO: So why "Esau I hated"? It's said of Edom, the nation. "Hated" means loved less. And Paul's point is that Jacob was chosen before birth, not because he was better. The "hated" brother is the one who ran to embrace the liar. [Malachi 1:2-4; Genesis 29:30-31; Romans 9:11-13; Genesis 33:4]

## Question
VO: What surprised you more: Leah being "hated," or Esau running to Jacob? [Genesis 29:31; 33:4]

## Value
- "Esau I hated" isn't in Genesis; it's Malachi, centuries later, about the nation Edom [Malachi 1:2-4]
- Edom sounds like "red": Esau's people carry the name of the stew he traded his birthright for [Genesis 25:30, 25:34]
- The Bible uses "hated" for "loved less": Jacob "loved Rachel more than Leah," and Leah "was hated" [Genesis 29:30-31]
- Paul's point: Jacob was chosen before the twins "had done nothing either good or bad," "not because of works" [Romans 9:11-12]
- God told Israel, "You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother," and gave Esau's people Mount Seir [Deuteronomy 23:7; 2:5]
- Esau ran to meet the brother who cheated him, and they wept [Genesis 33:4]
