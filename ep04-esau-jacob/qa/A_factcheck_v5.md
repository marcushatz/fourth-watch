# ESV check of the v5 changes (the same AI fact-checker), Nov 1

All 9 lines VERIFIED.
- Gen 28:2 "Laban your mother's brother" (uncle).
- Gen 29:25 is exact.
- Gen 28:14 is exact; the LORD speaks in 28:13. Gen 28:4 "the blessing of Abraham to you" supports "promise to Abraham".
- Ruling: the promise line is what Genesis says Jacob was given, and it takes no side on Romans 9. Optional "In Genesis," to keep it from sounding like Paul's own answer. v6 uses it.
- Obadiah 10 is exact. "Jacob" means Judah/Jerusalem there (v11-12), so "Jacob's people" is fair, and v14 supports "turned on".
- Obadiah announces the judgment as future ("shall"), so v6 says "condemned".
- Ruling: "God chose Jacob to carry the promise" is fair under both readings of Romans 9. Paul frames election as promise (9:8). The individual-salvation reading says Paul means more than this but does not deny it.
- "The 'hated' brother" still stands.
