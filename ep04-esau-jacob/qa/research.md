# Ep 4 research: "Why is Esau hated more than Jacob?" (Nov 1)

Stage 1 of the first full one-prompt run (ENGINE_ROADMAP.md). The ESV wording below is from memory and is to be checked word for word by the fact-check agent before any line is locked.

## What people believe
- God "hated" Esau as a person, and it seems unfair because Jacob was the liar and cheat.
- Many assume the line is in Genesis.
- Some read Romans 9 as God hating Esau before he was born.

## What the text actually says (the dots)
1. **The line isn't in Genesis.** "Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated" comes from Malachi 1:2-3, quoted in Romans 9:13. Malachi speaks centuries after the twins. The verse goes on: "I have laid waste his hill country" (1:3), and Edom is named in 1:4. So it is about the nation Edom. Sources: renew.org "Why did God hate Esau?"; crosswalk.com "Why did God say he loved Jacob but hated Esau?"; christiancourier, whchurch.
2. **Edom = "red" = the stew.** Genesis 25:30 has "Let me eat some of that red stew... (Therefore his name was called Edom.)" The ESV note says Edom sounds like the Hebrew for red. So the nation in Malachi carries the name of the meal Esau traded his birthright for.
3. **Esau's own fault, in the text.** "Thus Esau despised his birthright" (Gen 25:34). Hebrews 12:16 says he "sold his birthright for a single meal".
4. **"Hated" can mean "loved less."**
   - Gen 29:30: Jacob "loved Rachel more than Leah"; 29:31 continues "When the LORD saw that Leah was hated".
   - Jesus in Luke 14:26 says to "hate" one's father and mother; Matt 10:37 has "loves father or mother more than me".
   - Source: renew.org (Luke 14:26 / Matt 10:37).
5. **Paul's point.** Romans 9:11-12 says the twins "were not yet born and had done nothing either good or bad... not because of works but because of him who calls". Jacob wasn't chosen for being better. Christians debate whether Romans 9 is about individual salvation or about which line carries the promise (Reformed vs corporate/national readings); the script names both in one line.
6. **Edom's later guilt.** Obadiah 10 says "Because of the violence done to your brother Jacob..." (renew.org cites Obadiah 10-14, and Num 20:20-21 for Edom refusing Israel passage). This was cut for room: it explains Malachi's judgment but adds a third time period.
7. **God protected Edom.**
   - Deut 2:4-5: "your brothers, the people of Esau... I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession". Israel may not take "so much as for the sole of the foot".
   - Deut 23:7: "You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother."
8. **Esau's embrace.** Gen 32:7 says Jacob was "greatly afraid". Gen 33:4 says "But Esau ran to meet him and embraced him and fell on his neck and kissed him, and they wept." Gen 33:9 adds "I have enough, my brother". Note that Genesis doesn't use the word "forgave", so the script says "embraced", not "forgave" (the Ep 3 lesson from John 21).

## Care
- Don't say God "didn't really hate" Esau. Malachi does describe judgment on Edom, so the script states what each text says.
- Don't claim to know Esau's eternal state.
- No "forgave".
