## Hook
VO: Why did God "hate" Esau and love Jacob, the liar?
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID GOD "HATE" ESAU AND LOVE JACOB, THE LIAR?

## Stakes
VO: Jacob tricked his father, who could not see, into blessing him. Esau "cried out with an exceedingly great and bitter cry." [Genesis 27:1, 27:18-29, 27:34]
VO: But "Esau I have hated" isn't in Genesis. [Malachi 1:2-3; Romans 9:13]

## Story
VO: It's from the prophet Malachi, centuries later, about the nations that came from them: Israel, and Edom, which God judged. [Malachi 1:1-4]

## Turn
VO: In the Bible, "hated" can mean "loved less." Look at Jacob. The liar got lied to, tricked into marrying Leah: "Why then have you deceived me?" [Genesis 29:16-25]
VO: He "loved Rachel more than Leah." The next verse says Leah "was hated." [Genesis 29:28-31]
VERSE: "he loved Rachel more than Leah" — Genesis 29:30 (ESV)
VO: Paul quotes the line about the twins themselves. The choice came when they "were not yet born and had done nothing either good or bad." [Romans 9:10-13]
VO: So God chose Jacob, but not because he was better. It wasn't a reward. [Romans 9:11-12]
VO: And in Moses' law, God told Israel, "You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother." [Deuteronomy 23:7]

## Landing
VO: Esau wasn't innocent. He "sold his birthright for a single meal." [Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 25:33-34]
VO: But twenty years after the trick, Jacob headed home, terrified of him. [Genesis 31:41; 32:7, 32:9-11]
VO: Esau ran to meet him, embraced him, kissed him, and they wept. [Genesis 33:4]
VO: So why "Esau I hated"? Because God chose Jacob, not because Jacob was better. And the "hated" brother is the one who ran to embrace the liar. [Malachi 1:2-3; Romans 9:10-13; Genesis 33:4]

## Value
- "Esau I have hated" isn't in Genesis; it's the prophet Malachi, centuries later, about the nations Israel and Edom [Malachi 1:2-4]
- The liar got lied to: Jacob was tricked into marrying Leah and asked, "Why then have you deceived me?" [Genesis 29:23-25]
- The Bible uses "hated" for "loved less": Jacob "loved Rachel more than Leah," and the next verse says Leah "was hated" [Genesis 29:30-31]
- Paul's point: God chose Jacob when the twins "were not yet born and had done nothing either good or bad," so it wasn't a reward [Romans 9:11-12]
- Moses' law told Israel, "You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother" [Deuteronomy 23:7]
- Twenty years after the trick, Esau ran to meet the brother who cheated him, embraced him, and they wept [Genesis 31:41; 33:4]

## Caption
Jacob lied. So why "Esau I hated"? Christians differ on whether Romans 9 is about salvation or about Jacob's family line; both agree Jacob didn't earn it. Read it yourself: Genesis 27, 29 and 33; Malachi 1:2-4; Romans 9:10-13; Deuteronomy 23:7.

Did you know Leah was called "hated"?
