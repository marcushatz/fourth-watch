## Hook
VO: Why did God "hate" Esau and love Jacob, the liar?
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID GOD "HATE" ESAU AND LOVE JACOB, THE LIAR?

## Stakes
VO: Jacob tricked his father, who could not see, into blessing him. Esau cried, "He took away my birthright, and behold, now he has taken away my blessing." [Genesis 27:1, 27:18-29, 27:34-36]
VO: But "Esau I hated" isn't in Genesis. [Malachi 1:2-3; Romans 9:13]

## Story
VO: It's from the prophet Malachi, centuries later, and it's about two nations: Israel, from Jacob, and Edom, from Esau. [Malachi 1:1-4]

## Turn
VO: In the Bible, "hated" can mean "loved less." Jacob married two sisters. He "loved Rachel more than Leah." The next verse says Leah "was hated." [Genesis 29:30-31]
VERSE: "he loved Rachel more than Leah" — Genesis 29:30 (ESV)
VO: Paul, writing to the Romans, uses the line for one point. God chose Jacob when the twins "were not yet born and had done nothing either good or bad." [Romans 9:10-13]
VO: Why? So it would rest "not because of works but because of him who calls." [Romans 9:11-12]
VO: Christians debate whether Paul means salvation, or Jacob's family line. Either way, Jacob didn't earn it. [Romans 9:6-13]

## Landing
VO: Esau wasn't innocent. He "sold his birthright for a single meal." [Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 25:33-34]
VO: But God told Israel not to fight Edom for its land, and "You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother." [Deuteronomy 2:4-5; 23:7]
VO: Twenty years later, Jacob came home terrified of his brother. [Genesis 31:41; 32:7, 32:11]
VO: Esau ran to meet him, embraced him, kissed him, and they wept. [Genesis 33:4]
VO: So why "Esau I hated"? It means God chose Jacob's line, not that Jacob was better. And the "hated" brother is the one who ran to embrace the liar. [Malachi 1:2-3; Romans 9:11-13; Genesis 33:4]

## Question
VO: Was Esau the better brother? [Genesis 25:34; 33:4]

## Value
- "Esau I hated" isn't in Genesis; it's the prophet Malachi, centuries later, about two nations, Israel and Edom [Malachi 1:2-4]
- The Bible uses "hated" for "loved less": Jacob "loved Rachel more than Leah," and the next verse says Leah "was hated" [Genesis 29:30-31]
- Paul's point: God chose Jacob when the twins "were not yet born and had done nothing either good or bad," "not because of works but because of him who calls" [Romans 9:11-12]
- God told Israel not to fight Edom for its land, and "You shall not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother" [Deuteronomy 2:4-5; 23:7]
- Twenty years later, Esau ran to meet the brother who cheated him, embraced him, and they wept [Genesis 31:41; 33:4]
