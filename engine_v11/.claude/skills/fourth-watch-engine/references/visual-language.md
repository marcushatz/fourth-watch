# Production design, lighting and phone readability

## Medium selection: information before novelty
| Need | Preferred medium | Failure and workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Legible human action and reactions | 2D/2.5D rigged figures, limited 3D | avoid paper-doll limb movement; custom pose for meaningful beats |
| Geographic change / scale | layered 2.5D, low-poly 3D | use recognizable fixed landmarks, not random aerial shots |
| Relational logic, chronology or comparisons | graph/map/timeline diagram | animate the *relation* as evidence arrives |
| Quotations / a specific word | text-as-object with highlighted phrase | use typography to support spoken evidence, not replace whole video |
| One tactile significant object | stylized 3D close insert | keep scale and source fact credible |
| Memory, spiritual awe, transition | restrained metaphorical light/composition | symbolism must not impersonate a narrated biblical event |

## Established channel palette, conditionally applied
`#14100D` night, `#1C2433` blue, `#E3B25C` warm gold, `#F2E6CF` text/cream, `#6B4A33` clay, `#C2502E` ember. Maintain coherence through contrast, material texture and consistent character design; all scenes must not be dark. Night scenes must be readable. Gold is precious: if every character glows, the narrative symbol is meaningless.

## Mobile-first visual pass (evaluate at actual display size)
- 9:16 master 1080x1920; essential action typically centered clear of UI overlays at top and bottom, and right-side engagement icons. Safe area must be checked *per platform and placement*, not by assuming one universal pixel rectangle.
- Readability at downsampled 360x640: inspect value hierarchy and smallest text, then test overlay UI if available.
- Deliberate one focal event per visual beat. The background can be rich, but competing contrast should be subordinate.
- Foreground / subject / middle / far separation gives parallax; don't fake depth with indiscriminate blur.
- Lighting direction should remain coherent from shot to shot unless time/space changes. A single key may be joined by motivated practical lights.
- For faceless subjects, emotion comes from posture, silhouette, distance between bodies, speed, hesitation, action and response, and sound—not an invented face.
- Frame-by-frame darkness, text collisions, body clipping, color gamut, and object penetration must be checked on rendered frames.

## Visual motifs: the payoff mechanism
Motif must begin concrete, undergo a meaningful change, then return with new significance. Candidate sea motifs: a distant point of light / direction of boat / hand or reaching gesture / distance from shore. An abstract symbol that exists only for decoration doesn't count. Do not appropriate Lintel Network's distinctive door/lintel identity.

## Motion graphics grammar
- Graph grows from the first factual datum, not instant full diagram.
- Labels enter on the corresponding spoken noun; data line connects when the causal claim is spoken.
- Use arrow semantics consistently: motion arrow for travel, dotted arrow for inferred relation, double arrow for comparison. Do not visually turn hypotheses into proven equality.
- Long diagrams can hold if they undergo meaningful internal state changes. Fast cuts do not substitute for those changes.

## Reference-based reality check
Existing video studies record BibleProject hits with both continuous camera journeys and relatively static, composition-building motion graphics. Bible Animations hits rely heavily on figures entering/props appearing within held scenes. Neither supports mandating perpetual camera movement or a single style.
