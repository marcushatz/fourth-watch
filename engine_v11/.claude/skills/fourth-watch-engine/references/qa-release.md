# QA and release protocol

## Mandatory checks
- Evidence: all high-impact statements and implied biblical visual claims source-labeled, no unsupported claims in approved script; no unlicensed third-party narration, music, art or footage.
- Story: central audience question not manufactured; each scene changes what viewer knows/feels/sees; middle cannot be arbitrarily rearranged if the story claims to be causal; ending earns its emotional peak.
- Picture: readable silhouettes at phone scale, no missing first frames, no artifacting, no UI collision, no clipping, no duplicate placeholder assets presented as final.
- Camera: every big move has a story job; no wandering horizon, unjustified spins or axis confusions.
- Motion: body-ground/hand-object contact, easing, true reactions, continuity across cuts.
- Audio: correct dialogue, no words dropped, voice above music on phone, no clipping, sensible relative loudness, meaningful sonic changes.
- Technical: correct fps/aspect/frame count, stable decode, no long unintentional frozen sections, clean joins, captions text/sync verified, licensed credit/disclosure recorded.

## Human approval
The creator must evaluate motion as video with audio. A script or contact sheet is not proof of acting or mix quality. Get final explicit release approval; stage gate cannot be programmatically bypassed.

## Do not assert false QA precision
Frame difference and optical flow can flag stillness; they cannot tell whether the silence is dramatically right. Loudness meters cannot establish musical beauty. An LLM critic can identify mistakes but doesn't replace audience research. A unit test passing isn't evidence the film is masterpiece-level.

## Post-publication data
Store platform, post date, length, eligibility, views/impressions, viewed-vs-swiped (when offered), average watch time, completion or retention graph, sends, saves, shares, comments, follows, traffic source, first 48h and later snapshots. Compare only comparable cohorts. Record audience comments verbatim where permitted, with source link and privacy care. Retention becomes feedback, not a recipe for guaranteed viral performance.
