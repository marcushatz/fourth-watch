# Rights, attribution, and platform disclosure — verified 2026-11-04

## ESV (Crossway current policy)
Official: https://www.crossway.org/permissions/

The publicly stated ESV policy references **ESV Text Edition: 2025**. Crossway's standard use guidelines allow quotation up to 500 verses without a formal license **subject to conditions**, including no more than half any book, no more than 25% of total work text, and restrictions for commentary/biblical reference works. It specifies a full copyright notice for digital and audio works, and specifically requires **verbal credit to the ESV for audio uses**. Further conditions for Creative Commons publication and trademarks apply. The Fourth Watch legacy repo uses older boilerplate; this package deliberately does not blindly copy it. Verify with Crossway if the proposed use falls outside standard guidelines, especially on monetization, redistribution, or substantial quotation.

A local conservative word-count tool `scripts/script_rights_audit.py` identifies potential quotation-percentage issues, missing notices and items requiring human review. It is not a quotation verifier or legal clearance.

## YouTube AI disclosure, 2026
Official: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
Updated YouTube guidance distinguishes realistic synthetic/altered media from non-realistic animation and minor production assistance. A purely non-realistic code-animated Bible reel doesn't automatically require the same disclosure as photorealistic synthetic footage. AI music, realistic depictions and other use cases may be subject to disclosure. **Don't apply a blanket claim that every AI narrator or stylized animated reel must be labeled, or that none must.** Check the final media against the specific platform requirements in force during upload. Transparency can still be a voluntary editorial choice.

## Other platform policies
Instagram/TikTok terms and disclosure UI change. Verify directly immediately before release. Do not reuse YouTube's policy as if it automatically governs all platforms.

## Licenses
All source images, music samples, reference videos, generated voice assets and sound effects must have: origin, rights/license, transformations (if any), creator or provider, permitted platforms, expiry conditions and hash. Study of a creator's Short permits analysis, not copying their script, distinctive composition, voice or soundtrack.
