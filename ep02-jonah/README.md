## Nineveh (Marcus's call, Round 17: explain it with a separately sourced historical statement)

- **v5 says, at the first mention:** "Historically, Nineveh was one of Assyria's great cities." It's shown with a map and a source pill (HISTORY · WORLD HISTORY ENCYCLOPEDIA) that goes out before the line from the book begins.
- **The source:** World History Encyclopedia, "Nineveh" (Joshua J. Mark), opened Oct 31. It calls Nineveh "one of the oldest and greatest cities in antiquity" and says Sennacherib later made it the capital. That is why the line says "one of Assyria's great cities", not "the capital".
- **A longer version was tested and dropped:** "…and Assyria later conquered Israel's northern kingdom" (2 Kings 17:6). Placed after "the people of Nineveh believed God", both AI reviewers said it implied a motive the book never gives. The fact-checker's reason was that the conquest came decades after Jonah. The simulated viewer said it came away thinking the video was hinting "he hated them for what Assyria did". If you want that clause back, it needs the fact-checker's wording: "…and decades after Jonah, Assyria conquered Israel's northern kingdom. The book of Jonah never gives that as his reason." That adds about 8 s. Saved replies: `qa/round5/`.
