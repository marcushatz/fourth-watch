ESV check, Jonah lines A–F. All four quotes match the ESV word for word. C and F each need one small rewording.

**A. VERIFIED.** 1:2 reads "call out against it, for their evil has come up before me," so "because of their evil" is fair. In 1:3 he found the ship, then "paid the fare," so the order is right. The ESV phrases used here are not in quote marks but read as narration, so that is fine.

**B. VERIFIED.** 3:10: "When God saw what they did, how they turned from their evil way, God relented of the disaster… and he did not do it."

**C. OVERCLAIM (small).**
- **Quotes:** both are exact. In "slow to anger… and relenting from disaster" the ellipsis stands in for "and abounding in steadfast love," (4:2). "it displeased Jonah exceedingly" is exact (4:1).
- **Paraphrases:** "When God spared Nineveh" fairly describes the "it" in 4:1, which is 3:10. "He ran because he knew" fairly restates "That is why I made haste to flee… for I knew."
- **Problem:** "it wasn't fear" claims Jonah ruled out fear. He never does; he only gives another reason. Also, in 1:9 Jonah says "I fear the LORD." That is reverence, but a viewer could quote it.
- **Fix:** "So the reason Jonah gives isn't fear. He says he ran because he knew God is 'slow to anger… and relenting from disaster.' And when God spared Nineveh, 'it displeased Jonah exceedingly.'"

**D. VERIFIED.** This is opinion. Its only factual point, "the fish," matches 1:17 ("a great fish… the belly of the fish").

**E. VERIFIED.** 4:3: "please take my life from me, for it is better for me to die than to live." The same words come again in 4:8.

**F. OVERCLAIM (small).**
- **Correct:** "Made a plant grow to shade him" fits 4:6. "Overjoyed" fits "exceedingly glad." "At dawn" is right (4:7: "when dawn came up the next day"). "Appointed a worm" matches the ESV verb, and the order is right.
- **Problem:** in 4:7–9 the narrator never says Jonah was angry. The anger comes only through God's question and Jonah's reply in 4:9: "Yes, I do well to be angry, angry enough to die." The line also skips 4:8 (east wind, faintness, asking to die), so "angry again" right after "withered" squeezes the order.
- **Fix:** "At dawn, God appointed a worm, and the plant withered. Jonah said he was 'angry enough to die.'"
