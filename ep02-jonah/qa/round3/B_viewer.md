**1. Question and answer:** The question was why Jonah ran from God. The answer: he didn't run because he was scared. He ran because he knew God would forgive Nineveh, and he didn't want that. I'd rate it 4/5. The line "It wasn't fear, he knew God was merciful" is clear. What's missing is why he'd hate Nineveh. The video never says who they were.

**2. Swipe point:** I didn't swipe, but I got closest around 1:05 at "God made a plant grow to shade him." My question had already been answered, and the plant and worm part comes fast, like a list. "The ending is stranger than the fish" is what kept me watching.

**3. Confusing lines:**
- "Notice his message… One sentence." Notice what? It never told me why one sentence matters.
- "Yet forty days." It sounds old, and I had to work out what "yet" meant.
- "Made haste to flee" and "relenting from disaster" both sound old-fashioned.
- "God appointed a worm" sounds odd.
- I didn't know who Nineveh was or why Jonah would want them destroyed.
- "And also much cattle" was random, but kind of funny.

**4. Repetitive or preachy:** "Slow to anger" comes up three times. The recap "So why did Jonah run? Because…" felt like a rerun. "Slow enough to spare Nineveh, and to keep reasoning with Jonah" leans a little sermon, but it's gentle. Nothing felt judgmental.

**5. Strongest and weakest:**
- Strongest: "We never hear Jonah answer." It's a close second to the reveal of Jonah's own reason.
- Weakest: "Notice his message, as the book records it." It sets something up and never pays it off.

**6. What I learned:**
- Jonah explains his own reason, and it's not fear.
- He was mad that God spared Nineveh.
- He asked to die.
- The plant and worm story.
- His whole message to Nineveh was one sentence.
- The book ends on God's question.
- The 120,000 people, plus the cattle.

**7. Did the ending land?** Mostly yes. The two buttons make it easy to answer. I'd comment: "Jonah. Bro was mad God was too nice 💀"

**8. Watch and share:** Yes, I'd watch to the end. I'd send it to my friend from youth group who always says she "knows the Jonah story."

**9. Fixes:**
- **Nineveh is never introduced:** Early on, add "Nineveh, the great city of Assyria, Israel's enemy."
- **The one-sentence message goes nowhere:** Pay it off with "Five words in Hebrew. No 'turn back,' no offer of mercy." Or cut "notice."
- **"Yet forty days":** Have the narrator say "Forty more days, and Nineveh will be overthrown."
- **Old wording:** Keep the King James wording on the verse card, but use modern words out loud ("hurried to run," "holding back disaster").
- **"Appointed a worm":** Change it to "sent a worm."
- **"Slow to anger" three times:** In the recap, say "Because he knew God would forgive them, and he didn't want Him to." That also states the motive outright.
- **Plant section drags:** Trim it to two short beats: plant shades him, worm kills it, Jonah's angry.
