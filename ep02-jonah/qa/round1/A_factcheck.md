**Jonah Ep 2 ESV fact-check. Six lines are verified, seven overclaim, one needs a correction, and the verse card is exact.**

**Voiceover lines**
1. VERIFIED.
2. VERIFIED. 1:2 goes on: "Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city…". In the caption, write "Arise, go to Nineveh…" because the sentence continues past "Nineveh".
3. OVERCLAIM. In 4:2 Jonah "prayed to the LORD". He is talking to God, not to "us". Fix: "But Jonah gives his reason himself."
4. VERIFIED. This is the right order: 1:4, 1:17, then 3:3. Optional: in 3:1–3 he goes after "the word of the LORD came to Jonah the second time."
5. OVERCLAIM. Chapter 3 records only this one sentence, but 3:2 says "the message that I tell you", and the text never says this was all he said. Fix: "But notice his message, as the book records it. One sentence: 'Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown!'" The quote itself is exact.
6. VERIFIED. Exact quote from 3:5.
7. VERIFIED as a paraphrase, but it sounds like ESV wording. The ESV says "how they turned", not "turn". If this goes on screen, use "how they turned from their evil way… and he did not do it."
8. OVERCLAIM. 4:2 is the first place a reason is given. In 1:10 he only told the sailors "that he was fleeing". But in 4:2 Jonah says, "is not this what I said when I was yet in my country?", so he claims he said it before. Fix: "And only now do we hear why he ran."
9. OVERCLAIM. "Didn't want that mercy" is an inferred motive. Fix: "Because he knew God was 'gracious… and merciful… and relenting from disaster.' And when God spared Nineveh, 'it displeased Jonah exceedingly.'"
10. OVERCLAIM. The quote is exact (4:3, lowercase "it"). But 4:3 opens with "Therefore", which ties the request to 4:2, not to how angry he was. Fix: "Then he asked God to take his life: 'it is better for me to die than to live.'"
11. CORRECTION. The ESV never says the worm "killed" the plant, and the line leaves out that God sent the worm. The ESV wording is "God appointed a worm that attacked the plant, so that it withered" (4:7). The other verbs are "appointed a plant and made it come up over Jonah" and "exceedingly glad" (4:6). "Angry again" holds because of 4:9: "Yes, I do well to be angry, angry enough to die." Fix: "At dawn the next day, God appointed a worm that attacked the plant, so that it withered. And Jonah said, 'I do well to be angry, angry enough to die.'"
12. OVERCLAIM. "You pity a plant you didn't even grow" is not marked as a quote, but it sits next to a real quote and sounds like scripture. Fix: God said, "You pity the plant, for which you did not labor, nor did you make it grow… And should not I pity Nineveh, that great city…?" In 4:11 the question mark comes only after "cattle", so the quote needs the ellipsis.
13. VERIFIED. 4:11 is the last verse of the book, and Jonah never replies. "and also much cattle" is exact. Optional: say "persons", the ESV word, instead of "people".
14. OVERCLAIM. In 2:6 Jonah is speaking to God, from the belly of the fish (2:1): "yet you brought up my life from the pit, O LORD my God." The line's word order, "brought his life up", is not the ESV's. "Because God is merciful" also drops "he knew". Fix: "Because he knew God is merciful. And the God he ran from is the God Jonah said 'brought up my life from the pit.'"
15. VERIFIED.

**Verse card:** exact. It is 4:2 word for word and ends on the ESV's own comma. You could end it with "…".

**Pictures**
- a. PROBLEM (minor). Joppa, finding the ship and paying the fare are all in 1:3. The text gives no time of day, so "dusk" is added. It also doesn't say who he paid, so make that payee generic.
- b. OK. The storm is 1:4, "hurled him into the sea" is 1:15, the fish is 1:17 and "vomited Jonah out upon the dry land" is 2:10.
- c. OK. In 3:4 he "began to go into the city, going a day's journey. And he called out."
- d. PROBLEM. "From the king down" reverses the order in the text:
  - The people put on sackcloth first, "from the greatest of them to the least of them" (3:5).
  - Then "the word reached the king", and he put on sackcloth and "sat in ashes" (3:6).
  - Animals in sackcloth appear only in the king's decree, "let man and beast be covered" (3:8). The text never says it happened, so don't show animals in sackcloth.
- e. OK. The booth to the east of the city is 4:5. The plant, the worm at dawn and the withering are 4:6–7. The scorching east wind and the sun beating on his head are 4:8.
- f. OK. God relented in 3:10, and the persons and cattle are in 4:11. Don't show Jonah answering or leaving.
