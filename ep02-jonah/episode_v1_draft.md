# Why did Jonah run from God instead of going to Nineveh?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 105
answer: Jonah says it himself: he ran because he knew God is gracious and merciful, and he did not want that mercy for Nineveh.

## Hook
VO: Why did Jonah run from God instead of going to Nineveh?
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID JONAH RUN FROM GOD INSTEAD OF GOING TO NINEVEH?
VISUAL: Joppa dock at dusk. Jonah's hand drops coins into a ship-master's palm on "run"; the mooring rope slips free on "Nineveh" and the ship pulls away, the camera pulling back to the harbour.

## Stakes
VO: God told him, "Arise, go to Nineveh." Jonah found a ship going to Tarshish instead, and paid the fare. [Jonah 1:2-3]
VO: It's easy to guess he was afraid. But Jonah tells us his reason himself. [Jonah 4:2]

## Story
VO: Then came the storm, the sailors, and three days and three nights in a great fish. After that, he went to Nineveh. [Jonah 1:4-17, 3:3]

## Turn
VO: But notice his whole message. One sentence: "Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown!" [Jonah 3:4]
VO: And the people of Nineveh believed God. [Jonah 3:5]
VO: When God saw them turn from their evil way, He relented, and did not do it. [Jonah 3:10]
VO: That made Jonah angry. And only now does he say why he ran. [Jonah 4:1-2]
VERSE: "That is why I made haste to flee to Tarshish; for I knew that you are a gracious God and merciful," — Jonah 4:2 (ESV)
VO: So why did Jonah run? Because he knew God was merciful, and he didn't want that mercy for Nineveh. [Jonah 4:1-2]

## Landing
VO: He was so angry he asked to die: "it is better for me to die than to live." [Jonah 4:3]
VO: Then God made a plant grow to shade him, and Jonah was exceedingly glad. The next day, a worm killed it, and Jonah was angry again. [Jonah 4:6-9]
VO: God said, You pity a plant you didn't even grow. "And should not I pity Nineveh, that great city?" [Jonah 4:10-11]
VO: More than 120,000 people, "and also much cattle." The book ends right there, on God's question. We never hear Jonah answer. [Jonah 4:11]
VO: So why did Jonah run from God? Because God is merciful. And the God he ran from is the God who brought his life up from the pit. [Jonah 4:2, 2:6]

## Question
VO: Who surprises you more in this story: Jonah, or the people of Nineveh? [Jonah 3:5, 4:1]

## Value
- Jonah gives his own reason for running, and it is God's mercy, not fear of Nineveh [Jonah 4:2]
- His whole recorded message to Nineveh is one sentence, and the city believed God [Jonah 3:4-5]
- The book ends on God's unanswered question about 120,000 people "and also much cattle" [Jonah 4:11]

## Caption
Why did Jonah run? He tells us himself. Read Jonah 4:1–11, then chapter 3.
