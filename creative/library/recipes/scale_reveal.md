# Scale reveal

- **Job:** make the size of the place, or of the event, register.
- **Picture:**
  - cut from a close size straight to an XWS on the word that names the big thing ("building", "city", "sea");
  - the subject stays small and central, so the eye finds it first, then the scale around it.
- **Timing:** a 0.5 s place read (same set) before the big event lands; then the event; a slow pull-out of 8% while the consequence spreads.
- **Code:** a `hallWide`-style mark; `DIRECT.path` from the mark to `mark(..., { push: -0.08 })` with `ease: 'out'`.
- **Example:** pillars sdD. The whole house of Dagon, about 3,000 on the roof, Samson tiny between the middle pillars; it gives on "building" and falls on "fell".
- **Trap:** cutting to the wide on the event word itself leaves no time to read the place. Cut 0.5 s earlier.
