# Cause and effect in one frame

- **Job:** make it obvious that the character's action causes the consequence.
- **The muted test of pass 1 read "the roof falls; nothing tells me it was his doing".**
- **Picture:** the cause and the first effect share a frame:
  - his whole body and both pillars (low MS);
  - he drives on the verb, and the pillars lean under him in the same shot.
  - Only then cut to the wide for the full consequence.
- **Timing:** the effect starts on the drive (the verb's word) and grows until the give. Never cut away between cause and first effect.
- **Code:** dagon `T.lean0` = the drive beat; the drums' `strainLean` amplitude is 0.06 rad (vs 0.022 when only creaking). `twoPosts` mark.
- **Example:** pillars sdB-sdC (final).
