# Crane up the force

- **Job:** show where a push or a weight goes (into the stone, up to the roof) without inventing an event.
- **Picture:** low, at the point of contact (the hand on the pillar), then a crane up to what carries the load (the capital, the roof edge with the crowd).
- **Timing:**
  - the crane starts 0.12 s after the cut and takes 1.1 s, ease in-out;
  - the structure shifts under the force (the pillars lean outward).
- **Code:** two marks with the same position and different look-ats (`pillarLo` → `pillarUp`), then `DIRECT.path`.
- **Example:** pillars sdC ("with all his strength").
- **Keep the subject in the frame.** The muted test of pass 1 couldn't read a macro shot of a wrist and a pillar. Low, with him at the bottom and the capitals above, it read at once.
- **Fact-check:** sand pouring from the capitals before the house gives is an invented intermediate beat. No sand before the give.
- **Trap:** a foreground figure in the crane's path (a lord) steals the read. Move the camera inside the pillar's line.
