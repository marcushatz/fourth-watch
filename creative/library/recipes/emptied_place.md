# The emptied place (before/after)

- **Job:** carry a death or a loss without showing a body, and give the sequence its stillness.
- **Picture:** the place where the person stood, after the event. A new mark, one size different from the wide (no jump cut); a slow push of 3%; the dust settling.
- **Timing:** the payoff read is ≥ 1.2 s. It can hold on under the next line's verse card.
- **Code:** dagon `settle`: the puffs shrink to nothing and the haze thins to 30% over 1.3 s, 2.3 s after the impact. The `stumps` mark.
- **Learned:** the plan was a top-down view of two broken stumps. In the render, top-down sat inside the dust and the roof covered the stumps. The eye-level emptied place read clearly.
- **Example:** pillars sdE ("Samson died with them").
