# A prayer the text doesn't answer

- **Job:** show asking, and leave the answer as open as the text leaves it.
- **Picture:** high angle (weak), the figure small; the head lifts toward the upper left on the verb of asking.
- **No light change.** Gold only where the text says God acted. Judges 16:28-30 never says God answered, so there's no gold at the pillars (07 §6).
- **Code:** PP2 `pray` with `pitch` from 0.38 (bowed) to -0.72 (lifted) over 0.5 s on the beat.
- **Trap (seen in the demo):** with the blind band, a head lift alone is a weak read at MS. Pair it with a size change, or make the hands' contact on the stone the read.
- **Example:** pillars sdA ("Now, he asked God for strength").
