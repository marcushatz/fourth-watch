# Irony in the background

- **Job:** show that the people around don't know what is happening (a prayer while they laugh) without a word or a face.
- **Picture:** the subject sharp and central; the oblivious party in soft focus behind, still doing their thing (laughing, pointing, cheering).
- **Rule:** only if the text never says they stopped. In Judges 16:25-30 the text never mentions a hush, so v57's "hush on called" was cut.
- **Example:** pillars sdA and sdB: the lords keep laughing and pointing while he prays, then while he pushes.
