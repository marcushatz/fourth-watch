# Collapse run (a death or a judgment, no gore)

- **Job:** a big physical consequence (a house, a wall, a tower) that lands on its words.
- **Picture:**
  - the cause, close (sdB, sdC);
  - the wide for the fall (sdD);
  - the aftermath (sdE).
- **Timing:**
  - the give on the noun ("building");
  - the impact on the verb ("fell"), with a shake ≤ 12 px over 0.45 s and a brief flash;
  - figures topple into the dust;
  - nobody is shown under the stones.
- **Code:** `dagon.js`'s analytic collapse. Drums, roof pieces, rubble, puffs, haze and trickle are closed-form functions of t from beat times, so any beat set re-times the whole fall. `DIRECT.shake(t, T.land)`.
- **Reusable for:** Jericho's wall, the tower of Babel, the temple curtain (a tear, not a fall).
