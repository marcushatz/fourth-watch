Question (on-screen title): How did something God commanded become an idol?
Answer: People held on to the object and forgot what it pointed to. Hezekiah destroyed the object; Jesus kept its meaning.
Thread: the bronze serpent, with the golden calf as its mirror.

Chain:
  D1 The calf (not commanded) and the serpent (commanded) were both worshiped and destroyed → the viewer asks: how can God's own command become an idol?
  D2 Jesus used the destroyed serpent two verses before John 3:16 → why bring back something that was smashed?
  D3 Origin: God commanded a "fiery serpent"; Moses made it of bronze; look and live → so it really was God's idea

  Re-read: Hezekiah's smash isn't the end of the story. The object went; its meaning was waiting.
