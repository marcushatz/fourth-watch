## The chain

1. Why would Jesus reach for the enemy's symbol?
2. Israel asks God to take the snakes away, and God orders one made. Plant: seraph.
3. The cure becomes an idol and a king smashes it. Why bring it back?
4. Midpoint: he isn't the snake. He's lifted up to die, and the dying look and live. Why call an execution being lifted up?
5. Isaiah got there first: a servant high and lifted up, pierced, and his wounds heal.
6. The same words describe a throne. Seraph pays off: a seraph carries the cure to a man who says "I am lost." John says Isaiah saw Jesus' glory.
7. Echo: the bitten looked at the shape of what was killing them. The cross looks like death winning, and Jesus calls it glory.
8. "They will look." Nicodemus comes back with burial spices.

**Answer:** So he would be lifted up to die, and anyone dying could look and live.
