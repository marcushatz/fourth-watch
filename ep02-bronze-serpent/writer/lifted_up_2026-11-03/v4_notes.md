Marcus paused this episode while the debut is decided. These notes keep the rework, and no v4 script was written. `candidate_v3.md` is the latest draft.

**His notes for v4:** two or three discoveries that build; the seraph link can't carry the climax; Isaiah and John must escalate; Nicodemus stays only if planted; the calf can go.

**The chain to try:**
1. Hook: the snake is the enemy's symbol, it became an idol, and a king smashed it. Why reach for it?
2. Plant: he said it at night to Nicodemus, whose last words that night are "How can these things be?" (John 3:9).
3. Israel asked to lose the snakes, and God had one lifted up.
4. Midpoint: "lifted up" meant a kind of death. Most Christians know this.
5. First new step: Isaiah's servant is "high and lifted up", then pierced.
6. Second step: the same words describe the throne, and John quotes both chapters, then says Isaiah saw his glory. A pole, a servant, a throne.
7. Answer: the cross looks like death winning, and Jesus calls it glory.
8. Payoff: Nicodemus returns with burial spices. John never says whether he got his answer.

**Cut:** seraph (word echo only); the calf line, unless Marcus wants it kept; "side by side".

**His test:** would someone who knows the foreshadowing be rewarded? The first 50 seconds mostly no. After that, yes.

**Greek note, if it helps:** Isaiah 52:13 in the Septuagint pairs the two verbs John uses for the cross, lifted up and glorified. Check a modern commentary before use.
