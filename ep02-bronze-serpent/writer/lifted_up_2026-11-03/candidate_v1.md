## Hook
VO: Why did Jesus compare his death to a snake on a pole? [John 3:14] [John 12:33]
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID JESUS COMPARE HIS DEATH TO A SNAKE ON A POLE?

## Stakes
VO: From Genesis on, the serpent is the enemy. Why reach for that? [Genesis 3:15]

## Story
VO: Snakes were killing Israel, and God told Moses, "Make a fiery serpent." [Numbers 21:6] [Numbers 21:8]

## Turn
VO: In Hebrew, that's one word: seraph. Keep that word. [Numbers 21:8]
VO: Moses made it, and whoever looked at it lived. [Numbers 21:9]
VO: Centuries later, people were making offerings to it. Moses' serpent had become Aaron's calf. So King Hezekiah broke it in pieces. [2 Kings 18:4]
VO: Centuries after that, Jesus brings it back, at night, to a Pharisee named Nicodemus. [John 3:1] [John 3:2]
VO: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in him may have eternal life." [John 3:14] [John 3:15]
VERSE: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in him may have eternal life." — John 3:14-15 (ESV)
VO: John later says "lifted up" showed "by what kind of death he was going to die." [John 12:32] [John 12:33]
VO: So he would be lifted up to die, and the dying would only have to look. [Numbers 21:9]
VO: But why call an execution being lifted up? [John 12:33]
VO: Because Isaiah got there first. [Isaiah 52:13]
VO: He wrote that God's servant "shall be high and lifted up," then "pierced," and "with his wounds we are healed." [Isaiah 52:13] [Isaiah 53:5]
VO: That's the wilderness again, but this time it's a person. [Numbers 21:9] [Isaiah 53:5]
VO: Earlier in his book: "I saw the Lord sitting upon a throne, high and lifted up." [Isaiah 6:1]
VO: And above him? Seraphim. The word God said to Moses. [Isaiah 6:2] [Numbers 21:8]

## Landing
VO: A few verses after he explains "lifted up," John quotes both passages and says, "Isaiah said these things because he saw his glory and spoke of him." [John 12:38] [John 12:40] [John 12:41]
VO: The throne and the cross, in the same words. [Isaiah 6:1] [John 12:32]
VO: So why a snake on a pole? [John 3:14]
VO: Because he was telling Nicodemus where to look. [John 3:14]
VO: John's last Scripture at the cross: "They will look on him whom they have pierced." [John 19:37]
VO: Two verses later, John brings back the man who came by night. [John 19:39]
VO: He's carrying about seventy-five pounds of spices. [John 19:39]
VO: John never says what Nicodemus believed. He tells us what he carried. [John 19:39]

## Value
- The word for the fiery serpent is the same word as the seraphim above God's throne [Numbers 21:8] [Isaiah 6:2]
- Isaiah uses "high and lifted up" for both a throne and a pierced servant [Isaiah 6:1] [Isaiah 52:13]
- John quotes both passages right after "lifted up" and says Isaiah saw his glory [John 12:41]
- John's last Scripture at the cross is about looking, and Nicodemus comes back two verses later [John 19:37] [John 19:39]
