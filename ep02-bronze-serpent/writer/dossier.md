## Traps (the text doesn't say these)
- **Prostration before the serpent:** 2 Kgs 18:4 says "made offerings". No bowing posture is stated (Marcus, 17:33).
- **Where the serpent stood:** not stated.
- **Why people made offerings to it:** not stated. Don't say they thought it healed them.
- **Moses wasn't a king.** Marcus's draft opening says "another king"; v2 says "King Hezekiah".
- **The 3:16 speaker:** whether 3:16-21 is Jesus speaking or John's comment is debated, so v2 says "comes John 3:16", without attributing the words.
- **"Hilltop shrines":** don't call the high places all pagan.
