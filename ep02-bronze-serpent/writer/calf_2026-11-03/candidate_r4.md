## Value
- The calf and the serpent differ in what each asked: gold rings or a look [Exodus 32:3] [Numbers 21:8]
- A serpent people made offerings to became what the calf had been, and a king broke it in pieces [2 Kings 18:4]
- Jesus described his death with that destroyed serpent, two verses before John 3:16 [John 3:14] [John 3:16]
