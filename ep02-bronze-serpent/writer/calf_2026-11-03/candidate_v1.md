# What was the difference?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 93
answer: The calf was the god Israel wanted; the serpent was only a picture of what was killing them, and that is the picture Jesus used for his own death.

## Hook
VO: Aaron made a metal animal, and about three thousand men died. [Exodus 32:4]
VO: His brother Moses made one too, and the dying looked at it and lived. [Numbers 21:9]
VO: What was the difference?
ON-SCREEN: WHAT WAS THE DIFFERENCE?

## Stakes
VO: The obvious answer: the people said, "Make us gods." God said, "Make a fiery serpent." [Exodus 32:1] [Numbers 21:8]

## Story
VO: The deeper difference is what each one was. [Exodus 32:4]
VO: The calf was the god they wanted. [Exodus 32:1]
VO: The serpent wasn't a god at all. It was a picture of what was killing them. [Numbers 21:6]
VO: They wanted the serpents gone. God gave them one to look at. [Numbers 21:7]
VO: The calf cost them their gold. The serpent cost them one look. [Exodus 32:3] [Numbers 21:8]

## Turn
VO: Centuries later, people were making offerings to it. [2 Kings 18:4]
VO: Moses' serpent had become Aaron's calf. [2 Kings 18:4]
VO: King Hezekiah broke it in pieces, with the same Hebrew verb Moses used for the calf. [2 Kings 18:4] [Deuteronomy 9:21]
VO: Both were gone. Then Jesus brought one back. [John 3:14]
VO: Not the gold one. [John 3:14]
VO: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up." [John 3:14]
VERSE: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up." — John 3:14 (ESV)

## Landing
VO: He's describing his own death, with a picture of the killer. [John 12:33]
VO: Why?
VO: It had the killer's shape, but no poison. [Numbers 21:9]
VO: Paul says God "made him to be sin who knew no sin." [2 Corinthians 5:21]
VO: Many Christians see the pattern: the cure took the killer's shape.
VO: Aaron gave them the god they wanted. God gave them the cure they'd never have chosen.
VO: Two verses after the serpent comes John 3:16. [John 3:16]
VO: It can be translated, "For this is how God loved the world." [John 3:16]

## Value
- The calf and the serpent are opposites in what they were, not only in who ordered them [Exodus 32:1] [Numbers 21:8]
- The Hebrew verb for Hezekiah breaking the serpent is the verb Moses used for crushing the calf [2 Kings 18:4] [Deuteronomy 9:21]
- The serpent had the killer's shape and no poison, and Paul says Jesus was made sin though he knew no sin [2 Corinthians 5:21]

## Caption
Two brothers. Two metal animals. Only one of them is in John 3. Read it yourself: Exodus 32, Numbers 21, 2 Kings 18:4, John 3:14–16. Scripture quotations are from the ESV.
