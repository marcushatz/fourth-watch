# Review of v2 (Nov 1)

## Six rejection tests (run by me before the independent reads)
1. **Ordinary retelling? No.** The first 20 s are the calf/serpent reversal plus Jesus' use of it, two verses before John 3:16. The familiar story is one line (Num 21), about 12%.
2. **Superficial trivia? Borderline.** The 2 Kgs 18:4 list (shrines, pillars, Asherah) carries the point "filed with the idols", but the AI viewer heard it as "noise". v3 cuts the list to one clause.
3. **Forced cross-reference? No.**
   - The calf/serpent link is presented as the script's *comparison* ("The difference?"), not as a link Scripture makes.
   - John 3:14 names Moses' serpent.
   - 3:16's "For" is grammar.
   - John 12:33 explains the same phrase "lifted up".
4. **Predictable conclusion? No.** "Hezekiah destroyed the object; Jesus kept the meaning" can't be guessed from the title.
5. **Merely sounds profound? It did, partly.** "Forgot what it pointed to" was unearned: the script never said what it pointed to. v3 adds that the life came from God's promise, not the bronze.
6. **Unanswered next question?** Yes: "bitten by what?" and "who is Hezekiah?". v3 answers both.

## Independent read: a first-time viewer (AI, fresh agent, no tools)
**Scores:** value 4/5. Q1 clarity 3/5 ("I got what happened but not really how"); Q2 clarity 4/5.

**What landed:**
- The new knowledge in each window: a king smashed Moses' snake; "two verses before John 3:16"; the offerings; "This is how God loved the world".
- Asked for the connection they'd never have found reading separately, it named this one: "I've heard 3:16 a hundred times and never knew the verses right before it were about that snake."
- Its comment: "wait, John 3:14 is about the SNAKE??"
- Screenshot line: "People kept the object and forgot what it pointed to."

**Swipe risks:**
- Line 5 (the question restated) "felt like the video starting over".
- Line 7 (the list) "feels like a lecture".
- Line 13 ("the ESV notes") sounds like hedging.

**Confusions:** Hezekiah, Asherah, "fiery serpent", Nicodemus, "Son of Man".

## What v3 changes (the spine stays the same; these are clarity fixes, not new verses)
- Line 5 cut: the title already carries the question, and the landing re-asks it.
- **Bitten by what?** "God sent fiery serpents among the people. His cure: 'Make a fiery serpent and set it on a pole.'" This is Num 21:6, 8, quoted.
- The list cut to "right beside the pagan objects Hezekiah destroyed".
- "a faithful king, Hezekiah" (2 Kgs 18:3).
- "a Jewish leader, Nicodemus" (John 3:1).
- The life came "from God's promise, not from the bronze" (Num 21:8: "everyone who is bitten, when he sees it, shall live"). This earns the "forgot what it pointed to" line.
- John 12 named, with the exact phrase "what kind of death he was going to die".
- "the ESV notes" became "it can be translated".
