## Shared scene block

Used in all three prompts:

> Late afternoon in a rocky wilderness. A bronze serpent is fixed to the top of one straight wooden pole with no crossbar, set in a low mound of stones, standing above a camp of low black goat-hair tents. In the near foreground a weakened man in a plain wool robe lies propped on one arm and lifts his head to look up at the bronze serpent. Behind him a few people help one another and turn their faces toward the pole. Small dark snakes still move between the stones in the near foreground. No blood, no wounds, no bodies. Vertical 9:16. The top quarter of the frame is calm empty sky for typography. The man's line of sight forms one strong diagonal from lower left up to the serpent at upper right. Five clear depth layers: foreground stones, the man, the camp, far ridges, sky. Adult proportions, about seven heads tall, small heads, restrained faces shaped by light. No text, no letters, no logos, no halos, no cartoon proportions, no big eyes, no dot eyes, no photorealism, no 3D render, no vector gradients, no doorways, no medical symbol.

## A. Gold Leaf Fresco

**The idea:** sacred wall painting, composed like a film frame. "Gold only where God acts" becomes literal: gold leaf is the only gold in the picture.

**Why it could work:** reverent, distinctive, hard to mistake for another channel.

**The risk:** it may feel still. Faces must stay dignified.

**Model:** Nano Banana Pro, 2K. **Quoted cost:** 2 credits.

**Prompt:**

> A sacred wall painting in mineral pigments on dry plaster, composed like a modern film frame. Restricted earth palette of burnt umber, raw sienna, clay red, ash grey and deep night blue. Burnished gold leaf appears on one object only: the bronze serpent, with a soft worn glow around it. Flattened stacked planes of hills, elongated dignified figures, long simple drapery folds, modelled with two tones. Fine plaster grain, soft pigment wear and hairline craquelure. One warm light from the upper left. [SHARED SCENE BLOCK]

## B. Annotated Engraving

**The idea:** an old engraved Bible page brought to life. Gold lines, marks and notes can belong to the same world as the picture, which suits an investigation.

**Why it could work:** intelligent and scholarly, with easy consistency. It connects naturally to the John 3 page sequence.

**The risk:** very fine line work may shimmer at phone size. It may feel antique if the type is weak.

**Model:** Recraft V4.1, standard, 2K, with colours #14100D, #2A1E16, #F2E6CF, #E3B25C. **Quoted cost:** 8 credits.

**Prompt:**

> A nineteenth-century Bible wood engraving, fine parallel lines and cross-hatching in black ink on warm aged paper, with one spot colour: metallic gold ink on the bronze serpent only. Deep blacks, a vast sky drawn in long parallel strokes, one dramatic light from the upper left. Cinematic composition with a strong foreground. Figures have real weight and drapery. Faces fall partly in shadow. Subtle paper fibre and ink texture. [SHARED SCENE BLOCK]

## C. Cinematic Gouache

**The idea:** a hand-painted production painting for a prestige 2D animated feature. It relies on big shapes, controlled light and deep atmosphere.

**Why it could work:** the most cinematic of the three, and the easiest to move in layers.

**The risk:** the most familiar look. It needs strict design to avoid generic AI painting.

**Model:** GPT Image 2.5, Flare, high, 2K. **Quoted cost:** 2.75 credits.

**Prompt:**

> A hand-painted gouache production painting for a prestige 2D animated feature film. Big simple shapes, restrained palette of night umber, clay, ash and warm lamp gold, with one ember accent. Low camera close to the ground, looking up. A low sun behind haze at the upper left gives one controlled key light, with long shadows. Dry-brush texture, soft atmospheric perspective, painted depth. Designed characters with long elegant lines and expressive silhouettes that keep real form and light inside them. Not a cartoon. [SHARED SCENE BLOCK]
