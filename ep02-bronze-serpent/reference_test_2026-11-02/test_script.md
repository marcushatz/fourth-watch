King Hezekiah smashed something God told Moses to make. Why? Serpents were killing Israel. Moses lifted a bronze serpent on a pole, and whoever was bitten looked and lived. Centuries later, Israel was making offerings to it. So Hezekiah broke it in pieces. It was only ever a sign. Jesus said, as Moses lifted up the serpent, so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in Him may have eternal life. Don't worship the sign. Look to the One it points to.
