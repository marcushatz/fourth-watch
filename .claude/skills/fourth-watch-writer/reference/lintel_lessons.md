# What the Lintel reels teach about structure (measured), and what we never take from them

Source: `research/script_rules_from_hits.md` (14 Shorts, shot by shot, with Instagram views) and `research/lintel_transcripts_all.md` (12 transcripts).

**Use the numbers. Never reuse their words, questions or topics.**

## Measured structure (the top six vs the lows)
| Measure | Hits / top six | Lows | Our target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Familiar retelling | 0-5% (hits), 0-21% (top six) | 69-82% (story-led lows) | ≤ 15% |
| Hook | Fully asked by about 6 s; about a named person or object; third person | "you"/"we" hooks | Same |
| Second sentence | Sharpens the puzzle with a concrete fact | — | Same |
| First turn ("But notice…") | 14-25 s (14-26%) | Some lows turn early too (necessary, not sufficient) | ≤ 25% |
| Core answer | 33-50%, with ≤ 24 s from the first answer piece | 59-82%, dripped | 35-50% |
| Second half | Adds 2-4 new evidence beats; no more story, no more lessons | Story or applications | Evidence or a transformation |
| Outside authorities | 0 | Show (3 thinkers), A&E (C. S. Lewis) | 0 |
| Viewer application | 1-3 you-lines, ≤ 11% | 19-57% | ≤ 3 lines |
| Hook echo | Its words return at 82-89% | — | 75-90% |
| Ending | One comment question, an action, or nothing | Follow / "welcome to" | No follow ask |
| Words | 218-414 (median 275) | — | 230-270 |

**Inferred** (script_rules §2c-2d): the hits answer a *puzzle about the text* with a definite, checkable answer. Above about 500K views, the difference is mostly the topic and the hook's concrete puzzle, not the wording.

## Their topics: do not use (as of Nov 1, 2026)
- Why didn't God remove the tree?
- Where did Cain's wife come from?
- What happens to people who never heard of Jesus?
- Why does God allow suffering?
- Was Judas a bad person?
- Why did people live so long (Methuselah)?
- If God is love, why is there a hell?
- If God exists, why doesn't He show Himself?
- If you were born somewhere else…?
- Why do we hide from God after we sin?
- What if Adam and Eve never ate from the tree?
- Is money the root of all evil?
- Why blood on a doorway (Exodus 12)?
- The seven days of creation
- Praying for a job

A different question about the same passage is allowed only if its thread and discoveries are our own. Name the overlap in the angle card.
