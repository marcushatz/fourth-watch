# Rules that changed

The writing rules had grown across the writer skill, the story skill, `system/01` to `03`, `production/03` and `10`, the Lintel studies and `fw_check.py`, and they disagreed. On Nov 6 (Round 74) Marcus made Relate → Reveal → Transform the foundation (`EDITORIAL.md`). This table records how each conflict was settled. Only Marcus retires a rule, so every row cites his Round. Anything not listed still stands.

| "Investigate, don't retell": at most 15% of the words on the familiar story | Writer principle 1; checker "story share" | Bring viewers into the actual story: what happened, who, the stakes. Keep the famous parts moving, and make retelling build stakes or set up a reveal | Round 74: "We prefer storytelling over lecturing." Round 5 still guides pacing |
| The hook is a third-person question about a named person or object, under 12 words, and the first line equals the title | Writer principle 2 and Gate 1; checker "hook" and "hook form" | Open on a specific struggle, contradiction, question or consequential event, made concrete. ## What did not change

- ESV only. Every quotation is fetched and exact, and quoted verbs match.
- Claims are labeled. Cross-references pass the link test.
- No invented dialogue, motives or inner life.
- One clear message. Fix the spine, not the lines.
- Plain spoken English, followable on one listen.
- No churchy stock phrases. Never assume the viewer has strayed.
- No follow or community ask at the end.
- Approved scripts are locked.
- The screen rules: every clause gets a picture, verse cards and the art rules.
