# Angle scorecard (Stage 3)

Write at least 5 angles for a topic. They must be **genuinely different**: different question, different thread, different payoff. Three wordings of one idea count as one angle.

## Score each angle 1-5, with one line of evidence per score
| Criterion | 5 | 1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Originality** | No Lintel reel, top search result or common sermon takes this angle; the discoveries aren't the famous ones | The standard answer, or a Lintel topic |
| **Depth** | Each discovery transforms the previous one, and the principle could only come from this evidence | A list of facts, or a moral anyone could state without the passage |
| **Curiosity** | The question alone stops a scroll: a concrete puzzle about a named person or object that people already wonder about | Vague, abstract, devotional, or aimed at "you" |
| **Clarity** | The answer fits in one plain sentence, and the chain can be followed on one listen | It needs theology words, many names, or several partial answers |
| **Shareability** | A specific person would send it to a specific friend ("the friend who says the Bible makes God look petty"), and there is one screenshot line | Nobody in particular would send it |

## Kill rules
Kill an angle if ANY of these holds:
- any score is 1 or 2;
- it has fewer than 3 verified discoveries on its thread;
- the familiar story can't be kept under about 15% of the words;
- it needs a SPECULATION claim to work;
- it answers a question nobody asks (`research/script_rules_from_hits.md` rule 14: check that people type it).

## Ranking
Total the five scores.
- **Ties** are broken by Depth, then Curiosity. The Lintel data says the jump from 500K to millions is "mostly topic and the hook's concrete puzzle" (script_rules_from_hits §2c).
- **Only show Marcus angles you would defend.** At most 3 (Round 27).
