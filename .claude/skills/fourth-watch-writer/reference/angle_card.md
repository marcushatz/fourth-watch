# Angle card (Stage 3)

Write competing angles for a topic. They must be **genuinely different**: different question, thread or payoff. Three wordings of one idea count as one angle.

## Judge each angle in words
No scores or totals (Round 41). They turn judgment into false measurement.

| Question | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| **Is it original?** | No Lintel reel, top search result or common sermon takes this angle | The standard answer, or a Lintel topic |
| **Is it deep?** | The story changes how the viewer sees something, and that comes from this text | A list of facts, or a moral anyone could state without the passage |
| **Would someone stop?** | A concrete puzzle or human situation about a named person or object | Vague, abstract, devotional, or aimed at "you" |
| **Is it clear?** | The chain can be followed on one listen | It needs theology words, many names, or several partial answers |
| **Would someone send it?** | A specific person would send it to a specific friend | Nobody in particular would send it |

## Concept or draft?
A weak draft doesn't kill a concept. Rewrite it first. Drop an angle only if the concept itself fails:
- it needs a SPECULATION claim to work;
- its hook promises something Scripture doesn't provide;
- it answers a question nobody asks (`research/script_rules_from_hits.md` rule 14: check that people type it).

## Choosing
Choose the angle you'd defend as a creative director, and say why in a sentence. Label what comes from real audiences, your judgment and speculation.

**Only show Marcus angles you would defend.** At most 3 (Round 27).
