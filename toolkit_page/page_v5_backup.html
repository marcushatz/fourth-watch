Fourth Watch Toolkit

The files every Fourth Watch episode is built and checked with, kept here so each working session fetches the exact same copy and checks its fingerprint. Since Oct 31 it also holds the whole system: the direction rules measured from the Lintel Network's 14 Shorts, Marcus's standing feedback, the study tools and the episode plans.

The files

fw_motion.js

Text motion

Spring motion for the title, verse cards and captions. Words rise through a mask and lift out with weight. Captions read on the spoken word and swap by a push, so two lines never overlap. Nothing fades in or out.

Run them

  1. Load the motion file in the film, after the engine's boot.js and before the film's own scripts. 
    <script src="fw_motion.js"></script>
<script src="film.js"></script>
  2. Lint the script before approval. 
    python3 fw_check.py script episode.md --out qa/script
  3. Write the shot list in score.js with the direction fields, then gate it before any render. Show Marcus the shot list and a half-resolution motion draft before the full render. 
    node tools/shotlist_check.js ep01-samson/film27 --outro 3 --out qa/shotlist.md
node engine/render.js clip 0 30 2 --draft
  4. After every render: the checker, then the review pack for round N. 
    python3 fw_check.py video --video out/ep.mp4 --script episode.md \
  --subs out/ep.srt [--vo-audio out/voice.wav] --out qa/video

python3 fw_review.py --video out/ep.mp4 --round N --subs out/ep.srt \
  --shots qa/video/shots.json \
  --moments <title exit>,<card in>,<card out>,<turn> --out qa/review
  5. Hand fw_critic.md, the episode folder and N to a fresh critic that has not seen the code. Fix its 3 worst problems and every checker FAIL, then render again. Done at checker 0 FAIL and every critic score 8 or higher, after at least 3 rounds.

The whole system

Every file below is published here at the same path it has in the working folder (/home/claude/fourth-watch/). A fresh session restores one with the Artifact tool's read action and this page's link, giving the path below.

Rules (binding)

  • research/direction_rules.mdHow a script becomes shots: every clause shown happening. Measured from all 14 Lintel Shorts and their views.
  • research/feedback_rules.mdMarcus's standing corrections, round by round. Read the latest rounds before any draft.

Evidence

  • research/lintel_ref/yt/_patterns.mdCross-video patterns from 12 Lintel Shorts, with what separates their hits (7.6M, 1.8M, 1.6M views) from the lows.
  • research/lintel_ref/yt/<videoId>.mdOne shot table per Short: what happens on screen during every spoken line. Twelve files, listed in the study notes.
  • research/lintel_study_10.mdThe Oct 30 script study of 10 Lintel videos: hooks, formats, endings.

Study tools

  • tools/reference_study/README.mdHow to measure any channel shot by shot, from a video file or straight from a YouTube Short in the browser.
  • tools/reference_study/fw_shot_probe.jsThe in-browser probe: cuts, camera and on-screen motion, contact sheets.
  • tools/reference_study/shotstats.py · strips.py · audiostudy.pyThe same study for a video file, plus a mix comparison against the voice.

Engine notes

  • ep01-samson/film27/scenes/_API.mdThe scene contract, the direction fields every shot carries, and the overlay devices (counter, tally, stamp, flash).
  • ep01-samson/film27/lintel/PP2_API.mdThe character rig and every pose it can act.
