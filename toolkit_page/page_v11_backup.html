Fourth Watch Toolkit

The files every Fourth Watch episode is built and checked with, kept here so each working session fetches the exact same copy and checks its fingerprint. Since Oct 31 it also holds the whole system: the direction and script rules measured from 24 Shorts on three channels (the Lintel Network, Bible Animations, BibleProject), Marcus's standing feedback, the study tools and the episode plans.

The files

fw_motion.js

Text motion

Spring motion for the title, verse cards and captions. Words rise through a mask and lift out with weight. Captions read on the spoken word and swap by a push, so two lines never overlap. Nothing fades in or out.

fw_check.py

Checker

Lints the script, then checks the rendered cut: format, runtime, loudness, captions word for word and in sync with the voice, the title in frame one, verse cards, Instagram's covered zones, frozen frames, and since Oct 31 the direction: shot rhythm, shots per sentence, the visual beat clock (how much of the reel sits still), the hook and the time after the last spoken word. The script lint also checks the hit structure: word budget, hook form, turn time, story share, answer timing, the hook echo and the "you" lines.

shotlist_check.jsNew

Shot-list gate

Reads an episode's score.js and checks the shot list before anything renders: one picture per sentence, 3–5 s shots, the planned beat clock (a change every ~2 s, no gap over 6 s), the angle pass (variety of sizes and angles, jump cuts), what happens in every shot, pills, camera jobs, the hook, verse-card holds (longer when acted on) and the time after the last word.

Run them

  1. Load the motion file in the film, after the engine's boot.js and before the film's own scripts. 
    <script src="fw_motion.js"></script>
<script src="film.js"></script>
  2. Lint the script before approval. 
    python3 fw_check.py script episode.md --out qa/script
  3. Write the shot list in score.js with the direction fields, then gate it before any render. Show Marcus the shot list and a half-resolution motion draft before the full render. 
    node tools/shotlist_check.js ep01-samson/film27 --outro 3 --out qa/shotlist.md
node engine/render.js clip 0 30 2 --draft
  4. After every render: the checker, then the review pack for round N. 
    python3 fw_check.py video --video out/ep.mp4 --script episode.md \
  --subs out/ep.srt [--vo-audio out/voice.wav] --out qa/video

python3 fw_review.py --video out/ep.mp4 --round N --subs out/ep.srt \
  --shots qa/video/shots.json \
  --moments <title exit>,<card in>,<card out>,<turn> --out qa/review
  5. Hand fw_critic.md, the episode folder and N to a fresh critic that has not seen the code. Fix its 3 worst problems and every checker FAIL, then render again. Done at checker 0 FAIL and every critic score 8 or higher, after at least 3 rounds.
