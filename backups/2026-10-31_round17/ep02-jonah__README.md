## The open question for Marcus

**Who was Nineveh?** All three viewers asked who Nineveh was and why Jonah would mind it being spared.
- **The constraint:** the book of Jonah never names Assyria, and our rules allow Scripture only and no added motives.
- **Options:**
  1. Leave it to the text: "that great city", and God's "their evil" (1:2). This is the current draft.
  2. Name it from another book of the Bible, on screen only. Nahum's oracle against Nineveh says "Wasted is Nineveh" (Nahum 3:7) and "Your shepherds are asleep, O king of Assyria" (3:18), both checked on biblehub ESV, Oct 31. A label could read "NINEVEH · ASSYRIA'S GREAT CITY (NAHUM 3)". Nahum comes later than Jonah, so the label must not imply Jonah's motive.
  3. Say it in the voice, as a one-line context from Scripture. This costs about 3 s and needs a fact-check.
