# 10 · Publish and growth: posting, reading the numbers, and learning from them

**Use this when** a reel has passed QA (`09_render_and_qa.md`) and is ready to post, and every time you log or review results.

Researched Oct 31, 2026 (read-only web research). Instagram is primary; YouTube Shorts and TikTok get the same master file.

**How to read this file**
- Every factual claim has its source link right next to it. The date is the source's publication date.
- **(unconfirmed)** = not confirmed from a platform-owned source. It comes from trade press or a vendor, or I could not find it.
- **(may be out of date)** = the newest source found is older than 2025.
- **Rec:** = my recommendation for this channel. It is reasoning, not a sourced fact.
- Platform rules change often. Re-check §1, §6 and §7 every 3 months.

## 0. The ten things that matter most

1. **Watch time, likes and sends** are the top three Instagram ranking signals. Sends count slightly more for people who don't follow you. Track average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. ([Mosseri via Social Media Today, Jan 22 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); his video: [instagram.com/p/DFFyRp-pINJ](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFFyRp-pINJ/))
2. **3 minutes or less** to be recommended to non-followers ([Instagram Creators, Jan 21 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram); [Instagram Creators, recommendations](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)). All four Fourth Watch series fit (`00_series_canon.md` §2).
3. **The first 3 seconds are measured.** Skip rate is the share of views skipped within the first 3 s ([Social Media Today, Aug 24 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/)). Brand accounts sat at 60–65% in H1 2026 ([Socialinsider, Jun 2026](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/)) (vendor data).
4. **Originality.** Avoid watermarks, borders, mostly-text frames, muted audio and re-uploads of a reel already on Instagram ([Instagram, May 31 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)) (may be out of date). Accounts that mainly post unoriginal content are cut from recommendations ([Instagram Creators, Apr 30 2026](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)).
5. **Hashtags:** 5 at most, specific ones. Generic tags like #reels or #explore "could, in fact, hurt" ([BetaNews, Dec 19 2025](https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/); [Social Samosa, Dec 19 2025](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-hashtags-five-per-post-10923075)). Keywords go in the caption ([Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)).
6. **Label the AI narrator on Instagram.** Instagram lists "A reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover" as content that must be labeled ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/761121959519495)).
7. **ElevenLabs free-plan audio can't be used commercially** and must credit "elevenlabs.io" or "11.ai" in the title ([ElevenLabs Help](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform)). Decide the plan before Ep 1 goes up (§7.3).
8. **One clean master file goes to all three platforms.** YouTube classifies vertical uploads up to 3 min as Shorts ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)). TikTok won't put a video with someone else's watermark in For You ([TikTok FYF standards](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards)).
9. **Post at a pace you can keep.** Buffer's data: 3–5 posts a week more than doubles follower growth compared with 1–2 ([Buffer, Aug 13 2025](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)). Mosseri says there is no single right number ([RouteNote, Dec 6 2024](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/)).
10. **Judge trends, not single reels.** Reach swings with holidays and seasons ([Lindsey Gamble on Mosseri's Build Your Tribe interview, Apr 11 2025](https://lindseygamble.beehiiv.com/p/takeaways-from-instagram-head-adam-mosseri-s-latest-interview)).

**On length:**
- Socialinsider's H1 2026 brand data shows reach rate by reel length: under 30 s 5.2%, 30–60 s 5.6%, 60–90 s 5.3%, 90–120 s 4.3%, over 120 s 3.5% ([Socialinsider, Jun 2026](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/)). This is correlation, from brand accounts, not faith creators.
- The model channel's Shorts run 60–137 s, typically 85–115 s (`research/lintel_study_10.md`).
- Rec: aim for the low end of 90–120 s, and make every second over 120 s earn its place.

### 1.3 Trial reels

- **How they work** ([Instagram Creators, Dec 10 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)):
  - Turn on the **Trial** toggle before sharing. The reel goes to non-followers first.
  - Followers don't see it in Feed or the Reels tab, and it isn't on your grid unless you later share it with everyone.
  - After about 24 h you see views, likes, comments and shares, plus a comparison with your earlier trials.
  - You can set it to auto-share with followers "if it performs well based on views in the first 72 hours".
- **Who can use them:** public accounts with at least 1,000 followers ([Social Samosa, Jul 14 2025](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-wider-access-trial-reels-9493132)) (unconfirmed). A new account probably can't use them at first.
- **Rec:**
  - Once eligible, use trials to test a new series (e.g. the first MISQUOTED) or a riskier topic without showing it to followers.
  - Don't post two near-identical cuts of one episode publicly.
  - Whether a trial cut plus a later public re-cut counts as a duplicate is (unconfirmed).

## 2. Reading Insights

### 2.1 What each metric means

| Metric | Meaning | Source | Use it to judge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Views | The number of times the reel started to play or replay | [Lindsey Gamble, Aug 2024](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/instagram-shifts-to-views-as-the-primary-metric-implications-for-creators-brands-influencer-marketing) (unconfirmed current wording) | Volume only; replays inflate it |
| Accounts reached | Unique accounts that saw it, split into followers and non-followers | [Inro, Jul 2026](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-insights) (unconfirmed names) | The denominator for every ratio; the non-follower % shows whether it is being recommended |
| Skip rate | % of views skipped in the first 3 s | [SMT, Aug 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/); [SMT, Dec 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-announces-updates-for-the-instagram-marketing-api/807083/) | The hook (frame one, the first words, the title card) |
| Retention chart | % still watching at each second; flatter is better | [SMT, Aug 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/) | Where people leave (§2.4) |
| Average watch time | Named by Mosseri as a key number. Inro defines it as total watch time ÷ first plays. | [Mosseri via SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-insights) (unconfirmed definition) | The whole reel's hold, as a % of its length |
| Shares (sends) | Shares, including DM sends | [Mosseri via SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/) | Whether the answer was worth passing on |
| Likes, comments, saves | Counts | — | Satisfaction, conversation, reference value |
| Reposts | How often the reel was reposted | [SMT, Dec 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-announces-updates-for-the-instagram-marketing-api/807083/) | A second reach path (§4.3) |
| Follows | Follows attributed to this reel | [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-insights) (unconfirmed name) | Whether the reel turns viewers into an audience |

### 2.2 Ratios to compute yourself (Rec)

Instagram shows counts, so compute these per reel:

1. **Average watch % = average watch time ÷ reel length.** This compares reels of different lengths.
2. **Sends per reach = shares ÷ accounts reached** ([named by Mosseri](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)).
3. **Likes per reach = likes ÷ accounts reached** ([named by Mosseri](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)).
4. **Saves per reach = saves ÷ accounts reached.**
5. **Follows per 1,000 reach = follows ÷ accounts reached × 1,000.**
6. **Non-follower share = non-follower reach ÷ accounts reached.**

### 2.3 Benchmarks (use as rough outer bounds only)

- Instagram has published no official benchmark for a "good" skip rate ([Metricool, Feb 2026](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/)) (unconfirmed). I found none for sends per reach or average watch % either.
- **Socialinsider, H1 2026** (9.77 million organic brand Reels, [Jun 2026](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/)):

  | Followers | Skip rate | Reach rate (reach ÷ followers) | Reels per month |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1–5K | 65.5% | 9.78% | 8 |
  | 5–10K | 65.3% | 7.55% | 10 |
  | 10–50K | 63.4% | 7.10% | 13 |
  | 50–100K | 62.6% | 5.60% | 17 |
  | 100K–1M | 60.5% | 5.00% | 20 |

- **Socialinsider 2026 benchmarks** (organic brand accounts, [Feb 2026](https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks)):
  - Average views per reel: 580 (1–5K followers), 1,000 (5–10K), 2,460 (10–50K), 6,095 (50–100K).
  - Reels engagement by followers: 0.52%, counting likes and comments only.
- **Caveats:** these are brands, not faith creators. The Lintel hits (1.6–7.6 M views) are outliers, not a baseline.
- **Rec:** after 10 posts, your own medians become the benchmark (§9.3).

### 2.4 Reading a retention chart (Rec)

| Shape | Likely cause | Fix in |
|---|---|---|
| A steep drop in 0–3 s, high skip rate | Frame one, the first words, or the title card didn't land | Hook: `02_story_and_script.md`, `05_shot_list.md` |
| A cliff at one second | Something on screen there: a long verse card, a slow still, a jargon word, a scene change with no sound | Note the second and the beat in the log |
| The same cliff at the same beat in 3+ episodes | A format problem, not a topic problem | The script structure |
| A slow, even slope | Normal | — |
| A bump near the end | Replays or loopers | Keep the ending tight (≤ 4.5 s after the last word, `09_render_and_qa.md`) |

## 3. Caption, keywords, on-screen text, hashtags, cover, alt text

### 3.1 Caption and search

- **Keywords go in the caption, not the comments** ([Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)) (may be out of date).
- **Google and Bing:** public photos, videos, carousels and Reels from public professional accounts of users over 18 can appear in search results since Jul 10 2025 ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/instagram-content-becomes-searchable-on-google-starting-july-10/)) (unconfirmed primary wording).
  - The setting is Settings → Privacy → "Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results". It is reported to be on by default for eligible accounts.
  - Rec: confirm it is on. Marcus is over 18, so the account qualifies.
- **ESV notice** ([Crossway](https://www.crossway.org/rights-and-permissions/esv/)):
  - Video needs the full copyright notice on the product and a verbal credit to the "ESV".
  - Quotes must be verbatim, within 500 verses, never half a book.
  - The full notice goes at the bottom of every caption and description (`00_series_canon.md` §3).
  - Whether the verbal credit is spoken is still an open decision (`00_series_canon.md` §3).

**Caption template (Rec):**
```
Why did Samson lose his strength? (Judges 16)        ← line 1: the question in the words people search
The text gives a different answer than most people remember.   ← one-line tease, no spoiler
Which line surprised you?                              ← the spoken closing question, as text
Follow for more Bible questions answered from the text.   ← the follow line lives here (canon §3)
#Samson #Judges #BibleStories #BibleQuestions          ← 3–5 specific tags, no #reels/#fyp/#explore
Scripture quotations are from the ESV® Bible ... (full Crossway notice, exact text)
```

### 3.2 On-screen text

- Text must not cover most of the screen ([Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)). Majority-text reels are shown less ([Instagram, 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)).
- Instagram auto-generates captions for Reels with speech recognition. Viewers can turn them on or off ([Stanford UIT, May 2026](https://uit.stanford.edu/accessibility/accessible-social-media/instagram)) (unconfirmed by Instagram).
- **Rec:**
  - Keep the burned-in word captions in the top third (canon §4), so they don't collide with the app's own captions and buttons at the bottom.
  - The question as the top caption in frame one is the strongest hook text you have (never a static title card: `research/direction_rules.md` §3).

### 3.3 Hashtags

| Platform | What the platform says | Rec |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 5 max; targeted beats generic; #reels/#explore may hurt ([BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/)) | 3–5: the person or book, the topic, one channel tag |
| YouTube Shorts | "Are they required? No, of course not." They can tie a Short to a topic ([Todd Sherman, Creator Insider, Aug 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU); [SEJ](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953)) (may be out of date) | 2–3 in the description |
| TikTok | No official count found (unconfirmed) | 3–5 specific; use Creator Search Insights wording (§6.2) |

### 3.4 Cover and grid

- **Instagram:**
  - The profile grid changed from squares to rectangles in Jan 2025. Per post: ⋯ → "Adjust Preview" → Fit or Fill ([Business Today, Jan 21 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21)).
  - The grid crop is widely reported as 3:4 (unconfirmed).
  - Rec: one cover template for every episode. The question in large Cinzel inside the centre 3:4 area of the 9:16 frame, the series label small above it, on the palette (canon §4). Upload it as a custom cover in the Reels editor.
- **YouTube Shorts:**
  - Custom thumbnails started Jul 25 2026, first for Partner Program channels, desktop only. They show on the channel and home page, **not** in the Shorts feed, where most views happen. Keep key content in a 3:2 safe zone ([PPC Land, Jul 26 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-ends-2-year-wait-for-shorts-thumbnails-but-blocks-a-b-testing/)) (unconfirmed primary).
  - A new channel can only pick a frame on mobile (same source).
  - Rec: make sure one frame in the reel works as a cover, e.g. the hook frame with the question on screen.

### 3.5 Alt text

- One vendor article gives the path for Reels alt text as Edit → Advanced settings → Accessibility → Write alt text ([Lilach Bullock, Oct 2026](https://www.lilachbullock.com/instagram-reels-random-auto-generated-descriptions/)) (unconfirmed). Official guides I found describe alt text for photos only ([Stanford UIT](https://uit.stanford.edu/accessibility/accessible-social-media/instagram)).
- No evidence that alt text affects reach (unconfirmed).
- Rec: if the field exists, write one sentence: what the animation shows plus the question.

## 4. Cadence, timing, trial reels, collaborations, pinned comments

### 4.1 How often

- **Official:**
  - "Post original content consistently" ([Instagram Creators, Jan 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram)).
  - Mosseri says there is no single answer: some successful accounts post once a month, others five Reels a day. More posting generally means more reach, but watch for burnout ([RouteNote summary of his video, Dec 6 2024](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/)) (secondary summary).
- **YouTube:** "There is nothing in our systems that's like when you hit the five video…" mark ([Todd Sherman, Creator Insider, Aug 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=345s)) (may be out of date).
- **Data, Buffer** ([Aug 13 2025](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/); 2.1 M posts from about 102,000 accounts; within-account comparison; all formats together):
  - 3–5 posts a week more than doubles follower growth compared with 1–2 a week.
  - Reach per post is about 12% higher at 3–5 a week.
  - Weeks with no posts show a "no-post penalty".
- **Rec:**
  - Each episode is heavy (script, fact-check, review, voice, render, QA).
  - Two posts a week you can keep for 10 weeks beat five a week for two.
  - Bank 3 finished episodes before launch, and never skip a whole week.
  - The 30–60 s series (MOST PEOPLE MISS, STRANGE BUT TRUE, MISQUOTED) are the way to keep cadence between ASK THE TEXT episodes.

### 4.2 When

- **Official:** I found no current Instagram statement on the best time to post (unconfirmed). YouTube's Shorts lead said time of day is "not something that we think about optimizing for" ([Creator Insider, Aug 2023](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=327s)) (may be out of date).
- **Data, Buffer** ([Sep 23 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram); 9.6 M posts from 2024–25; median engagement by followers):
  - Wednesday and Thursday are strongest.
  - 6–11 p.m. is best on most weekdays; 1–5 a.m. is weakest.
  - All formats are pooled, not Reels only.
- **Rec:**
  - Pick one fixed evening slot in US Eastern time and keep it for 10 posts. Change timing only after that, and only one thing at a time.
  - Sunday is worth testing as a second slot for Bible content (a hypothesis, unconfirmed).

### 4.3 Collaborations and reposts

- Use **Collabs** (or Enhanced Tags) to work with other creators, instead of reposting their content ([Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators); [Apr 2026](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)).
- **Reposts** (since Aug 2025): anyone can repost public reels. Reposts credit you and can be recommended to the reposter's followers ([GSMArena, Aug 6 2025](https://www.gsmarena.com/instagram_introduces_repost_and_location_sharing-news-68934.php)).
- The number of co-authors allowed on a Collab: (unconfirmed).
- **Rec:**
  - After about 10 posts, offer a Collab to a small faith creator whose audience asks the same questions: their question, your answer from the text, posted as co-authors.
  - Keep the "ESV only, what the text says" rule (canon §3).

### 4.4 Pinned comments

- You could pin up to 3 comments since 2020. Since Sep 2025 you can also pin your own comment. Instagram has said nothing about pins affecting reach ([Social Media Today, Sep 7 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-option-pin-your-own-post-comments/759457/)).
- **Rec:** pin your own comment right after posting, with:
  - the passage reference ("Judges 16:4–22, ESV");
  - the one-line answer;
  - the closing question.

  Later, pin one strong viewer answer under it.

## 5. Comments

### 5.1 Do replies change reach?

- Mosseri's top three signals don't include comments ([SMT, Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)). Instagram lists commenting among the "your activity" signals that shape what a viewer sees next ([Instagram, 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)) (may be out of date).
- **Buffer** (700,000+ posts, about 68,000 accounts): posts where the creator replied averaged about 21% more engagement than the account's own baseline ([Buffer, Jan 28 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-comments-engagement/)). This is correlation, and 37% of profiles saw no lift.
- **Comment dislikes** (rolling out 2026): private, with no public count and no notice to the author. Mosseri said heavily disliked comments may sink in the comment order ([GBC Ghana, May 7 2026, citing Mosseri on Threads](https://www.gbcghanaonline.com/technology/instagram-rolls-out-dislike-button-for-comments/2026/)) (unconfirmed global availability).
- **Rec:** in the first hour, reply to real questions. Replies are part of the product on a Q&A channel, whatever they do for ranking.

### 5.2 The spoken closing question vs engagement bait

- Meta (Facebook, 2017) demotes "engagement bait" such as "LIKE this if you're an Aries!". Posts that "ask people for help, advice, or recommendations" are not affected ([Meta, Dec 18 2017](https://about.fb.com/news/2017/12/news-feed-fyi-fighting-engagement-bait-on-facebook/)) (may be out of date; whether it applies on Instagram is unconfirmed; it matters if reels are cross-posted to Facebook).
- **Rec:**
  - Ask a real question with room for real answers: "Which part of Judges 16 did you not expect?"
  - Never: "Type AMEN", "Share if you believe", "Comment 1 if…".

### 5.4 House rules for a faith comment section (Rec)

1. **Remove** (delete, then Restrict or Block): slurs, threats, sexual content, spam, scam links, doxxing, and attacks on a person or group for their faith or lack of it.
2. **Keep:** disagreement, hard questions, skeptics' challenges, and other traditions' readings, when they are said respectfully. Hard questions are where new episodes come from.
3. **Hide, don't argue with,** bait that adds nothing ("cope", one-word insults).
4. **Never reply in anger.** Draft it, wait 10 minutes, then reply or don't.
5. **Starter Hidden Words list** for scams that target faith and creator accounts: `whatsapp`, `telegram`, `crypto`, `forex`, `investment`, `DM me`, `promote`, `collab dm`, `cashapp`, `sugar daddy` (Rec; edit as real spam shows up, and remove a term if it hides real questions).
6. **Put a one-line rule in the pinned comment** if a thread turns hostile: "Questions and disagreement welcome. Insults get hidden."

### 5.5 Reply playbook (Rec)

| Comment | Response |
|---|---|
| An honest question | Answer in 1–2 sentences with the verse (ESV). If several people ask it, pin the answer and log it as a topic for an episode. |
| A doctrinal disagreement (another tradition) | Acknowledge that the view exists, state what the text says with the reference, and name the scope: "We stick to what the text says; traditions read this differently." One reply only, no denominational debate. |
| A correction that is right | Thank them, pin the correction, add "Correction: …" to the caption, and log it in `11_lessons_log.md`. |
| A skeptic's challenge | One calm reply with the text. No second round. |
| Hostile or insulting | No reply. Hide or Restrict. Add repeat words to Hidden Words. |
| Spam or scam ("DM me for prayer/$$") | Delete and block; add the words to Hidden Words. |
| A prayer request or personal pain | A short, kind reply. Never medical or legal advice. If someone mentions self-harm, point them to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US) ([988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org/)). |

## 7. Music, voice, and AI disclosure

### 7.1 What a Fourth Watch reel contains

- **Visuals:** drawn in code, cartoon-style 2D billboards on low-poly sets, with no photoreal images (`00_series_canon.md` §1, §4). No AI image or video model is used.
- **Narrator:** an AI text-to-speech voice. Marcus records in ElevenLabs (`04_voice.md` §1); the earlier engine used Kokoro neural TTS (`engine/voice.py`). Either one is a realistic-sounding synthetic voice.
- **Soundtrack:** synthesized in code, with no samples (`08_sound.md` §5). This is procedural synthesis, not a generative-AI music model.
- **Text:** ESV quotations.

### 7.2 What each platform requires for these elements

| Element | Instagram / Facebook (Meta) | YouTube | TikTok |
|---|---|---|---|
| Code-drawn cartoon visuals | **No label needed.** Instagram's no-label example: "Video of an outdoor landscape, created in a style resembling a cartoon" ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/761121959519495)). | **No disclosure.** Not realistic; the page's example is "an AI-generated or altered animation of a missile in a fully animated video" ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)). | **No label.** The rule covers "synthetic or manipulated media that shows realistic scenes" ([TikTok guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)). |
| AI narrator voice | **Label required.** Meta requires a label on "photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio" that was digitally generated or altered; one example is "A reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover". "There may be penalties if you do not label content as required." ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/761121959519495)) | **Not clearly required.** Disclosure is required for content that "seems realistic": a real person saying or doing something they didn't, altered real events or places, realistic scenes that didn't happen, AI music. A synthetic narrator who imitates no real person over an animated video is not addressed. If you choose "AI use: Yes", the label for "non-music AI-generated audio or AI content that is non-photorealistic or animated" appears in the expanded description, not on the player ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)). | **Label (Rec).** TikTok was reported in 2024 to require labels on AI content with "realistic images, audio, and video" ([NBC New York, May 9 2024](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/national-international/tiktok-to-start-labeling-ai-generated-content-as-technology-becomes-more-universal/5399109/)). The current guidelines page names realistic scenes only, so the audio part is (unconfirmed). |
| Code-synthesized music | **No label:** not AI-generated. | **No disclosure:** the trigger is AI-generated music ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)), and this is not that. | **No label.** |

**Decision (Rec):**
1. **Instagram:** turn on the AI label toggle on every reel. Instagram's help page calls it "Label as made with AI"; the public label is "AI info" ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/761121959519495); [Meta, Apr 5 2024, updated Jul and Sep 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)).
2. **TikTok:** turn on the AI-generated content toggle.
3. **YouTube:** set "AI use: Yes". It isn't clearly required, but it keeps the three platforms consistent, and the label sits in the description.
4. **Add one plain line** at the end of every caption: "Narration: synthetic voice. Animation drawn in code." On a channel whose promise is accuracy, viewers who find out later will trust you less.
5. **Does the AI label cost reach?** I found no official statement either way (unconfirmed). Instagram limits reach of AI-persona accounts that don't use the "AI generated profile" label. That rule targets fake people, not human creators who use AI tools ([TechSpot](https://techspot.com/news/113695-instagram-cracks-down-ai-accounts-pretending-human.html)) (secondary). Fourth Watch has no AI persona.

### 7.3 Rights

- **ElevenLabs voice (decide before Ep 1):**
  - Free plan: "does **not** include a commercial license and cannot be used for any commercial purpose". Published free-plan content must credit "elevenlabs.io" or "11.ai" in the title.
  - Paid plans include a commercial license, unless you use Beta Services. Content made during a paid subscription "can be used commercially, and indefinitely". Content made outside one, before or after, cannot ([ElevenLabs Help](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform)).
  - **Rec:** if the channel will ever earn money (Creator Rewards, YouTube Partner Program, sponsors, a product), record every take on a paid plan from the start. Takes recorded on the free plan stay non-commercial forever. Whether an unmonetized channel counts as "commercial" is (unconfirmed — read the ElevenLabs Terms).
  - Never clone another creator's narrator (`04_voice.md`).
- **Kokoro** (if it is ever used again): check the model card's license before publishing (unconfirmed here).
- **Your synthesized soundtrack:**
  - YouTube's **Content ID** is only for owners of "exclusive rights to a substantial body of original material" ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797370)). A matched claim can block, monetize or track the video.
  - If the soundtrack is ever released through a distributor that registers it in Content ID, **your own videos can be claimed** unless your channels are allowlisted ([RouteNote, Aug 31 2026](https://routenote.com/radar/youtube-content-id-allowlisting-explained/)).
  - A Short over 1 minute with an active copyright claim **is blocked globally** ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)).
  - **Rec:** don't register Fourth Watch music anywhere.
  - Copyright in procedurally generated music is (unconfirmed — not legal advice).
- **Instagram original audio:**
  - Audio mixes made with audio layering are attributed to the creator, and others can save and remix them ([Instagram Creators, Jan 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram)). That other people can tap "Use audio" on any public reel's original audio is widely reported (unconfirmed).
  - Whether a public account can stop reuse per reel is (unconfirmed).
  - Renaming "Original audio" to "Fourth Watch – [title]" is reported (unconfirmed).
- **No licensed or trending songs** in Fourth Watch reels (`08_sound.md` §5). If you ever add one on YouTube: most Shorts Audio Library songs can run at most 90 s within a 3-minute Short ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)).
- **ESV:**
  - Video needs the full notice plus the verbal "ESV" credit.
  - Setting ESV text to **music** (sheet music, recordings) needs Crossway's written permission ([Crossway](https://www.crossway.org/rights-and-permissions/esv/)). This matters only if a verse is ever sung.

## 8. Publishing checklist (tick every line; a "no" stops the post)

### A. The file (after `09_render_and_qa.md` is done)
1. Has the checker shown 0 FAIL, has Marcus watched it, and is the runtime within the series target (canon §2)? The platform limit for recommendation is 180 s.
2. Is it 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, with no border or letterbox, and no watermark or logo from any app?
3. Is the question on screen in frame one and spoken by about 5 s?
4. Is the audio never muted, the voice clear on a phone speaker, and the mix at −14 LUFS?
5. Does text never fill most of the frame, with captions in the top third?
6. Is every quote word-for-word ESV (fact-check passed), and the verbal ESV credit present if Marcus decided on it?
7. Is the closing question spoken, and a real question (not "type Amen", not "like if")?
8. Was the voice take recorded on the ElevenLabs plan Marcus chose (§7.3)?

### 9.3 How to learn from it (Rec, except where linked)

**Ground rules**
- Compare 7-day numbers with 7-day numbers.
- Use **medians**, not averages; one hit distorts an average.
- Change **one** thing at a time and keep it for at least 3 posts.
- Note holidays and seasons (Easter, Christmas, summer). Mosseri says big reach shifts follow holidays, seasons and world events, and creators should watch long-term trends ([Lindsey Gamble, Apr 2025](https://lindseygamble.beehiiv.com/p/takeaways-from-instagram-head-adam-mosseri-s-latest-interview)).

**After 5 posts: check the mechanics, not the topics**
1. Is any reel's non-follower reach near zero? Check Account Status and the §8 A–B list. That points to an eligibility problem, not a content problem.
2. Is skip rate trending down? Compare it with the 60–65% band (§2.3) only as a rough sense check.
3. Does the retention chart show a cliff at the same structural beat in 3+ reels? Fix the format in `02_story_and_script.md`.
4. Did any checklist line fail (label, ESV notice, cover)? Fix the process.
5. Write one line in `11_lessons_log.md`.

**After 10 posts: set your own baselines**
1. Compute the medians of skip rate, average watch %, sends per reach, likes per reach and follows per 1k. These are now the targets.
2. Rank the reels by sends per reach and by average watch %. Put the top 3 and bottom 3 side by side and list the differences: question type, hook line, first frame, length, cover, slot.
3. Write at most **two** hypotheses, e.g. "'Did X really…?' questions get more sends than 'Why did God…?'". Test each over the next 3+ posts, or with trial reels if eligible.
4. Check comment `questions_asked` and pull the most-asked one into `01_topic_selection.md`.

**After 20 posts: decide on the format and the mix**
1. Group the reels by `series`, `topic_type`, length band (< 60 s, 60–90, 90–120, > 120) and slot, using only groups with 5+ reels. Compare the median average watch %, sends per reach and follows per 1k.
2. Keep the winners and cut or rework the weakest group. Follows per 1k show what turns viewers into an audience; sends per reach show what spreads.
3. Compare platforms: follows per week on each against the extra minutes each upload costs. Drop a platform only if it's near zero after 20 posts.
4. Revisit cadence (§4.1) and slot (§4.2) with real numbers.
5. Re-check §1, §6 and §7 against the platforms' latest announcements. Rules here date from Oct 31 2026.
