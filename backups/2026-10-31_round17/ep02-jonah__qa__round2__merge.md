## What wasn't taken, and why

- **Saying who Nineveh was to Israel** (both viewers). Jonah never names Assyria. Outside history is ruled out by the bank's risk note, and inferring Jonah's hatred would add a motive the text doesn't give. Instead, the text's own reason ("their evil", 1:2) now lands clearly, and the on-screen label reads "NINEVEH · THAT GREAT CITY". **For Marcus:** if he wants the Assyria context, 2 Kings 19:36 places Assyria's king at Nineveh, but that is a later reign, so it would need careful wording.

- **"Forty more days" instead of "Yet forty days."** It's an ESV quote, so the wording stays. The 40 DAYS counter carries the meaning.
