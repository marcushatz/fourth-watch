## Hook
VO: Why did Samson's strength keep working, even the night he slept with a prostitute?
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID SAMSON'S STRENGTH KEEP WORKING, EVEN THE NIGHT HE SLEPT WITH A PROSTITUTE?

## Stakes
VO: That same night in Gaza, men waited to kill him at dawn. At midnight, he pulled up the city gate, posts and all, and carried it to the top of a hill. [Judges 16:1-3]

## Story
VO: It looks like God didn't care how he lived. But look at where the text says his strength came from. [Judges 14:6]

## Turn
VO: Three times, it says the same thing: "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him." [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
VO: Even when he demanded a Philistine wife, his parents "did not know that it was from the LORD, for he was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines." [Judges 14:1-4]
VO: So his strength was never a reward. It was for a job: he would "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines." [Judges 13:5]

## Landing
VO: And when it worked, that never meant God approved. [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20]
VO: While he slept, Delilah had his seven locks shaved off, and his strength left him. [Judges 16:19]
VO: He woke up expecting it to work like before: "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." [Judges 16:20]
VO: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VO: Blinded and brought out to entertain his captors, Samson prayed, "O Lord GOD, please remember me and please strengthen me only this once..." [Judges 16:21, 16:25, 16:28]
VO: Then he leaned his weight against the two middle pillars and bowed with all his strength, and the house fell. [Judges 16:29-30]
VO: And Hebrews 11 still names him in a list of people who, through faith, "were made strong out of weakness." [Hebrews 11:32-34]
VO: So why did Samson's strength keep working? Because it was God's, given for God's purpose, not a sign of God's approval. And even at his lowest, he could still call on God. [Judges 13:5, 16:20, 16:28]

## Question
VO: What stands out more to you about Samson: his failures, or his prayer at the end? [Judges 16:28]

## Value
- Three times the text says "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him": the strength was never his own [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- Even his Philistine marriage was "from the LORD, for he was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines" [Judges 14:4]
- His strength had a job: to "begin to save Israel" [Judges 13:5]
- He expected it to work "as at other times", but "did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- Hebrews 11 still names him among those who through faith "were made strong out of weakness" [Hebrews 11:32-34]

## Caption
Why did Samson's strength keep working? Read Judges 13:5, 14:4 and 16:20, then Hebrews 11:32-34.
