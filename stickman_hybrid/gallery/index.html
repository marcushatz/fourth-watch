Hybrid Stickman Study

Coloured hand-drawn stick figures inside layered cinematic worlds, made three ways. The cast and the blocking are the same in all nine frames. Only the drawing and the world change. Tap any frame to see it full screen. Tap a star to mark the version you'd take forward.

One cast for all three versions. They're adult figures about eight heads tall, with shoulders, hands with fingers and a thumb, and feet. Each face is a profile with a nose, one eye line and a brow. Adam is clay-coloured, a nod to the ground he was formed from (Genesis 2:7). Eve is dusty rose, and the fruit is the only ember red. In scene 2 Adam stands in the tree's shade, and his hand reaches past the edge of the light. In scene 3 he wears fig leaves (Genesis 3:7), and the only warm light is far off among the trees (Genesis 3:8).

Every frame is built back to front from separate planes, and each plane is saved as its own image with transparency. A camera move could slide them past each other at different speeds. The haze and light between the planes are still baked into the finished frame.
