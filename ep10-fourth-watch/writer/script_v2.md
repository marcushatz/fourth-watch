## Stakes

## Story

## Turn
VO: When he finally comes, he's walking on the sea. And Mark writes, "He meant to pass by them." [Mark 6:48]
VO: Mark doesn't say why. [Mark 6:48]
VO: But in the Old Testament, that's how God shows himself. He passed by Moses on the mountain. He passed by Elijah at the cave. [Exodus 33:22] [1 Kings 19:11]
VO: Many readers think that's the point: he wasn't leaving them, he was showing them who he was. [Mark 6:48]
VO: But all they could see was a ghost. They cried out. [Mark 6:49]
VO: And he spoke: "Take heart; it is I. Do not be afraid." [Mark 6:50]
VERSE: "Take heart; it is I. Do not be afraid." — Mark 6:50 (ESV)
VO: He got into the boat, and the wind stopped. [Mark 6:51]
