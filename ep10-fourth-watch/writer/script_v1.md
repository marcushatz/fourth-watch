# Why did Jesus try to walk past his own disciples?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 85
answer: So he may not have been leaving them. He may have been showing them who he was.

## Hook
VO: Why did Jesus try to walk past his own disciples? [Mark 6:48]
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID JESUS TRY TO WALK PAST HIS OWN DISCIPLES?

## Stakes
VO: Mark says, "Immediately he made his disciples get into the boat." He made them. And he stayed behind. [Mark 6:45] [Mark 6:46]

## Story
VO: John writes, "It was now dark, and Jesus had not yet come to them." [John 6:17]
VO: The wind was against them. They rowed past midnight. Mark says Jesus saw them "making headway painfully." [Mark 6:48]
VO: And he didn't come until the fourth watch of the night, the last watch before morning. [Mark 6:48] [Matthew 14:25]

## Turn
VO: When he comes, he's walking on the sea, and Mark writes, "He meant to pass by them." [Mark 6:48]
VO: Why walk out to exhausted men and keep going? [Mark 6:48]
VO: Mark doesn't say. But the book of Job says God alone "trampled the waves of the sea." Three verses later: "he passes by me, and I see him not." [Job 9:8] [Job 9:11]
VO: Walking on the waves. Passing by. That's how Scripture talks about God. [Job 9:8] [Job 9:11]
VO: So he may not have been leaving them. He may have been showing them who he was. [Mark 6:48]
VO: But they didn't see God. They saw a ghost, and they screamed. [Mark 6:49] [Matthew 14:26]
VO: "Take heart; it is I. Do not be afraid." [Mark 6:50]
VERSE: "Take heart; it is I. Do not be afraid." — Mark 6:50 (ESV)
VO: He got into the boat, and the wind stopped. [Mark 6:51]

## Landing
VO: You'd expect Mark to end there. Instead he writes, "they were utterly astounded, for they did not understand about the loaves, but their hearts were hardened." [Mark 6:51] [Mark 6:52]
VO: The loaves? It's the middle of the night. Why is Mark talking about bread? [Mark 6:52]
VO: Because hours earlier, five loaves had passed through these same hands and fed five thousand men. [Mark 6:41] [Mark 6:44]
VO: They'd held the miracle in their own hands, and they still didn't understand. [Mark 6:41] [Mark 6:52]
VO: He meant to pass by them. [Mark 6:48]
VO: He got in the boat. [Mark 6:51]

## Value
- Mark says Jesus meant to pass by the disciples [Mark 6:48]
- Job describes God walking on the waves and passing by [Job 9:8] [Job 9:11]
- Mark ties their astonishment to the loaves, and he gets in the boat anyway [Mark 6:51] [Mark 6:52]
