# 00 · Series canon: what every Fourth Watch reel is

The fixed identity of the channel. Change it only when Marcus says so. The sources are Marcus's rounds in `research/feedback_rules.md`, `brand/handles.md` and the showrunner skill.

## 1. The channel

- **Name:** Fourth Watch. It comes from Matthew 14:25 (ESV): "And in the fourth watch of the night he came to them, walking on the sea."
- **Handles:** @fourthwatchgospel on Instagram (secured Oct 30, 2026). The same handle is to be claimed on TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. Display name: "Fourth Watch | Bible Stories". The bios are in `brand/handles.md`.
- **The promise (Round 74):** Bible stories that help people recognize their own struggles, discover meaningful truths in Scripture, and understand God more deeply. ESV only, exactly as the text says. The philosophy is `EDITORIAL.md`.
- **Form:** faceless, 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps. No photoreal images. **Under review (Round 30):** the code-animated look is no longer accepted, and the replacement art direction isn't approved yet. See `production/README.md`.
- **Model:** our own editorial philosophy (`EDITORIAL.md`). The Lintel Network's structure is one option, for the investigation format only, never its topics, labels or door imagery.

## 3. Words (binding)

- ESV only, word for word in quotes; quoted verbs must match.
- Never state what the text only implies; nothing is "last" unless the text makes it last.
- No collective description applied to one person.
- Never assume the viewer has strayed; no churchy stock phrases; no finger-pointing.
- A spoken closing question is wanted; follow/subscribe lines are not (they go in the caption).
- No spoken intro and no "thanks for watching".
- Plain words for places and objects ("building", not "temple").
- **ESV credit (Crossway):**
  - The full notice goes at the bottom of every caption or description.
  - **A spoken credit is required on video.** Crossway: "Verbal credit must also be given to the 'ESV'" (re-checked Oct 31). The default line is "Scripture quotations are from the ESV.", read lower and quieter under the outro hold. Ep 1's wording choice is in `ep01-samson/v60_plan.md` §4.

- **Disclosure:** the narrator is a synthetic voice. Turn the AI label on when posting (Instagram requires it for realistic AI voiceovers), and add a caption line: "Narration: synthetic voice. Animation drawn in code." (`10_publish_and_growth.md` §7)

## 4. Look

- **Palette:**
  - Night #14100D
  - Umber #2A1E16
  - Clay #6B4A33
  - Lamp gold #E3B25C
  - Parchment #F2E6CF
  - Ember #C2502E (sparingly)
  - Night sky #1C2433
  - Ash #8C8174
- **Type:**
  - Titles and the wordmark: Cinzel.
  - Verse cards: Cormorant Garamond Italic.
  - Narration captions: the top third, italic serif, building word by word, with 1–3 key words in gold.
- **Characters:** flat 2D billboards (`lintel/PP2_API.md`):
  - one style, mitten hands, dot eyes, no outlines on robes;
  - identity by costume and one prop;
  - no facial expressions, so acting is by pose, props, light and camera (`system/07_motion_and_acting.md`).
- **Sets:** low-poly diorama sets with depth of field, haze, one key light from the upper left, god-rays, dust.
- **Two lights:**
  - Gold from the upper left for what God did, only where the text says so.
  - Ember for the person's own choices and the Philistine side.
  - Never a balance or weighing.
- **No gore:** no blood, no wounds, nobody shown dead.

## 5. Logo and outro (approved Oct 30)

- **The scene:** Jesus (the v5 character) standing ON a rough sea, the boat full-length farther back, wind visible in His mantle and hem. No lightning or rain, no gold glints on the water.
- **The wordmark:** style 4, "Inscription, clean". Cinzel SemiBold caps, cream #F2E6CF, letter-spacing 0.14em, a soft gold glow, no foil, no ornament.
- **The outro:** Jesus appears slowly and calmly (~2 s fade-in, ease-in-out) and stays perfectly still: no bobbing, rising or sliding. The camera stays locked. The wordmark fades in with no rise.
- **Length (open decision, Oct 31):**
  - Option A: start it under the last spoken line and hold 2–2.5 s.
  - Option B: a 2.5–3 s logo moment after the question.
  - The reel should end ≤ 4.5 s after the last word.
