## Proposed safeguards (need Marcus's OK)

Real people's stories carry risks that an educational channel doesn't. These are proposals, not rules yet.

- **Consent:** get the person's written OK for the story, likeness, voice and publishing.
- **Their story stays true:** no invented events, quotes or miracles. They approve the script and final video.
- **Other people:** take care with family or others named in the story. Change or remove identifying details unless those people agree.
- **Sensitive content:** the person decides what's told, especially on abuse, addiction, illness or crime.
- **Likeness:** generate a real person's likeness only with their permission.

## Privacy

**GitHub reports this repository as public. Never put a real person's name, story, script, photo, voice or contact details here.**

- Keep only general rules and templates in this folder.
- Keep real testimony material somewhere private. A separate private repository is the simplest option, and it needs Marcus's setup and OK.
- The ledgers here use anonymous project codes only, such as `T-001`.
