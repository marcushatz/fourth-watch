# 09 · Approved techniques and rejected patterns

Add an entry after every test or episode, with its evidence. Newest first.

Status words: **Approved** means Marcus accepted the result. **Keep** means it worked technically or showed promise, but he hasn't approved the final form. **Rejected** means don't repeat it.

## From the Bronze Serpent test (Nov 2, 2026)

Evidence: `ep02-bronze-serpent/reference_test_2026-11-02/EVALUATION.md`.

### Keep: methods

| Method | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Higgsedit for assembly | 31.6 s at 1080×1920 in about 3 minutes, no credits |

### Keep: visual ideas, to redraw in the approved look

| Idea | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Split screen, As/So: serpent and cross rise on the same beat | Shows Jesus' comparison directly |
| Time-lapse: the world changes while the serpent stays | Shows centuries passing around one object |
| A line of sight from a person to the object | Turns "look and live" into a picture |
| Focus shifts from the broken serpent to the cross | Makes "pointing toward Christ" visible |
| Light reaching a crowd row by row | Shows the promise spreading to "whoever" |

### Rejected

| Pattern | Why |
|---|---|
| Content Factory for episodes | Replaced the approved script and allows only code-drawn art |
| Code-drawn characters: round heads, dot eyes, tube arms | Marcus: childish |
| Bell-shaped crowds | Identical pawns with no acting |
| Sun disc behind a centred subject | About 23 of 32 sampled seconds. Simplistic and repetitive |
| Flat vector gradients as the main style | Generic, with little depth or light on figures |
| Pictures that repeat the narration | Marcus: too literal |
| Serpent as a hatched tube with a cartoon head | Reads as a medical symbol or a worm |
| Anton, Bebas and Montserrat | Off brand |
| Captions mid-frame, and verse cards over the action | Compete with the image |
| Shots under 1 s outside transitions | Choppy |
| Accepting a preset's script because the prompt was short | The wrong script reached audio and render |
| Cost checks that haven't been announced | Looked like unapproved generation |
