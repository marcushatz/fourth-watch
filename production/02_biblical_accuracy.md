# 02 · Biblical research and accuracy

Marcus: "Every claim in every video must be fact-checked." This covers words and pictures. Full reviewer brief: `prompts/A_esv_factcheck.md`. Budget rule: one independent fact-check on the final candidate, covering changed lines (Round 20).

## Research

1. Start from the ESV text itself, not from a summary.
2. Record each claim with its reference.
3. Mark each item as **stated in the text**, **implied**, **interpretation** or **history from outside the Bible**.
4. Use only ideas the text supports. Surprise never justifies a stretch.
5. Outside history is allowed only when Marcus asks. Say it as history, source it on screen, and never let it imply a motive.

## Words

- ESV only. Quotes are word for word, and quoted verbs match the ESV.
- If a spoken quote drops words, flag it before approval.
- Never state an implied event as fact.
- Nothing is called "last" or "first" unless the text makes it so.
- Paraphrases get the same check as quotes.
- Interpretive claims must sound like interpretation, or be grounded in a cited verse.
- After any cut, re-check every pronoun.
- Never assume the viewer has strayed. No finger-pointing lines.

## Pictures

Check every shot against four questions:

1. Who does what, where and when, according to the text?
2. What has the picture added that the text doesn't say?
3. Is the addition neutral staging, or does it create a claim?
4. Should it be labelled or styled as symbolic?

**Rules:**
- A place the text doesn't state stays generic. Don't label it.
- Symbolic images must read as symbolic, not as events.
- No gore, no wounds, nobody shown dead.
- Never let an image model write Scripture or any other text. All text is added in the edit.
- Depict Jesus with restraint. Marcus approves the design before it's used in generated art.

## Bronze Serpent fences

From Rounds 28 and 29 and `ep02-bronze-serpent/creative/direction_v2.md`:

- God commanded "a fiery serpent" (Num 21:8). Moses made it of bronze (Num 21:9).
- 2 Kings 18:4 says "offerings". People may kneel holding bowls, but no prostration.
- The serpent's location in Hezekiah's day isn't stated: a generic stone court, never labelled "temple".
- The pole is straight, with no crossbar.
- The cross appears only as a comparison or a distant final image, grounded in John 12:32–33.
