# 01 · Brand identity and visual references

## Who we are

Fourth Watch is a faceless Christian educational channel. Each episode reveals a surprising, textually supported connection within Scripture.

**We make:** intelligent, reverent, clear and visually memorable explanations.

**We don't make:** generic devotional content, shallow retellings, invented biblical details, exaggerated theological claims or generic automated cartoon videos.

**The standard (Marcus, Nov 1):** every visual deepens understanding, raises curiosity, clarifies a connection, or heightens the emotional impact of a revelation.

## Target visual system (Marcus, Nov 2)

Professional art direction comes before automatic video generation. The visual system must have:
- Sophisticated stylized art. The medium is undecided: cinematic illustrated 2.5D or premium stylized 3D.
- Cinematic compositions and controlled lighting.
- Subtle textures.
- Expressive silhouettes with real form inside them.
- Layered environments.
- Intentional visual metaphors.

Also rejected earlier and still binding: black rim-lit cut-out figures, an all-black silhouette Jesus, and flat vector icon marks.

**The approved look:** none yet. When Marcus picks one, record here its name, the approved still, its palette, its character rules, its prompt block and its model.

## How to use references

- **Study quality, never copy artwork.** Reference reels teach structure, pacing and clarity. Our look stays our own.
- **What to study:** one idea per image, a type layer on screen throughout, repeated framings, match cuts on objects. The measured notes are in `research/visual_techniques.md`.
- Never put other creators' frames or video in this repo.
- Never name a living artist in a prompt. Describe the craft instead.
- A prompt can name an old public-domain tradition, such as fresco or wood engraving.

If AI-generated art is used, the current caption line "Animation drawn in code" becomes untrue and must change. Marcus approves the new wording before anything is posted.
