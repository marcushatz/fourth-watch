# 03 · Script structure and narration

The editorial philosophy is `EDITORIAL.md` (Round 74), and the process is the `fourth-watch-writer` skill. `system/02` and `03` are older craft guides, and `EDITORIAL.md` wins where they disagree. This file holds the standing requirements.

## Script requirements

- **Relate, Reveal, Transform:** the script's header states each in one line, along with the one-sentence message. Every line serves the message.
- **Story over lecture:** open on a concrete struggle, question or event, and bring viewers into what actually happened.
- **A genuine reveal:** something the text supports that the viewer probably missed. Never manufactured.
- **Integrity:** every claim is labeled and every quote checked against the ESV. Interpretation is said as interpretation.
- **Ending:** a specific insight, a thoughtful question or a practical application that grows out of the story.
- **Length:** earned. Usually 60–120 s, and about two minutes when the story needs it.
- **Approval:** the nine questions in `EDITORIAL.md` are answered in words. A script never passes on structure or word count alone.

## Locked scripts

Once Marcus approves a script:
1. Save it as `ep*/script_approved*.md` with the date and his words.
2. Treat it as locked. Nobody rewrites, trims or "tightens" it.
3. If you find a factual error, name it, explain it and propose the fix. Marcus decides.
4. Every voice take must match it word for word. Check with a transcript.
5. If a tool can't preserve it, use a more controllable tool.

**Before writing anything:** look in the episode folder. On Nov 2 a session wrote a new script because it didn't check, and the approved v4 sat unused.

## Narration

- **Order:** the voice comes after script approval and before the storyboard, so shots are timed to the real take.
- **Voice:** not yet decided for the series. Gideon on Eleven v4 has been tried once, on the test script. Marcus chooses the voice.
- **Never** clone or imitate another creator's narrator.
- **Direction:** clear, reverent, intelligent and curious. No sermon voice and no hype.
- **Pace:** about 2.6–2.8 words per second. Pace comes from pauses, never time-stretching.
- **Checks:** transcribe the take with word timings. Tags must not be read aloud, and no word may differ.
- **Pronunciation:** check names such as Hezekiah and Nicodemus.
- **Spoken credit:** an ESV credit is required on video (`system/00_series_canon.md`).
- **Label:** the Instagram AI label stays on for realistic AI narration.
- **Revisions:** changed lines are paid generations and need approval.
