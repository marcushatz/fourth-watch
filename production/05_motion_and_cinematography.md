# 05 · Motion graphics and cinematography

Detail guides: `system/06_camera_and_angles.md`, `system/06c_shot_recipes.md`, `system/13_story_director.md`, `research/visual_techniques.md`.

## The test for every scene

Each scene must advance the viewer's understanding, not merely decorate narration. If a picture only repeats the words, redesign it. Good alternatives:

- A comparison the viewer can see.
- A repeated framing with one meaningful change.
- A match cut that links two objects or events.
- One symbolic object that something happens to.
- A diagram or line that builds.
- A change of light that carries the meaning.

## Storyboard requirements

Every storyboard row needs:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Time | Tied to the real voice take |
| Words | The exact narration |
| Picture | What is on screen |
| Discovery | What the viewer learns |
| Camera | Size, angle, move, and the reason for the move |
| Motion | What changes, and on which word |
| Type | Which text appears |
| Fidelity | Text, staging or symbolic |
| Asset | Reused or new, with the cost if new |

Before asking for approval, critique each sequence, revise it, and name the hardest shots.

## Cinematography

- Vary distance, angle, movement and composition. Every move has a story reason.
- No repeated central layout. Change the subject's position from shot to shot.
- Use high wide shots for the bird's-eye story, and low angles for scale or awe.
- Use a foreground layer in at least every other shot.
- Don't jump between two shots of the same size and angle.

## Motion

- **Meaningful movement only.** A push reveals, a pan connects, and a tilt follows a thought.
- **Layered parallax (2.5D):** separate foreground, subject, middle and far layers.
- **Cloth, dust, light and smoke** add life without new art.
- **Transitions carry meaning:** match cuts, light sweeps and light floods when God acts. Keep flashes rare.
- **Timing:** impacts land on their words. Moves ease in and settle before the cut.
- **Pacing:** something new about every 2 s. Median shot 3–5 s. Nothing under 1 s except a true transition.

## Typography

- One text element at a time.
- Narration captions stay in the top third, build word by word and highlight key words.
- Every text layer must read at phone size, with a safe margin from platform buttons.
- Verse cards are exact ESV, sit next to what they prove, and don't cover the key action.
- Numbers, chips and labels are small, purposeful and timed to words.
- All type is set in the edit, never generated inside an image.

## Sound

- Voice first, with a soft and even bed under it.
- Impacts and transitions land on picture beats.
- −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
