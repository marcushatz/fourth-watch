# Why did Jesus appear first to Mary Magdalene?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 80
answer: She didn't know him when she saw him. She knew him when he said her name.

VO: Why did Jesus appear first to Mary Magdalene? [John 20:14] [John 20:16]

## Stakes
VO: She came to the tomb "early, while it was still dark." [John 20:1]
VO: Luke says seven demons had gone out of her. Now the man who healed her was dead, and the stone was gone. [Luke 8:2] [John 20:1]

## Story

VO: "But Mary stood weeping outside the tomb." [John 20:11]

## Landing
VO: Ten chapters earlier, Jesus had said the shepherd "calls his own sheep by name," and "they know his voice." [John 10:3] [John 10:4]
VO: She didn't know him when she saw him. She knew him when he said her name. [John 20:14] [John 20:16]
