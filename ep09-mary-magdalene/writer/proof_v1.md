## Hook
VO: Why did Jesus appear first to Mary Magdalene? [John 20:14] [John 20:16]

## Stakes
VO: Not Peter. Not John. A woman "from whom seven demons had gone out." [Luke 8:2]

## Story
VO: She came to the tomb "early, while it was still dark," and the stone was gone. [John 20:1]
VO: She ran for Peter and John. They looked in. And then John writes this: "Then the disciples went back to their homes." [John 20:2] [John 20:10]

## Turn

VO: They went home. She stayed. [John 20:10] [John 20:11]

VO: Her eyes hadn't known him. Her name did. [John 20:14] [John 20:16]

## Landing
VO: Then he sends her: "go to my brothers." [John 20:17]
VO: She tells them, "I have seen the Lord." [John 20:18]
VO: And Luke tells us how that went: "these words seemed to them an idle tale, and they did not believe them." [Luke 24:10] [Luke 24:11]
VO: So why her? [John 20:16]
VO: Nobody inventing this story would pick a witness the apostles themselves didn't believe. [Luke 24:11]
VO: But John gives us a simpler reason. [John 20:11]
VO: She came while it was still dark, and she was still there when he came. [John 20:1] [John 20:11] [John 20:14]
