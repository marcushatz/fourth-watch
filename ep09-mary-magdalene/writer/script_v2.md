# Why did Jesus appear first to Mary Magdalene?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 80
answer: She didn't know him by sight. She knew his voice.

<!-- Recommended debut script, Nov 3 2026. Candidate only, not locked. -->

## Hook
VO: Why did Jesus appear first to Mary Magdalene? [John 20:14] [John 20:16]
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID JESUS APPEAR FIRST TO MARY MAGDALENE?

## Stakes
VO: She wasn't one of the twelve. Luke remembers her as the woman "from whom seven demons had gone out." [Luke 8:2]

## Story
VO: She came to the tomb "early, while it was still dark," and the stone was gone. [John 20:1]
VO: She ran for Peter and the other disciple. They saw the empty tomb. And John writes, "Then the disciples went back to their homes." [John 20:2] [John 20:6] [John 20:10]
VO: "But Mary stood weeping outside the tomb." [John 20:11]

## Turn
VO: They went home. She stayed. [John 20:10] [John 20:11]
VO: She thinks someone has taken his body, and now that's all she wants. [John 20:13] [John 20:15]
VO: She turns, and a man is standing there. She thinks he's the gardener. [John 20:14] [John 20:15]
VO: "Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have laid him, and I will take him away." [John 20:15]
VO: She's looking right at him, and she doesn't know him. [John 20:14]
VO: Then he says one word. [John 20:16]
VO: "Mary." [John 20:16]
VERSE: "Jesus said to her, 'Mary.'" — John 20:16 (ESV)
VO: Ten chapters earlier, Jesus had said a shepherd "calls his own sheep by name," and they follow him, "for they know his voice." [John 10:3] [John 10:4]
VO: She didn't know him by sight. She knew his voice. [John 20:14] [John 20:16]

## Landing
VO: Then he sends her to the men who went home: "go to my brothers." [John 20:17]
VO: Luke says that when Mary and the other women told the apostles, "these words seemed to them an idle tale, and they did not believe them." [Luke 24:10] [Luke 24:11]
VO: So why her? John never says. [John 20:16]
VO: He only shows us this. She came while it was still dark, and she was still there when he came. [John 20:1] [John 20:11] [John 20:14]
