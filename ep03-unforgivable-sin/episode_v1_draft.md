# What is the unforgivable sin?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 100
answer: In the text, it is what Jesus's accusers did, calling the Holy Spirit's work the devil's, and in the same breath Jesus said every other sin and blasphemy will be forgiven.

## Hook
VO: What is the unforgivable sin?
ON-SCREEN: WHAT IS THE UNFORGIVABLE SIN?

## Stakes
VO: Jesus said one sin "will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come." [Matthew 12:32]
VO: Many people fear they've done it by accident. But look at the day he said it, and who he said it to. [Matthew 12:22-32]

## Story
VO: Jesus healed a man who was blind and couldn't speak, and the crowds asked, "Can this be the Son of David?" [Matthew 12:22-23]
VO: But the Pharisees said he did it by "Beelzebul, the prince of demons." [Matthew 12:24]
VO: Jesus answered by pointing to "the Spirit of God." [Matthew 12:28]

## Turn
VO: Then came the warning, and notice how it starts: "every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people." [Matthew 12:31]
VERSE: "every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people, but the blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven." — Matthew 12:31 (ESV)
VO: Even "whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven." That's Jesus's name for himself. [Matthew 12:32]
VO: Mark tells us why he said it: "for they were saying, 'He has an unclean spirit.'" [Mark 3:30]
VO: So the unforgivable sin, in the text, is what those men did: calling the Spirit's work the devil's. [Matthew 12:24, 12:28, 12:31; Mark 3:29-30]

## Landing
VO: Christians read it two main ways: calling the Spirit's work evil, as they did, or refusing the Spirit to the very end. [Matthew 12:31-32; Mark 3:28-30]
VO: And look who was forgiven. Paul wrote that he was once "a blasphemer, persecutor, and insolent opponent. But I received mercy." [1 Timothy 1:13]
VO: Peter denied knowing Jesus three times, the last time calling a curse on himself. Later Jesus asked him three times, "Do you love me?" and told him, "Feed my sheep." [Mark 14:66-72; John 21:15-17]
VO: So what is the unforgivable sin? Calling the Spirit's work the devil's. And in the same breath, Jesus said every other sin and blasphemy will be forgiven. [Matthew 12:31-32; Mark 3:28-30]

## Question
VO: What surprised you more: Jesus forgiving words against Himself, or Paul calling himself a blasphemer? [Matthew 12:32; 1 Timothy 1:13]

## Value
- The warning opens with "every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people" [Matthew 12:31]
- Even "whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven" [Matthew 12:32]
- Mark gives the reason Jesus said it: "for they were saying, 'He has an unclean spirit.'" [Mark 3:30]
- Paul calls himself a former "blasphemer" who "received mercy" [1 Timothy 1:13]
