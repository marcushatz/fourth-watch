**ITEM 1: Quote VERIFIED, framing OVERCLAIM.**
- Mark 3:30 ESV reads "for they were saying, 'He has an unclean spirit.'" The "for" ties it to the warning in 3:28-29, so "says why Jesus warned them" is fair.
- "The same scene" overclaims. Mark's accusers are "the scribes who came down from Jerusalem" (3:22), and Mark doesn't mention the blind and mute man or the Pharisees.
- Fix: "Mark records the same accusation and says why Jesus warned them: 'for they were saying, "He has an unclean spirit."'"

**ITEM 2: VERIFIED.** Matthew 12:31 ESV opens "Therefore I tell you, every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people, but the blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven." The forgivable sins come first, and the quote is exact.

**ITEM 3: OVERCLAIM.**
- "Like those men" asserts the Pharisees committed it, and the sources disagree. TGC says "They were on the brink of committing the unpardonable sin." gotquestions says they "committed the unpardonable sin."
- "Two main ways" is a compression. Both sources treat the dispensational view as a distinct third one: the sin was specific to Jesus' earthly ministry and "cannot be duplicated today" (gotquestions).
- "Not by accident" holds for every view. TGC: "not an accidental, impulsive, or unguarded slip of the tongue." CCC 1864 (recall): "deliberately refuses." Augustine's final impenitence (recall) is persistence, not a slip. TGC quotes Calvin as "with evil intention, resist God's truth."
- "Refusing God's mercy until death" fairly summarizes CCC 1864 and Augustine, and gotquestions' "continued unbelief."
- Fix: "Christians explain the sin in a few ways, such as knowingly calling the Spirit's clear work evil, or refusing God's mercy until death. In every case, it isn't something done by accident."

**ITEM 4: OVERCLAIM.**
- "That's why" doesn't follow. The previous line says the sin is deliberate. The fear-as-a-sign point is a separate pastoral argument.
- "Many pastors" is not supported. I found two sources. TGC says it directly: "if you are worried that you have committed the unpardonable sin, that is a reliable sign that you have not committed it." gotquestions only says "If you are suffering under a load of guilt today, rest assured that you have not committed the unpardonable sin." That site is an anonymous Q&A ministry, and the page never says "many pastors."
- Nothing is presented as Scripture, but it should stay clearly labeled as teaching.
- Fix: "Some pastors and Bible teachers say that if you're afraid you've done it, that fear is a sign you haven't."

**ITEM 5: OVERCLAIM (minor).**
- "has an unclean spirit" is exact.
- "By the Spirit of God" is exact as a fragment, but Matthew 12:28 is conditional: "But if it is by the Spirit of God that I cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you." "He said he cast out demons by the Spirit of God" flattens the "if."
- Mark 3:30 gives only "they were saying 'He has an unclean spirit'" as the reason. "Giving the devil credit for the Spirit's work" is a fair synthesis of Matthew 12:24, 12:28 and 12:31, but it is interpretation. Keep "In the text" only if the quotes stay exact.
- Fix: "In the text, Jesus warned men who said he cast out demons by 'Beelzebul, the prince of demons.' He answered, 'if it is by the Spirit of God that I cast out demons,' and Mark says why he warned them: 'they were saying, "He has an unclean spirit."'" [Matthew 12:24, 12:28; Mark 3:30]
- The same conditional wording affects the already-verified script line "Jesus said he cast out demons 'by the Spirit of God.'" You may want to align it.
