The one fix, per judge:
- Cut the Peter detour and the closing recap ('So what is the unforgivable sin? In the text...'), and end on one sharp line from Paul's mercy instead of the quiz-style 'what surprised you more' question.
- Drop the Peter detour (his denial isn't the sin in question) and spend that time making the Mark 3:30 reason land plainly: Jesus said it because they called the Spirit's work an unclean spirit.

AI judges score scripts, not audiences. They flag weak hooks, thin payoffs, little new value and dead lines; they can't measure retention or sharing (Round 17).
