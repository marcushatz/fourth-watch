## Our scripts: what the judges said

### ep03-unforgivable-sin/episode_v5_draft.md: GOOD ENOUGH

Weakest line, per judge:
- "In the text, it's tied to what those men were doing: calling the Spirit's work the devil's."
- "In the text, it's tied to what those men were doing: calling the Spirit's work the devil's."
- "So what is the unforgivable sin?"

The one fix, per judge:
- Cut the recap before the Paul quote, and end on a question about the viewer's real fear (whether they could have done it by accident), not a quiz about which detail surprised them.
- Replace the quiz-style closing question with one aimed at the fear raised in the hook, and let Paul's mercy line be the final beat with its point stated plainly.
- Sharpen the hook into a specific puzzle (Jesus said one sin will never be forgiven, and he said it to men who had just watched him heal a blind, mute man). Simplify the dense middle so the Mark 3 'unclean spirit' explanation lands as the clear payoff.

AI judges score scripts, not audiences. They flag weak hooks, thin payoffs, little new value and dead lines; they can't measure retention or sharing (Round 17).
