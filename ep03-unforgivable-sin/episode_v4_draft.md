## Hook
VO: What is the unforgivable sin?
ON-SCREEN: WHAT IS THE UNFORGIVABLE SIN?

## Stakes
VO: In Matthew 12, Jesus said one sin "will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come." Many people fear they've done it by accident. [Matthew 12:32]

## Story
VO: But look at what had just happened. Jesus healed a demon-oppressed man who was blind and mute, and the Pharisees said he cast out demons only by "Beelzebul, the prince of demons." [Matthew 12:22-24]

## Turn
VO: Then came the warning, and it starts with forgiveness: "every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people." [Matthew 12:31]
VERSE: "every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people, but the blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven." — Matthew 12:31 (ESV)
VO: Even words against Jesus himself: "whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven." [Matthew 12:32]
VO: Mark tells the same scene and gives the reason Jesus said it: "for they were saying, 'He has an unclean spirit.'" [Mark 3:22-30]
VO: But Jesus said he cast out demons "by the Spirit of God." That's what he was warning them about. [Matthew 12:28; Mark 3:29-30]

## Landing
VO: Christians read it two main ways: knowingly calling the Spirit's clear work evil, as those men were doing, or a final, lifelong refusal of the Spirit's call to repent. Either way, it isn't something done by accident. [Matthew 12:31-32; Mark 3:28-30]
VO: And who received mercy? Paul wrote that he was once "a blasphemer, persecutor, and insolent opponent. But I received mercy because I had acted ignorantly in unbelief…" [1 Timothy 1:13]
VO: Peter denied Jesus three times. The last time, he swore, "I do not know this man…" Later Jesus asked him three times, "Do you love me?" and told him, "Feed my sheep." [Mark 14:66-72; John 21:15-17]
VO: So what is the unforgivable sin? In the text, it's tied to what those men were doing: calling the Spirit's work the devil's. And in the same breath, Jesus holds out forgiveness for everything else, even words against himself. [Matthew 12:31-32; Mark 3:28-30]

## Question
VO: What surprised you more: that the warning starts with forgiveness, or why Jesus gave it? [Matthew 12:31; Mark 3:30]

## Value
- The warning starts with forgiveness: "every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people" [Matthew 12:31]
- Even "whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven" [Matthew 12:32]
- Mark gives the reason Jesus said it: "for they were saying, 'He has an unclean spirit.'" [Mark 3:30]
- Paul, once "a blasphemer, persecutor, and insolent opponent," wrote "But I received mercy" [1 Timothy 1:13]
