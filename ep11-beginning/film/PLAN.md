# Ep 11 film: production plan

Script v11 (locked) and the 34-shot storyboard are the foundation. The look is Marcus's Nov 6 choice: Editorial Elegant figures (B) in the Painterly Cinematic world and light (A). Every start and end frame is drawn in code (`src/`), so the cast matches from shot to shot.

## Moving shots made in Higgsfield

Each is image-to-video with a code-drawn first and last frame: Kling 3.0 Omni Frames, Pro, 5 s, 9:16, sound off. That cost 6.25 credits a clip on Nov 6. Every clip is a separate approval, and the first one is a style test.

| Shot | Moment | Why it needs generated motion |
|---|---|---|
| S13 | She gives him the fruit, he eats | Two people and a hand-off. First test |
| S09 | Adam and Eve walk together | Walking |
| S11 | The serpent slides down toward Eve | A creature moving |
| S14 | Their hands sew fig leaves together | Close hand work |
| S15 | The grass bends toward them, they run | Running and moving grass |
| S18 | Adam's head turns toward the gold leaves | His reaction to God's call |
| S21 | Clothes of skin settle on their shoulders | A change of state |
| S22 | Eve touches the clothing and looks up | A small, emotional gesture |
| S29 | Both faces lift a little as the light reaches them | The climax |
| S33 | They walk on at sunrise | Walking |

First-pass estimate: 10 clips × 6.25 = 62.5 credits, before any redo.

## Shots animated in code (free)

The other 24: S01 to S08 (the universe, the question, the easy answers), the verse cards, the high views (S16, S28), the held moments (S17, S25, S26), S19, S20, S23, S24, S27 and S30 to S32, S34. They use parallax on the saved layers, slow pushes and light changes.
