# If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?

target_seconds: 110
answer: Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you.

## 1. The question
VO: If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?

## 2. The stakes
VO: Most people in our lives need something from us. God doesn't.
VO: The New Testament says he isn't served "as though he needed anything." [Acts 17:25]
VO: So what does that make us? Extra?

## 3. The easy answers fail
VO: Maybe he was lonely. But lonely is a need, and he has none.
VO: Maybe he wanted praise. But in Job, God says that when he laid the earth's foundation, "the morning stars sang together." [Job 38:4] [Job 38:7]
VO: There wasn't a single person yet.

## 4. The real reason
VO: So it wasn't need. Nothing forced his hand.
VO: That leaves one reason. He wanted to.
VO: That's easy to say. The real test is the worst day.

## 5. The proof
VO: In Genesis, that day comes fast.
VO: The serpent tells the woman, "you will be like God." [Genesis 3:5]
VO: She eats the fruit. She gives some to the man. He eats. [Genesis 3:6]
VO: They sew fig leaves. They hide among the trees. [Genesis 3:7] [Genesis 3:8]
VO: God doesn't need them. He could leave them hiding.
VO: Then they hear him, walking in the garden. [Genesis 3:8]
VO: And he calls, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]
VO: He didn't need the answer.

## 6. The comeback, then the bigger story
VO: He does send them out of the garden. [Genesis 3:23]
VO: But first, "the LORD God made for Adam and for his wife garments of skins and clothed them." [Genesis 3:21]
VO: The God who needs no servants is dressing the two people who just betrayed him.
VO: Later, Jesus says he "came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life." [Mark 10:45]
VO: He needed nothing. He gave his life anyway.

## 7. The answer
VO: So if God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?
VO: Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you.
VO: Even on the worst day, his first words were, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]

## 8. The ask
VO: Know someone who feels like they're extra? Send them this.
