# Plan for script v1

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** The only thing God called not good in his new world was a person alone, and he didn't call it very good until that was fixed.

**Form:** dramatic retelling with a reframe. **Opening:** Marcus's question.

## The four parts

| Part | In this story |
|---|---|
| Desire | God is finishing a world he calls good. The viewer wants to know what could spoil it |
| Obstacle that grows | Alone, then a promise, then every creature comes and none is like him |
| What can't be undone | God puts the man to sleep and makes the woman |
| Unexpected outcome | "Not good" turns out to come before "very good," not after it |

## Feeling and understanding

| Beat | Event | Viewer feels | Viewer understands |
|---|---|---|---|
| Six times good | The world is made | Wonder, a steady rhythm | Everything God makes is good |
| "It is not good" | God breaks the pattern | Shock | Something's wrong before sin |
| "That the man should be alone" | The answer | Recognition | The first "not good" is a person alone |
| "God said it first" | The turn to the viewer | Comfort | Feeling this isn't a failure |
| The animals | God makes him wait | Ache | None of them is like him |
| "At last" | The first human words | Joy | He'd been waiting |
| "He just wasn't finished" | The reframe | Surprise | The order of events changes the meaning |
| "Very good" | The last line | Awe and peace | The question is answered |

## Claims

| Line | Label | Support |
|---|---|---|
| Six "good" statements, then "very good" | TEXT | Genesis 1:4, 10, 12, 18, 21, 25, 31. Verse 4 reads "the light was good." The text says God "saw," and the question says "call" |
| "Darkness covered the deep" | TEXT, paraphrase | Genesis 1:2 |
| Food, work, God speaking to him | TEXT | Genesis 2:9, 15, 16 |
| "Hasn't said once," "first thing God ever calls not good" | TEXT | Genesis 2:18 is the first "not good" in the Bible |
| No sin, serpent or death yet | TEXT | They come in Genesis 3 and later |
| "God said it first" | INFERRED | God says being alone is not good. The text doesn't say the man felt lonely, so the script doesn't either |
| God promises, then brings the animals first | TEXT | Genesis 2:18 to 20, in that order |
| "First human words in the Bible" | TEXT | Genesis 2:23 is the first quoted human speech. The naming isn't quoted |
| "Very good" only once, on the sixth day, after "male and female" | TEXT | Genesis 1:27, 1:31 |
| Genesis 2 is a closer look at that day | INTERPRETATION | A common reading, and the script says "seems." Others read the chapters as separate accounts |
| "Not good" came before "very good" | INFERRED | Depends on that reading |
| "When no one in the world is alone" | INFERRED | Follows from male and female both existing before verse 31 |

Quotes checked against esv.org on Nov 5.

## Risks

- **Single viewers:** the woman is the answer in the story. The script says "alone," never "unmarried," but someone single could still hear "you need a spouse."
- **"Is God not enough?":** the line "He's the one saying it" answers this, but a pastor should read it.
- **The reframe rests on a reading.** The script says "seems," and the text-only part still stands without it.

## What changed in revision

The first draft ended on an explanation and then "God said it first." Moving that line to the middle lets the video end on Scripture and the answer to the hook. The draft then ran 342 words, over two minutes, so it was trimmed to about 300.
