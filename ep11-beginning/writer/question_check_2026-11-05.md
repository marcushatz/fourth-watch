## Reading

The structure wasn't the main problem. The question was. Lintel's blueprint is for a hard objection people already hold, and ours is a puzzle about two words. The script had to invent its stakes, and the fall became an add-on, which is why section 6 grew to 29%.

**Proposed replacement, not approved:** "If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?" It stays in the beginning of the world. Its stakes are the viewer's own worth, and the fall becomes the proof instead of an add-on.
