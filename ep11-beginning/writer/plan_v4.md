# Plan for script v4

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** A person alone was the one thing God wouldn't leave that way, even after sin.

**Blueprint:** the fix that fails (`.claude/skills/fourth-watch-story/references/blueprints.md`). The eight section jobs come from that structure. The topic, argument, examples and words are ours.

## How it differs from the source

| Section | The source's material | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Objection | Why God left the tree | Why God said "not good" |
| Easy fix | A fix the viewer imagines | Two fixes the text itself runs: more of God, and the animals |
| Real reason | Freedom and love | People were made to need each other, and the first job needs more than one person |
| Proof | A verse from Deuteronomy | The man's own words in Genesis 2:23 |
| Comeback | Sin was planned for | "Very good" comes after "not good" |
| Bigger story | A second tree | The serpent, the fall and God's first question |
| Ask | A one-word comment | Reach out to someone |

## Risks

- **Single viewers:** "One man can't do that" is about humanity's calling. The line could still sound like a comment on marriage or children, so it needs a pastoral read.
- **The ending line** states more than any one verse says.
- **Timing of the command:** Genesis 1:28 is spoken after both people exist, so "One man can't do that" is our reasoning from the verse, not the reason Genesis 2 gives.
- **The proportions differ from the source.** The order matches, but the easy-fix section is shorter than the source's, and section 6 is much longer because it carries the fall. Marcus asked for the serpent and the fruit, and that outranks the blueprint.
- **The story of the fall is now very compressed** to keep the length down.
