# Plan for script v8

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** God doesn't need us. He made us because he wanted us, and he kept caring for us even after we turned against him.

**Blueprint:** the fix that fails (`.claude/skills/fourth-watch-story/references/blueprints.md`). The eight section jobs come from that structure. The topic, argument, examples and words are ours.

## How v8 stays clear

- The narrator says each claim in plain words.
- The narrator names the passage for each major claim.
- The exact ESV verse appears on screen, so the viewer can see it's in the Bible.
- The script states what a first-time viewer wouldn't know, such as God forbidding the fruit.

## Verse cards

Exact ESV. One card at a time, and each stays up long enough to read. These are locked to the narration line beside them.

| Narration | Card |
|---|---|
| "The Bible says in Acts 17:25..." | **Acts 17:25:** "nor is he served by human hands, as though he needed anything, since he himself gives to all mankind life and breath and everything." |
| "That's what ancient Babylon believed about its gods." | Label only: **Enuma Elish, ancient Babylonian creation story** |
| "But the Bible says the opposite." | **Acts 17:25** again, showing the end of the verse: "since he himself gives to all mankind life and breath and everything." |
| "Genesis 3:8 says..." | **Genesis 3:8:** "And they heard the sound of the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool of the day, and the man and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the LORD God among the trees of the garden." |
| "But he calls out..." | **Genesis 3:9:** "But the LORD God called to the man and said to him, 'Where are you?'" |
| "Genesis 3:21 says..." | **Genesis 3:21:** "And the LORD God made for Adam and for his wife garments of skins and clothed them." |
| "John 3:16 says..." | **John 3:16:** "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life." |
| "Revelation 4:11 says..." | **Revelation 4:11:** "for you created all things, and by your will they existed and were created." |

The Genesis 3:8 card is the longest, so the picture needs to hold long enough to read it. Don't draw God as a man walking.

## Plain words for verses

| Narration says | Verse says | Why it's fair |
|---|---|---|
| "God doesn't need anything from us" | "nor is he served by human hands, as though he needed anything" | Same meaning. Paul is speaking about temples and idols, but "anything" is general. Psalm 50:12 says the same about sacrifices |
| "God gives us 'life and breath and everything'" | "he himself gives to all mankind life and breath and everything" | Same meaning, and the quoted words are exact. "The opposite" compares this with Babylon's belief about who works for whom. People still have work in Genesis 2:15 |
| "They hear God walking in the garden, and they hide" | Genesis 3:8 | Same order and same action |
| "God makes them clothes" | "made for Adam and for his wife garments of skins and clothed them" | Same action |
| "God created everything because he chose to" | "by your will they existed and were created" | "By your will" means by his own choice |

## Readings to know about

- **"Where are you?"** It's also the start of God's questioning, and judgment follows. The script includes the judgment, but the warmth of the ending is our reading.
- **"Walking in the garden":** Christians differ on how literally to take it. The script reports what Genesis says and doesn't explain how God was present.
- **Book count:** Acts, Genesis, John and Revelation, plus one historical fact. Marcus's newest note, that claims need named Scripture, outranks the older limit of three books.
- **A pastor should read it** before it's locked. No outside reader has seen it.
