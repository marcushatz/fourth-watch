# If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?
series: FOURTH WATCH
target_seconds: 126
answer: Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you.

<!-- Nov 5 2026. Candidate only, not approved, not locked. After "Where are you?" the script now explains it, applies it, concludes and closes on an encouraging question (Round 60). Verse cards and claims: plan_v10.md -->

## 1. The question
VO: If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?

## 2. The stakes
VO: The Bible says in Acts 17:25 that God doesn't need anything from us. [Acts 17:25]
VO: But we all want to feel needed.
VO: So do we even matter to him?

## 3. The easy answers fail
VO: Maybe he was lonely. But that would mean he needed us.
VO: Maybe he wanted our worship. He deserves it, but he doesn't need it. [Acts 17:25]

## 4. The turn
VO: So what's the real reason? It shows up when we turn against him.

## 5. The story
VO: In Genesis, God told Adam and Eve not to eat from one tree. [Genesis 2:17] [Genesis 3:3]
VO: But the serpent tells Eve she'll "be like God" if she does. [Genesis 3:5]
VO: She eats the fruit. She gives some to Adam, and he eats. [Genesis 3:6]
VO: They realize they're naked, and they sew fig leaves together. [Genesis 3:7]
VO: Genesis 3:8 says they hear God walking in the garden, and they hide. [Genesis 3:8]
VO: God doesn't need them. He could just leave them there.
VO: But he calls out to them. We'll come back to what he said. [Genesis 3:9]

## 6. The comeback
VO: There are consequences. They will die, and God sends them out of the garden. [Genesis 3:19] [Genesis 3:23]
VO: But before they leave, Genesis 3:21 says God makes them clothes. [Genesis 3:21]
VO: They turned against him, and he's still taking care of them.

## 7. The moment
VO: Here's what he said, in Genesis 3:9.
VO: God's first words to them aren't "How could you?"
VO: They're "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]

## 8. What it means
VO: Hebrews 4:13 says no one is hidden from God. [Hebrews 4:13]
VO: He wasn't asking for information. He was calling them out of hiding.

## 9. How it applies to us
VO: And he never stopped. John 3:16 says, "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son." [John 3:16]
VO: So when we fail, we don't have to hide from God. Hebrews 4:16 says we can come to him "with confidence." [Hebrews 4:16]

## 10. The conclusion
VO: So why did God make us?
VO: Revelation 4:11 says he created everything because he chose to. [Revelation 4:11]
VO: Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you.
VO: If he still wanted Adam and Eve after all that, why would he ever stop wanting you?

## Caption
Acts 17, Genesis 2 and 3, Hebrews 4, John 3:16, Revelation 4:11. Scripture quotations are from the ESV.
