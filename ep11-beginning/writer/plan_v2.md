# Plan for script v2

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** The first thing God called not good was a man alone, and the first thing he did after sin was go looking for him.

**Form:** fast dramatic retelling from creation to the fall. **Opening:** the broken pattern.

## What changed from v1

| Marcus's note | Change |
|---|---|
| The opening takes too long | Three examples in one line, and "not good" arrives by about 8 seconds |
| Too slow, fast-forward the story | 33 fewer spoken words, even with Genesis 3 added |
| The serpent and the fruit can't be left out | The story now runs through the fall |
| The emotional moment comes too early and interrupts | "Weak faith" is gone. The feeling comes from the events |
| The ending explains chronology | The chronology is one beat, and the ending is God coming to find him |
| Experience Adam's solitude first | "Not one of them can answer him," then a pause |
| Don't imply singleness is incomplete | The "no one in the world" line is gone. The ending is about God looking for a person |
| Remove "Nobody has died" | Removed |
| "Very good" covers the whole creation | The script says only that Genesis says it now, not that the woman caused it |
| Genesis 2 as a zoom is an interpretive choice | The script says "Read Genesis 1 and 2 together" |

## The four parts

| Part | In this story |
|---|---|
| Desire | A man with everything, and no one like him |
| Obstacle that grows | Every creature comes, and none can answer. Then one does, and it's the serpent |
| What can't be undone | They eat |
| Unexpected outcome | The God they're hiding from comes looking |

## Feeling

| Beat | Event | Viewer feels |
|---|---|---|
| "It is not good" | The pattern breaks | Surprise |
| "He's the one saying it" | God names the need | A deep recognition |
| "Not one of them can answer him" | Silence after the naming | Ache |
| "At last" | The first human words | Joy |
| "He just wasn't finished" | The reframe | Understanding |
| "One of the animals talks" | The answer nobody wanted | Dread |
| "Alone again" | The hiding and the blame | Heartbreak |
| "Where are you?" | God comes | Relief and awe |
| "Go looking for him" | The two firsts meet | Hope |

## Planted and paid off

- "Not one of them can answer him" pays off in "Then one of the animals talks." Genesis 3:1 calls the serpent a "beast of the field," the same phrase as Genesis 2:19 and 2:20.
- "Not good" and "alone" return as "Alone again."
- The first line's "called not good" returns in the last two lines.

## Risks

- **"So he comes" gives God a reason the text doesn't state.** It's the emotional hinge, and it's our inference. A softer line would be "And he comes walking through the garden."
- **"Alone again"** is our reading of two people who are physically together.
- **The ending leaves out judgment.** Genesis 3 goes on to curses and exile. The video stops before them on purpose.
