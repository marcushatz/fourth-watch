# Plan for script v3

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** The first thing God called not good was a man alone, and the first thing he said after sin was, "Where are you?"

**Form:** fast dramatic retelling from creation to the fall. **Opening:** the broken pattern.

## What changed from v2

| Critique | Change |
|---|---|
| "No serpent" isn't accurate, because the serpent already existed | Now "No sin yet" |
| "Not one of them can answer him" makes speech the problem | Now "Not one of them is like him," after the verse. "One of those animals" keeps the link to the serpent |
| "Behind leaves. Behind trees." joins two separate actions | Now two plain sentences |
| "So he comes" gives God a motive the text doesn't state | Removed. God simply comes |
| "Alone again" reaches past the text | Removed. "He finally has someone like him. And he's blaming her." joins the two halves |
| The two halves need an unmistakable connection | The same line, and the final pair of "firsts" |
| Keep "Where are you?" | It's heard once as an event, and again as the last line |
| Order of events | Now strictly in order, so "will soon" is gone |
| "Go looking for him" says more than the text | Now "The first thing he said after sin" |

**Not adopted:** "Already alone" and "Not one of them answers." Both still go beyond the text.

## The four parts

| Part | In this story |
|---|---|
| Desire | A man with everything, and no one like him |
| Obstacle that grows | Every creature comes, and none is like him. Then one of them talks |
| What can't be undone | They eat |
| Unexpected outcome | He has what he waited for, and he blames her. God's first words are a question |

## Feeling

| Beat | Event | Viewer feels |
|---|---|---|
| "It is not good" | The pattern breaks | Surprise |
| "He's the one saying it" | God names the need | Recognition |
| "Not one of them is like him" | The list ends | Ache |
| "At last" | The first human words | Joy |
| "He just wasn't finished" | The reframe | Understanding |
| "One of those animals talks" | The serpent | Dread |
| "Where are you?" | God comes | Tension, tenderness |
| "And he's blaming her" | Before and after meet | Heartbreak |
| The last two lines | The two firsts meet | Awe and hope |

## Claims

| Line | Label | Support |
|---|---|---|
| God called the light, sea and living things good | TEXT | Genesis 1:4, 10, 25. The text says God "saw" it was good |
| First "not good," before sin | TEXT | Genesis 2:18, before Genesis 3 |
| Food, work, God speaking to him | TEXT | Genesis 2:9, 15, 16 |
| Every beast and bird named | TEXT | Genesis 2:19, 20 |
| "Not one of them is like him" | INFERRED | Plain sense of "not found a helper fit for him" |
| First human words in the Bible | TEXT | Genesis 2:23 is the first quoted human speech |
| "Only now does Genesis say" | INTERPRETATION | Reads Genesis 2 as a closer look at day six. Others read the chapters as separate accounts. "Very good" covers everything God made |
| "He just wasn't finished" | INFERRED | Depends on that reading |
| "One of those animals talks" | TEXT | Genesis 3:1 calls the serpent a "beast of the field," as in Genesis 2:19, 20 |
| Eats, gives, eats | TEXT | Genesis 3:6. Genesis says fruit, not apple |
| Leaves, then trees | TEXT | Genesis 3:7, 8 |
| God walking, calling, "Where are you?" | TEXT | Genesis 3:8, 9 |
| "Bone of my bones" to the blame | TEXT | Genesis 2:23, 3:12 |

Quotes checked against esv.org on Nov 5.

## Risks

- **The last line is a question.** Marcus's rule is never to end on an unanswered question. The video's own question is answered, and this one is God's words, but it's his call.
- **The ending stops before judgment.** Genesis 3 goes on to curses and exile, and the video leaves them out on purpose.
