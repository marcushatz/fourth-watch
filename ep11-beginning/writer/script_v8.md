# If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?
series: FOURTH WATCH
target_seconds: 117
answer: Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you.

<!-- Nov 5 2026. Candidate only, not approved, not locked. Rewritten for clarity after Marcus's notes (Round 58). Each major claim names its passage aloud, and the exact verse appears on screen. Verse cards and claims: plan_v8.md -->

## 1. The question
VO: If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?

## 2. The stakes
VO: The Bible says in Acts 17:25 that God doesn't need anything from us. [Acts 17:25]
VO: But we all want to feel needed.
VO: So if God doesn't need us, do we even matter to him?

## 3. The easy answers fail
VO: Maybe he was lonely. But that would mean he needed us.
VO: Maybe he wanted our worship. He deserves it, but he doesn't need it. [Acts 17:25]
VO: Maybe he made us to work for him. That's what ancient Babylon believed about its gods.

## 4. The real reason
VO: But the Bible says the opposite. God gives us "life and breath and everything." [Acts 17:25]
VO: We need everything from him. He needs nothing from us.
VO: But would he still want us if we turned against him?

## 5. The proof
VO: Genesis answers that.
VO: God told Adam and Eve not to eat from one tree. [Genesis 2:17] [Genesis 3:3]
VO: But the serpent tells Eve she'll "be like God" if she does. [Genesis 3:5]
VO: She eats the fruit. She gives some to Adam, and he eats. [Genesis 3:6]
VO: They realize they're naked, and they sew fig leaves together. [Genesis 3:7]
VO: Genesis 3:8 says they hear God walking in the garden, and they hide. [Genesis 3:8]
VO: God doesn't need them. He could just leave them there.
VO: But he calls out, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]

## 6. The comeback, then the bigger story
VO: There are consequences. They will die, and God sends them out of the garden. [Genesis 3:19] [Genesis 3:23]
VO: But before they leave, Genesis 3:21 says God makes them clothes. [Genesis 3:21]
VO: They turned against him, and he's still taking care of them.
VO: And he never stopped. John 3:16 says, "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son." [John 3:16]
VO: He gave his Son to die for us. [Romans 5:8]

## 7. The answer
VO: So why did he make you?
VO: Revelation 4:11 says God created everything because he chose to. [Revelation 4:11]
VO: Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you.
VO: Even after sin, God's first words were, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]

## 8. The ask
VO: Send this to someone who feels unwanted.

## Caption
Acts 17, Genesis 2 and 3, John 3:16, Revelation 4:11. Scripture quotations are from the ESV.
