# Why did God call everything good, then say something was not good?
series: FOURTH WATCH
target_seconds: 120
answer: God wasn't finding a flaw. He just wasn't finished.

## Hook
VO: Why did God call everything good, then say something was not good? [Genesis 1:31] [Genesis 2:18]
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID GOD SAY "NOT GOOD"?

## Story
VO: In the beginning, darkness covered the deep. [Genesis 1:2]
VO: God says, "Let there be light." [Genesis 1:3]
VO: And he calls it good. [Genesis 1:4]
VO: Seas and dry land. Good. [Genesis 1:10]
VO: Trees and fruit. Good. [Genesis 1:12]
VO: Sun, moon and stars. Good. [Genesis 1:18]
VO: Everything that swims and flies. Good. [Genesis 1:21]
VO: Every animal on the land. Good. [Genesis 1:25]
VO: Then God makes a man and puts him in a garden. [Genesis 2:7] [Genesis 2:8]
VO: He has food. He has work. He has God speaking to him. [Genesis 2:9] [Genesis 2:15] [Genesis 2:16]
VO: But then God says something he hasn't said once. [Genesis 2:18]
VO: "It is not good." [Genesis 2:18]
VO: No sin yet. No serpent. Nobody has died. [Genesis 3]
VO: So what isn't good?
VO: "That the man should be alone." [Genesis 2:18]

## Turn
VO: The first thing God calls not good isn't sin. It's a man by himself. [Genesis 2:18]
VO: And God isn't missing. He's the one saying it. [Genesis 2:18]
VO: So if being alone has ever felt wrong, that isn't weak faith. God said it first. [Genesis 2:18]
VO: God promises someone for him. But first, he brings the animals. [Genesis 2:18] [Genesis 2:19]
VO: Every beast. Every bird. The man names them all. [Genesis 2:19] [Genesis 2:20]
VO: "But for Adam there was not found a helper fit for him." [Genesis 2:20]
VO: Not one is like him. [Genesis 2:20]
VO: So God puts him to sleep, makes a woman from his rib, and brings her to him. [Genesis 2:21] [Genesis 2:22]
VO: Then come the first human words in the Bible. [Genesis 2:23]
VO: "This at last is bone of my bones." [Genesis 2:23]
VO: At last. [Genesis 2:23]

## Landing
VO: Now go back to Genesis 1. God says "very good" once, on day six, after "male and female he created them." [Genesis 1:27] [Genesis 1:31]
VO: Genesis 2 seems to zoom in on that same day.
VO: So "not good" didn't come after "very good." It came before it.
VO: God wasn't finding a flaw. He just wasn't finished.
VO: Only when no one in the world is alone does Genesis say:
VO: "And God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very good." [Genesis 1:31]
