# Plan for script v5

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** God didn't make us because he needed us. He wanted us, and the worst day didn't change that.

**Why this question:** see `question_check_2026-11-05.md`. People already watch it, and the viewer's own worth depends on the answer.

**Blueprint:** the fix that fails (`.claude/skills/fourth-watch-story/references/blueprints.md`). The eight section jobs come from that structure. The topic, argument, examples and words are ours.

## The story

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| Desire | The viewer wants to matter for more than being useful |
| Obstacle that grows | God needs nothing, then the easy reasons fail, then people betray him |
| What can't be undone | The fruit |
| Unexpected outcome | The God who needs nothing calls for them, then clothes them |

## How it feels

| Section | Feeling | Event that causes it |
|---|---|---|
| Question, stakes | Unease | God needs nothing from us |
| Easy answers | Curiosity | Two reasons break |
| Real reason | Hope, then doubt | "He wanted to," then "the real test" |
| Proof | Dread, then ache | The fruit, the hiding, "Where are you?" |
| Comeback | Surprise, then awe | The clothes, then Jesus |
| Answer | Being wanted | The opening question gets its answer |

## Questions the hook raises

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Does God really need nothing? | Acts 17:25 |
| Was he lonely? | Section 3 |

## Claims

| Line | Label | Support |
|---|---|---|

| "The morning stars sang together" | TEXT | Job 38:4, 7, spoken by God. Poetry. Reading it as praise is INTERPRETATION |

Every quote was checked against esv.org on Nov 5.

## Risks

- **Motive:** Genesis doesn't state why God made people, and "he wanted you" is our conclusion. The script signals it as reasoning but doesn't say so outright.
- **Book count:** Acts, Job, Genesis and Mark. That's one more than the usual three books and a passing fact, but all four stay on one thread.
- **Mark and Acts** are linked by meaning only. The script doesn't claim Mark is answering Acts, and a shared word doesn't carry the climax.
- **Praise:** the line can't suggest God doesn't want worship. Revelation 4:11 calls him worthy of it. It needs a pastoral read.
- **"Most people... need something from us"** could sound cynical.
