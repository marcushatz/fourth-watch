answer: Because a person alone was the one thing he wouldn't leave that way.

## 1. The question
VO: Why did God call everything good, then say something was not good? [Genesis 1:31] [Genesis 2:18]

## 2. The stakes
VO: There's no sin yet. The man has food, work, and God. [Genesis 2:9] [Genesis 2:15] [Genesis 2:16]
VO: And God says, "It is not good that the man should be alone." [Genesis 2:18]
VO: So either God made a mistake, or God wasn't enough.

## 3. The easy answers fail
VO: Maybe he needs more of God. But God isn't missing. He's the one saying it. [Genesis 2:18]
VO: Maybe he needs company. So God brings him the animals, and the man names them all. [Genesis 2:19] [Genesis 2:20]
VO: "But for Adam there was not found a helper fit for him." [Genesis 2:20]

## 4. The real reason
VO: So the problem isn't God. The man was made to need someone like him.
VO: The first job God gives human beings is to "fill the earth." One man can't do that. [Genesis 1:28]

## 5. The proof
VO: So God puts him to sleep, takes a rib, and makes a woman. [Genesis 2:21] [Genesis 2:22]
VO: And the man says the first human words in the Bible. [Genesis 2:23]
VO: "This at last is bone of my bones." [Genesis 2:23]
VO: At last. [Genesis 2:23]

## 6. The comeback, then the bigger story
VO: Read Genesis 1 and 2 together, and "very good" comes after this. [Genesis 1:31]
VO: God wasn't finding a flaw. He just wasn't finished.
VO: Then one of those animals talks. [Genesis 3:1]
VO: She eats the fruit. She gives some to the man. He eats. [Genesis 3:6]
VO: They hide among the trees, and God calls, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:8] [Genesis 3:9]
VO: The man who said "bone of my bones" now says, "The woman whom you gave to be with me, she gave me fruit." [Genesis 2:23] [Genesis 3:12]
VO: He finally has someone like him. And he's blaming her.

## 7. The answer
VO: So why did God say "not good"?
VO: Because a person alone was the one thing he wouldn't leave that way.
VO: Even after sin, his first words were, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]

## 8. The ask
VO: Think of someone you haven't heard from in a while. Ask them where they are.
