# Plan for script v6

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** God doesn't need anything, so he made us to give, not to take. If he didn't need you and made you anyway, you're wanted.

**Blueprint:** the fix that fails (`.claude/skills/fourth-watch-story/references/blueprints.md`). The eight section jobs come from that structure. The topic, argument, examples and words are ours.

## What changed from v5

| Problem in v5 | Fix in v6 |
|---|---|
| "They hide," then "they hear him" reversed Genesis 3:8 | They hear him, then they hide |
| "That leaves one reason" contradicted Scripture, which also says we were made for his glory and for him | Removed. The argument is now only about need |
| "He wanted to" was stated with no text behind it | The script now rests on the end of Acts 17:25, and "wanted" is shown as reasoning: "If he didn't need you and made you anyway" |
| "Maybe he wanted praise" could sound like God doesn't want worship | "Maybe he needed praise. He deserves it" |
| Death was left out of the consequences | "They will die, and he sends them out" |
| "That day comes fast" and "on the worst day" implied one day, which the text doesn't state | "It takes three chapters" and "Even after sin" |
| Mark 10:45 stopped before "ransom" | The quote now includes it, and "He paid for us" explains it |
| "Most people in our lives need something from us" sounded cynical | "We feel like we matter when someone needs us" |
| "He didn't need them" repeated the hook | Cut |
| Job added a fourth book | Replaced by one historical fact, which keeps to three books and a passing fact |

## What the viewer should know is a reading

- **"Where are you?"** The text doesn't say this is tender. It's also the start of God's questioning, and judgment follows. The script says what happened, includes the judgment, and lets the viewer feel it, but the warmth of the ending is our reading.
- **Why God made people:** Scripture gives more than one answer. This script answers only whether he needed to.
- **A pastor should read it** before it's locked. No outside reader has seen it.
