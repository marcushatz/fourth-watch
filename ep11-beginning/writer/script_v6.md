# If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?

target_seconds: 103
answer: If he didn't need you and made you anyway, you're not extra, you're wanted.

## 1. The question
VO: If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?

## 2. The stakes
VO: The New Testament says he isn't served "as though he needed anything." [Acts 17:25]
VO: But we feel like we matter when someone needs us.
VO: So what does that make us? Extra?

## 3. The easy answers fail
VO: Maybe he was lonely. But lonely is a need.
VO: Maybe he needed praise. He deserves it, but "anything" means anything. [Acts 17:25]
VO: Maybe he needed workers. Ancient Babylon's creation story says people were made to carry the gods' work, so the gods could rest.
VO: In Genesis, God feeds them: "I have given you every plant." [Genesis 1:29]

## 4. The real reason
VO: The verse we started with ends, "he himself gives to all mankind life and breath and everything." [Acts 17:25]
VO: He isn't taking. He's giving.
VO: The real test is what he does when we turn on him.

## 5. The proof
VO: It takes three chapters.
VO: The serpent says, "you will be like God." [Genesis 3:5]
VO: The woman eats the fruit. She gives some to the man. He eats. [Genesis 3:6]
VO: They sew fig leaves. [Genesis 3:7]
VO: They hear God walking in the garden, and they hide. [Genesis 3:8]
VO: He could leave them there.
VO: But he calls out, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]
VO: He didn't need the answer.

## 6. The comeback, then the bigger story
VO: Sin costs them. They will die, and he sends them out of the garden. [Genesis 3:19] [Genesis 3:23]
VO: But first, God makes them clothes out of skins, and dresses them himself. [Genesis 3:21]
VO: They turned on him, and he's still giving.
VO: Later, Jesus says he came "to give his life as a ransom for many." [Mark 10:45]
VO: He needed nothing from us. He paid for us anyway.

## 7. The answer
VO: So why did he make you?
VO: If he didn't need you and made you anyway, you're not extra, you're wanted.
VO: Even after sin, his first words were, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]

## 8. The ask
VO: Send this to someone who feels extra.
