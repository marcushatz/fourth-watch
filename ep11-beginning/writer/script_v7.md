# If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?
series: FOURTH WATCH
target_seconds: 107
answer: Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you.

## 1. The question
VO: If God doesn't need anything, why did he make you?

## 2. The stakes
VO: The Bible says God isn't "served by human hands, as though he needed anything." [Acts 17:25]
VO: But being needed is how we feel like we matter.
VO: So what does that make us? Unnecessary?

## 3. The easy answers fail
VO: Maybe he was lonely. But then he'd need us.
VO: Maybe he wanted our praise. He deserves it, but he doesn't need it.
VO: Maybe he needed workers. That's the old Babylonian creation story: the gods made people to do their work, so the gods could rest.

## 4. The real reason
VO: The Bible says the opposite: "he himself gives to all mankind life and breath and everything." [Acts 17:25]
VO: We don't keep God going. He keeps us going. [Acts 17:28]
VO: But what happens when we turn on him?

## 5. The proof
VO: In Genesis 3, we find out.
VO: The serpent tells Eve, "you will be like God." [Genesis 3:5]
VO: She eats the fruit. She gives some to Adam. He eats. [Genesis 3:6]
VO: They sew fig leaves to cover themselves. [Genesis 3:7]
VO: Genesis says they heard "the sound of the LORD God walking in the garden." [Genesis 3:8]
VO: That's how close he was.
VO: And they hide. [Genesis 3:8]
VO: He could walk away.
VO: But he calls out, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]
VO: He didn't need the answer.

## 6. The comeback, then the bigger story
VO: It costs them. They'll die one day, and God sends them out of the garden. [Genesis 3:19] [Genesis 3:23]
VO: But before they leave, God makes them clothes, and covers them himself. [Genesis 3:21]
VO: They turned on him, and he's still giving.
VO: He never stopped. John 3:16: "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son." [John 3:16]
VO: He needed nothing from us. He gave his Son to die for us. [Romans 5:8]

## 7. The answer
VO: So why did he make you?
VO: Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you.
VO: Even after sin, his first words were, "Where are you?" [Genesis 3:9]

## 8. The ask
VO: Send this to someone who feels unwanted.
