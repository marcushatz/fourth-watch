# Plan for script v7

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** God doesn't need anything, so he didn't make us to get something. He made us because he wanted us, and even our sin didn't stop him giving.

**Blueprint:** the fix that fails (`.claude/skills/fourth-watch-story/references/blueprints.md`). The eight section jobs come from that structure. The topic, argument, examples and words are ours.

## What changed from v6

| v6 | v7 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "To give his life as a ransom for many" | "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son" | Marcus's note. Same meaning, far better known, and God is the one giving |
| "They hear God walking in the garden" | "Genesis says they heard 'the sound of the LORD God walking in the garden.'" | Marcus's note. It now comes from Genesis in its exact words, not from us |
| "Extra" | "Unnecessary," then "unwanted" in the ask | "Extra" is slang for over the top |
| "The woman," "the man" | Eve, Adam | Names people know |
| "The verse we started with ends" | "The Bible says the opposite" | Reads like an essay |
| "Lonely is a need" | "But then he'd need us" | Plain speech |
| "'Anything' means anything" | "He deserves it, but he doesn't need it" | Plain speech |
| "He isn't taking. He's giving." | "We don't keep God going. He keeps us going." | Clearer, and "he doesn't take" would clash with Job 1:21 |
| "The real test is..." and "It takes three chapters" | "But what happens when we turn on him?" and "In Genesis 3, we find out." | A question pulls the viewer forward |
| "They sew fig leaves" | "to cover themselves" | Says why, and sets up "covers them himself" |
| "Dresses them himself" | "Covers them himself" | Ties to the fig leaves |
| "If he didn't need you and made you anyway, you're not extra, you're wanted" | "Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you" | Plain, and John 3:16 now sits right before it |
| "In Genesis, God feeds them" line | Cut | Length |
| Mark | John | The book count stays at three, plus one historical fact |

## Screen notes

- **"Walking in the garden":** show "Genesis 3:8" on screen with the quote. Don't draw God as a man walking. Show the sound moving through the garden, with leaves stirring, and Adam and Eve turning toward it.
- **John 3:16:** show the reference on screen.

## Readings to know about

- **"Where are you?"** It's also the start of God's questioning, and judgment follows. The script includes the judgment, but the warmth of the ending is our reading.
- **"Walking in the garden":** Christians differ on how literally to take it. The script quotes it and doesn't explain how God was present.
- **A pastor should read it** before it's locked. No outside reader has seen it.
