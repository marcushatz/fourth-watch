# Plan for script v11

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** God doesn't need us. He made us because he wanted us, and he still wanted us when we hid from him.

**The rule for this ending (Round 61):** nothing new comes after "Where are you?" No new verse, fact or idea. Everything after it comes from that moment or from what the script has already said.

## What changed from v10

| v10 | v11 |
|---|---|
| Hebrews 4:13 came after the moment | It now comes when they hide, which sets up "He already knew" |
| John 3:16 came after the moment | It now closes section 6, before the moment |
| Revelation 4:11 came after the moment | It now sits in section 4, before the story |
| Hebrews 4:16 came after the moment | Cut |
| "He wasn't asking for information. He was calling them out of hiding." | "He already knew where they were. He didn't need an answer. He wanted them." |
| "Not because he needed you, but because he wanted you." | "He didn't make us because he needed us. He made us because he wanted us." This saves "you" for the last line |

## After the moment

Only one verse card appears after the story: Genesis 3:9, on "Where are you?" Everything after that is picture and narration, with no new cards.

## Readings to know about

- **"Where are you?"** It's also the start of God's questioning, and judgment follows. The script includes the judgment before the ending, but the warmth of the ending is our reading.
- **The final question** could be heard as saying everyone is saved. It doesn't claim that, but a pastor should check it.
- **A pastor should read it** before it's locked. No outside reader has seen it.
