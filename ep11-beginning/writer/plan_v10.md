# Plan for script v10

**Status:** candidate. Not approved, and no storyboard yet.

**Message:** God doesn't need us. He made us because he wanted us, and when we hide, he calls us out of hiding.

**Structure:** it starts from the fix-that-fails blueprint (`.claude/skills/fourth-watch-story/references/blueprints.md`). After "Where are you?" it follows Marcus's order from Round 60: what it means, how it applies, the conclusion, and an encouraging question.

## What changed from v9

| v9 | v10 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "If God asked you that today, what would you say?" | "If he still wanted Adam and Eve after all that, why would he ever stop wanting you?" | Marcus's note. The old question could make someone feel judged. The new one has only one answer, and it's good news |
| Nothing after "Where are you?" | Hebrews 4:13 and "He was calling them out of hiding" | Explains what it means |
| Nothing on our lives | "So when we fail, we don't have to hide from God," with Hebrews 4:16 | Says how it applies to us |
| "Not because he needed you..." came before the moment | It now comes after, as the conclusion | The answer lands last |
| John 3:16 came before the moment | It now leads into how it applies to us | The giving points to our lives |
| "He gave his Son to die for us" | Cut | Length. John 3:16 carries it |
| "That same verse says he's the one who gives us..." | Cut | Length. The verse is still on screen at the start |

## The ending on screen

1. **"Here's what he said, in Genesis 3:9."** Return to the same shot of Adam and Eve among the trees.
2. **"God's first words to them aren't 'How could you?'"** Hold on them, with no card yet.
3. **"They're 'Where are you?'"** The Genesis 3:9 card appears on these words. This is the longest hold in the video.
4. **"What it means" and "How it applies":** the Hebrews and John cards appear one at a time, on their references.
5. **Final question:** warm light as Adam and Eve step out from the trees. This is a symbol of being called out of hiding, and Genesis 3:10 only records Adam answering.
6. **End:** a short logo, within 4.5 seconds of the last word. No follow ask.

## Plain words for verses

| Narration says | Verse says | Why it's fair |
|---|---|---|
| "God doesn't need anything from us" | "nor is he served by human hands, as though he needed anything" | Same meaning. Paul is speaking about temples and idols, but "anything" is general. Psalm 50:12 says the same about sacrifices |
| "They hear God walking in the garden, and they hide" | Genesis 3:8 | Same order and same action |
| "God makes them clothes" | "made for Adam and for his wife garments of skins and clothed them" | Same action |
| "No one is hidden from God" | "no creature is hidden from his sight" | Same meaning |
| "We can come to him 'with confidence'" | "Let us then with confidence draw near to the throne of grace" | Same meaning. In Hebrews this is said to believers |
| "He created everything because he chose to" | "by your will they existed and were created" | "By your will" means by his own choice |

## Readings to know about

- **Hebrews 4:16** is written to believers. The script applies it to "we," which a viewer outside the faith could take more widely.
- **The final question** could be heard as saying everyone is saved. It doesn't claim that, but a pastor should check it.
- **Book count:** Acts, Genesis, Hebrews, John and Revelation. Marcus's rule that claims need named Scripture outranks the older limit of three books. All five stay on one thread.
- **A pastor should read it** before it's locked. No outside reader has seen it.
