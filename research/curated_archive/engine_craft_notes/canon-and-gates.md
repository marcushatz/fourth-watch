## Repair loop
Sort defects into: `FATAL` (Bible falsification, missing footage, inaccessible text, clip corruption, rights), `SERIOUS` (major story confusion, absent climax, glaring acting, unintelligible voice), `POLISH` (minor tempo, atmospheric detail). Fix all FATAL, fix prioritized SERIOUS, and disclose remaining POLISH. No fabricated `9/10` critic scores. No forced minimum number of reviews.
