## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook = a face in the dark plus a name.** Elijah's profile fades up from black (0.25–2.0 s) under "whose name means 'my God is Yahweh'", then a title panel names him (2.5 s). No question; the title of the Short ("The Lesson Elijah Teaches You") promises the lesson.
- **Thesis early, payoff last.** The idea ("God's power to create life out of death", 8.9 s) is stated in the third sentence; the last line ("he never really dies… partnering with the life-giver", 54.5–59.5 s) pays it off over the ascension.
- **Comic-panel insets** (8.1, 31.0, 36.5, 53.3 s): a second framed picture slides into the frame to show a parallel action (prayer + result; bulls + prayers; pouring + altar). Two beats per shot without cutting.
- **One verb, one picture, story-style:** "drenches his bull" → pouring (37.7 s); "fire rains down" → a fireball streaks across (40.3 s); "head down to the Jordan" → two figures walking (49.4 s); "pulled into the sky" → lifted in a beam (54.6 s).
- **Crowds as silhouettes** to show a nation's response (16.6, 42.5 s): raised arms = "turns back to Yahweh".
- **God's action is light:** fire from above (40 s), the beam that lifts Elijah (54.6 s).
- **Ending:** logo 1.5 s, no CTA. Tail (closing line start 54.5 s → end) 5.5 s.
