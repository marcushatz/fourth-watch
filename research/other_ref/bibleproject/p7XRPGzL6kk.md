## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook = the name as an object.** No question and no person in frame 1: the title types in (0.5 s) and the Aramaic name brush-writes (1.5 s), then the letters themselves become the land (4.25 s). The answer to the title question starts in the first word.
- **Graphic morphs instead of cuts.** Letters → coastline (4.25 s), page → scrolls (9.5 s), crown → sunburst → iris (27.2, 56.8 s). Each scene grows out of a shape in the last one.
- **Continuous camera journeys:** the probe reads 4.6–24.8 s and 41.0–60.0 s as single moving segments (zoom −64% / −57%). The camera almost never stops.
- **One recurring symbol carries the argument:** the gold crown appears on "God's reign" (26 s), "Kingdom of God" (27.2 s), "enthroned as King by dying" (42.9 s, behind the cross) and "King of the world" (50.5 s), and closes the video (56.8 s).
- **Scripture is a picture of a scroll**, not a quote card: red text lines with one readable line, "ALL NATIONS BLESSED THROUGH YOU" (19.5 s).
- **Limited palette with one accent** (red sash = Jesus in every shot), so the protagonist is findable in silhouette crowds.
- **Ending:** no CTA, no logo, no comment ask; the last line plays over the crown motif and an iris-out. Tail ≈ 3.2 s.
