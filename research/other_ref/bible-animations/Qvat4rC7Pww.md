## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook = the three objects plus the three people within 2 s**, then a question: "How are any of these offerings going to help raise a baby boy?" (finished 6.6 s). First change at 0.25 s.
- **One silhouette per list item, filled in when named.** Three black silhouettes rise (8.4 s); each later turns into king (22.4 s), priest (32.2 s), saviour (45.75 s). The list structure is visible before it is filled.
- **Scripture as a small reference tag** inside the scene ("1 Kings 10", "Exodus 30:7-10"), not a card.
- **Shape rhyme:** the wrapped body (40 s) and the swaddled baby (42.4 s) share a silhouette for "born to die".
- **Callback** to the hook image at the answer (46.9 s), then the end card.
- *(Judged)* Compared with the same-week hits: more, shorter shots (median 2.7 s vs 3.9–5.1 s), two 0.2 s flash cuts, and the answer is a list of three symbols rather than one reveal. The hook names a **personal reaction** ("I always thought… were really weird") rather than a widely held objection or a "did you know".
