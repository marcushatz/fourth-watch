## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook = a faceless "everyman" plus the claim, then the subject swaps in.** The video opens on a blank-faced generic person (0.0 s) for "people don't believe", who is replaced by Jesus on the word "Jesus" (2.3 s). The bubble "I'M NOT GOD" (4.5 s) states the puzzle as an image. The hook statement is complete by ~5.2 s; the first visual change comes at 1.3 s (gesture) and the first big change at 1.8 s.
- **The hook is a statement of the objection, not a question.** "A big reason why people don't believe in Jesus is because he never specifically claims he is God" (0.1–5.0 s). The question itself ("why doesn't John just say…") is spoken at 17.4–20.6 s, mid-video.
- **Everything enters by sliding or whooshing in with motion blur** (1.8, 2.3, 7.0, 28.75, 29.5, 31.0 s). Most "cuts" are subject swaps on a held background, not camera cuts.
- **One word on screen at a time**, centred on the figure's chest; quotes go in speech bubbles (4.5, 20.0, 32.8 s).
- **Lists build one line per spoken item** (21.1–28.0 s): the seven signs appear as named, then six fade so the last one ("Raising Lazarus") carries the next line.
- **Objects for ideas:** a crown for "is God" (17.0 s), a brain for "intelligent" (32.0 s), a traveller and a donkey rider for "investigation … combining the evidence" (35–40 s).
- **Fast run for a climax:** cross (1.3 s) → angel (1.1 s) → John (2.2 s) at 28.0–32.8 s, the shortest shots in the video, for "he raises himself from the dead".
- **Callback that casts the viewer:** the blank-faced person from frame 1 returns at 44.5 s and is labelled "YOU" on the end card (45.1 s) for "that conclusion is for you to reach as the reader".
- **Ending:** no logo, no comment question. The last line runs straight into a 2.3 s "check out the new video" card. Tail (closing segment start → end) 2.3 s.
