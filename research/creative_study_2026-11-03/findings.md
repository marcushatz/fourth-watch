# Creative study: real audience evidence (Nov 3, 2026)

**Collected in the built-in browser from public YouTube pages.** Read-only. No usernames, video or frames saved. The numbers are in `youtube_engagement_2026-11-03.csv`.

**Limits.** Small samples. Hits and lows were chosen in the Oct 31 study. Zack D. Films videos were days old when read, so their rates aren't comparable. Instagram pages weren't available in the browser, so Lintel's Instagram comments are missing, and its YouTube views are tiny (the Tree reel has 1.6K there against 7.6M on Instagram). "Most replayed" heatmaps show replays, not true retention. Heatmaps existed on some videos only.

## Other measurements

- **Like rate didn't track reach in this set.** The two highest rates (12.3%, 10.7%) were lows. Comment rate leaned the other way: hits 0.12% to 0.42%, lows 0.07% to 0.12%.
- **Heavy tail.** Bible Animations: median 63K across the 38 Shorts listed; best 2.2M, about 35 times the median.
- **Word Shorts stay small.** BibleProject's latest 48 Shorts have a 27K median and a 90K top, including three Hebrew or biblical word Shorts at 52K to 76K. Its story and portrait hits reached 1M and 3M. Age and format differ, so this is a direction, not a test.
- **Plain retelling also stays small.** "How David became king of Israel..." has 16K, on a channel with a 63K median.
- **People-centred titles travel on Bible Animations:** "How all of Jesus Disciples Died" 1.3M; "God Tried to KILL Moses for no reason?" 297K.
