# AI reads of the debut candidates (Nov 3, 2026)

All of these are AI readers playing viewers. They're not audience evidence. They can flag confusion, overclaims and insensitive lines.

## Blind opener comparison (seven openings, three viewers)

Hagar and Abraham worked on all three viewers. Lazarus and Peter worked for believers only. The fourth watch opening was weakest and sounded like a sermon. Lazarus and Moses read as familiar. The Bronze Serpent read as slightly worn.

## Lazarus proof v1

A church viewer who lost a parent called the ending glib: "My dad stayed dead." The non-church viewer lost the thread at the names and found the ending cryptic. The fact-check found five overclaims, including "He loved them, so he stayed".

## Hagar proof v1

Two of three viewers stayed to the end and said they'd follow. The mother walking away was the strongest moment. "He tells her to go back" left out "and submit to her", which the readers called dishonest and dangerous for abuse survivors. v2 quotes the verse in full.

## Hagar fact-check

"First person" overclaimed, since Melchizedek and Abram use titles for God earlier. "Promises a son" was loose. Paul's use of Hagar in Galatians 4 is a likely objection.

## Final blind comparison (Hagar v2, Mary Magdalene, Abraham; four viewers)

Hagar came out as the best first video and the freshest. Mary Magdalene had the best ending, and moved the reader who had lost a parent. Abraham was called overdone, and its Jesus ending felt unearned to two readers. Three of four flagged "Return to your mistress and submit to her", left unexplained, as harmful. "Nobody inventing this story" sounded smug.

## Final fact-check

Mary: John never says "first" and Mark 16:9 sits in the longer ending; only the other disciple stooped to look in; Luke 24:11 is about the women as a group; John gives no reason for choosing her.
Abraham: Golgotha was near the city, not in it; the Moriah identity is disputed; "still to come" is a Christian reading.
Hagar: "Not Jacob" removed; the angel's first spoken word to her is her name.

**Not yet checked:** the two new lines about the hard verse in Hagar proof v3.

## Fourth watch round (three stories, three more reads)

The human script held more readers, and the mystery script had the better opening and ending. Readers without Bible knowledge left at Job. The combined script and its human-led rewrite ranked last against Hagar and Mary in both final comparisons, though one Bible-literate reader ranked the first of them highest.
Final scores (AI panel, out of 35): Hagar 31, Mary 29, fourth watch 19. The panel still chose Mary as the best first video, because of the risk in Hagar's "submit" line.

## Mary script cold read and fact-check

Cold read: strongest line was the voice line, weakest was "So why her? John never says." The idle-tale line and "go to my brothers" dragged after "Mary."
Fact-check: "She stayed" skipped that she left and came back, so it's now "She didn't." "One word" and "knew his voice" overclaimed, so both were reworded. "First" remains the one word a careful reader may challenge.
v3 has not had a further cold read.
