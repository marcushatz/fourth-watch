# AI reads of the debut candidates (Nov 3, 2026)

All of these are AI readers playing viewers. They're not audience evidence. They can flag confusion, overclaims and insensitive lines.

## Blind opener comparison (seven openings, three viewers)

Hagar and Abraham worked on all three viewers. Lazarus and Peter worked for believers only. The fourth watch opening was weakest and sounded like a sermon. Lazarus and Moses read as familiar. The Bronze Serpent read as slightly worn.

## Lazarus proof v1

A church viewer who lost a parent called the ending glib: "My dad stayed dead." The non-church viewer lost the thread at the names and found the ending cryptic. The fact-check found five overclaims, including "He loved them, so he stayed".

## Hagar proof v1

Two of three viewers stayed to the end and said they'd follow. The mother walking away was the strongest moment. "He tells her to go back" left out "and submit to her", which the readers called dishonest and dangerous for abuse survivors. v2 quotes the verse in full.

## Hagar fact-check

"First person" overclaimed, since Melchizedek and Abram use titles for God earlier. "Promises a son" was loose. Paul's use of Hagar in Galatians 4 is a likely objection.
