## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook = the subject made into one image.** The one man who "never heard" is a single lit figure; the pull-back (0–3.4 s, −64%) leaves him tiny in the dark. The full question is on screen from frame 1 and spoken by 3.0 s.
- **The viewer's own objections, shown as comments.** Three comment bubbles pop up one at a time (10.9 s) on "people ask this in our comments all the time". The same mock-comment device returns as the CTA (129.5 s).
- **A Bible story told as a chain of verbs, one action per beat.** In the Cornelius run (15.8–38.4 s), each clause gets its own action. Named and labelled: 15.8 s. Gives to the poor: 22.2 s. Kneels to pray: 27.0 s. Angel appears: ~32 s. Messengers leave: ~34 s. Peter arrives and Cornelius bows: 35.5 s. All of it is on one set, using soft cuts and time jumps.
- **Numbered answers with a counter.** A red numeral pops at the start of each answer (44.9, 53.2, 74.8 s). A bottom pill names the point and stays for the whole section, so viewers know where they are in a 137 s video.
- **Light as the running metaphor.** Lamp in the dark (0 s), "little light he had" + beam to his door (44.9 s), sun over the village for "no one has zero light" (53.2 s), beam on Abraham (84.9 s), beam on the cross (112 s), the messenger's lamp arriving (122.5 s).
- **Abstract ideas as objects.** "Judge fairly" is a balance scale settling level (74.8 s). "10 righteous" is ten glowing dots over the city (92.3 s). "Christians disagree" is two labelled groups either side of the cross, then one light that covers both (97.9 s).
- **Evidence beats.** 6 verse cards, each followed (or preceded) by a picture shot. Card hold times: 5.9, 3.8, 3.6, 4.5, 2.9, 2.0 s (mean 3.8 s). Cards fill 22.7 s (17%).
- **Bookend callback.** The village house from the hook comes back at the first answer (44.9 s) and at the closing turn (122.5 s), where a messenger finally brings a lamp to the door. The story's problem is visibly solved in the same place it was posed.
- **Ending.** It turns the question back onto the viewer at 115.8 s, 21.7 s before the end, then gives a concrete, personal CTA ("type their name in the comments") with a typed example "My grandmother" (129.5–134.5 s), then a 2.7 s logo with tagline. The CTA is not a debate question; it asks for a name.
