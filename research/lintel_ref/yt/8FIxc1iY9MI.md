## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook in 2.9 s, built on a visual mystery.** The wife is drawn as a blank, faceless, pale figure with a giant "?" over her (0–2.9 s). The setup line cuts to the family lineup with name pills (3.1 s). Then it cuts back to the same faceless-woman framing for "who did Cain marry?" (7.4 s, 1.2 s long). That is a question → context → question bookend inside the first 8.6 s.
- **Social proof as a UI insert.** "One of the most asked questions" is shown as a search bar typing the query and showing autocomplete (8.8–13.6 s).
- **Evidence = full-screen verse cards.** 4 cards (17.7, 29.5, 38.3, 61.7 s), each up 3.4–4.4 s (mean 4.05 s). Each card is answered by a picture shot of the claim it proves. Cards fill 15.9 s of 84 s (19%), the largest share in the hits.
- **"Appears" acted literally.** At 22.2 s the wife pops into the frame beside Cain on "She just appears".
- **Counter-by-crowd.** "Weren't the only children" is shown by the village filling up with more and more people (34.3 s).
- **Labels and rings make the logic explicit.** CAIN / A SISTER pills with red rings under both (41.9 s). ABRAHAM / SARAH · GEN 20:12 pills (50.3 s).
- **A timeline for "much later".** A three-dot ADAM → ABRAHAM → MOSES line draws with a "LAW AGAINST CLOSE MARRIAGE" box (46.1 s).
- **Refuting an alternative.** The camera pans to an empty green area labelled "OTHER PEOPLE?" and a red X stamps on it as the voice says "never" (56.5–61.5 s).
- **"One blood" made physical.** A line of different-looking people is joined by one red thread. The same continuous camera move then carries on to the garden and then to the cross (66.2–75.4 s), so the argument's three steps (one family, fell, saved) are one unbroken journey.
- **Ending.** There is no question and no comment ask. It closes on a "you'll know what to say" line over the family by the fire (75.6–80.5 s, 4.9 s), then the logo crossfades in with the tagline "Covered, every day." (80.7–84.1 s, 3.3 s).
