## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook is a mood shot, not an action.** A man slumped on the floor in a dark room for 8.9 s while the question is asked twice. Nothing changes during the hook.
- **Modern ↔ Bible cross-cutting.** Adam and Eve hide (8.9 s), then "we do the same" brings back the modern man (15.9 s). Short list shots follow: "skip church" (24.3 s, 1.7 s) and "avoid your Bible" (26.0 s, 2.3 s). These are the video's only fast beats.
- **Light as God's approach.** The window glows (28.3 s), light sweeps toward the tree (73.2 s), and the window brightens at the end (141.5–147.7 s).
- **Leaves as the shame object.** Leaves stick to a man and fall off on "you can't fix it with fig leaves" (96.0 s).
- **Covering → cross link drawn as a line.** A dotted connector runs from the skin coat to the cross, labelled THE FIRST / THE LAST (126.5 s).
- **Five full-screen verse cards** (37.3, 70.4, 89.1, 109.7, 132.0 s), up 8.9, 2.8, 6.2, 7.1, 5.0 s (mean 6.0 s, the longest holds in the set). Cards fill 30 s of 164 s (18%).
- **Bookend.** It opens in the dark room and closes in the same room, now lit with the man standing (147.7 s). Adam and Eve step out from the trees in the last picture shot (154.6 s).
- **Ending.** There is no question and no comment CTA. The last line is an exhortation ("Stop sewing fig leaves. Come out from the trees.") over 6.0 s, then a 3.2 s logo with tagline.
- **Pacing contrast with the hits.** Median shot 6.2 s vs 3.65–4.8 s, 1.9 words/s vs 2.7–3.3, one camera move in 164 s, and side-on flat staging with almost no character action.
