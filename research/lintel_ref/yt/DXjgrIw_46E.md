## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **One locked set that changes**: the same isometric block in every shot. Each "Let there be…" is shown as a visible addition to it: light (9.8 s), water (12.0 s), land and trees (17.0 s), sun and moon (21.6 s), birds and fish (25.8 s), a man in a light beam (33.8 s), golden rest (38.4 s). The picture is the cumulative result.
- **Counter**: big "DAY N" title plus a 7-dot progress row top-right. Every day change is a flash or cut with the number updated.
- **Night/day pair for "two great lights"** (21.6 s day → 23.8 s night).
- **Hook**: a title card "THE FIRST 7 DAYS" over a dark forming block (0–4.8 s). It asks no question and has no human subject.
- **Ending**: a 9.1 s end card (17% of the video) with a series line, a "NEXT: GENESIS 2 · THE GARDEN" tease and "FOLLOW". It has no comment question.
- **Contrast with the hits (inference)**: no person to follow, no tension or question, KJV-only narration, and a long end card. Visually it is clean and coherent, but it is one of the two lowest performers.
