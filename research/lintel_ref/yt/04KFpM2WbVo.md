## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Title-card hook in two halves**: "WHAT IF THEY NEVER ATE?" over a calm Adam and Eve (0–4.1 s), then "OUR WORLD TODAY?" over a family standing outside a house (4.1 s). Nobody acts in either shot.
- **Hypothetical shown as a scene**: a multi-generation dinner table "great-grandparents would still sit at your table" (14.3 s), with GRIEF struck out at the end.
- **Before/after pairs for "work"**: Adam watering in the lush garden (24.6 s) → man with a hoe among thorns (29.5 s) → a woman tending plants with love (34.1 s). Three shots, one per clause.
- **Abstract as particle effects**: "blame… fingerprints" as red rings spreading from a pointing hand (47.2 s). "Distance" as a dotted line from a praying man to the moon (63.1 s). "Memory of Eden" as floating lights (81.3 s).
- **Symbolic empty object**: an empty garden bench for "the greatest loss" (54.2 s).
- **Quote cards:** 3 (9.9, 43.5, 71.7 s), up 3.8, 3.2, 9.2 s. The C.S. Lewis card at 9.2 s is the longest single hold in the video.
- **Ending**: question card with 4 answer chips that callback the video's own points (DEATH, GRIEF, BLAME, THE DISTANCE) for 5.9 s, then "Welcome to The Valiant" crest 4.0 s, then logo 2.0 s. The CTA run is 12.5 s (13%).
- **Low physical action overall**: the probe's own-motion is under 3% in 22 of 23 shots, the stillest of the 3D-set videos. Actions exist (watering, hoeing, pointing) but are small gestures, not events.
