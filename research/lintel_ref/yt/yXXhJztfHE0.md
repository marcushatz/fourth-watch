## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Static, title-led hook.** One 6.0 s shot of a tiny person under a huge night sky. The question is carried by big centred titles in two parts (1 s and 3 s). There is no action and no cut in the first 6 s, the slowest opening in the set.
- **Each claim is followed by a historical case.** The sea splitting (18.5 s) and Sinai thunder with lightning flashes (21.5–23.9 s) set up the golden-calf dance (23.9 s). That is a three-beat "they saw → they still turned" sequence, labelled "SEEING IS NOT LOVING".
- **Parable acted in three shots.** The king and maiden meet (40.8 s), royal splendour makes her kneel in fear (45.3 s), then the king as a servant and a heart pops (48.7 s). The same couple appears in all three, with one label pill per shot.
- **Metaphor objects.** Closed curtains with a line of light for "hold back" (38.3 s). Light and shadow on a candle for Pascal (66.8 s). A path lit only one step ahead for "reveals enough… leaves love free" (76.6 s).
- **Quote cards:** 4 cards (31.3, 58.5, 69.2, 84.4 s), up 5.8, 2.7, 7.0, 3.6 s (mean 4.8 s). One quotes a non-Bible author (Pascal).
- **Ending is long and has two CTAs.** Either/or buttons + mock comment for 7.2 s, a community "Welcome to the Valiant" crest card for 4.9 s, then the logo for 2.0 s. The CTA run is 14.7 s (14% of the video); the hits use 5–7 s.
