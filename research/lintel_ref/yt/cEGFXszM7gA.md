## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook as a two-shot either/or.** "Bad person" over Judas alone in the dark (0.3 s). A flash cuts to "hero who made salvation possible" over the lit cross (3.0 s). Each side of the question gets its own picture and its own light (night vs beam).
- **A news-style cold open for the controversy.** Papyrus + "2006" stamp (6.1 s), clippings piling up (9.7 s), modern scholars at desks (12.1 s). That is 3 shots in 8.5 s, each about 2.5 s.
- **Metaphor turned into a set.** "Playing a part he was given" moves to a stage with red curtains and a spotlit actor holding a script (29.4 s).
- **Counters and tallies.** The coin counter ticks 21 → 30 on "30 pieces of silver" (55.0 s). Three red tally marks appear on "denied Jesus three times" (82.1 s).
- **Modern analogy shot in a modern set.** An office where a colleague lies, a fired man, a new lit door, and a "STILL WRONG" stamp (69.8–82.1 s, 4 shots in 12.3 s).
- **Parallel lives cut side by side.** Peter weeps (86.0 s) and comes back to Jesus on the shore (88.6 s). Judas walks away alone (90.3 s). The contrast is carried by direction of walking: toward the light vs away.
- **Labels for the two options.** "A BAD PERSON?" / "INSTRUMENT?" sit either side of a lone Judas in a grey void (95.2 s). The "own choice" fork path (99.3 s) is the same path device as "The tree".
- **Hypothetical staged on the earlier set.** The closing question re-uses Peter's shore and fire, with Judas in Peter's place (106.0 s). It is a callback that asks the viewer to imagine the alternative.
- **Pacing.** This is the fastest-cut video of the four most-viewed: 52% of shots are 3 s or shorter, with many flash transitions (10).
- **Ending.** The answer comes over the crowd at the cross (102.0 s). The what-if question shot lasts 5.1 s, then a 1.8 s comment card with "Yes, because…" typing, then a 2.0 s logo. The CTA starts 9.1 s before the end, counting the question shot.
