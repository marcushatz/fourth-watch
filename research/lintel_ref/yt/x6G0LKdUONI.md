## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook: three quick "another…" shots.** Globe pin (0 s, 2.6 s), another culture (2.8 s, 1.7 s), another religion (4.5 s, 1.8 s), then the question as a big title over a woman in light (6.3 s). That is a one-shot-per-phrase list in 8.6 s.
- **Metaphor props.** Dice for "accident of birth" (8.8 s). Belief cards dropping away until "FAITH" is kept (16.7 s). A lamp handed from an older woman to a young man who walks his own path for "inheritance → choice" (27.3 s).
- **Logic as diagrams.** The genetic-fallacy flow with a red X (33.3 s). A curved arrow with "IT CUTS BOTH WAYS" (49.1 s). These are the most diagram-heavy shots in the set.
- **Authorities as pill labels on a scene** ("ALVIN PLANTINGA · PHILOSOPHER", 42.4 s; "RUTH · A MOABITE", 62.3 s; "PAUL IN ATHENS · ACTS 17", 69.4 s).
- **Quote cards:** 2 (65.4, 71.8 s), up 3.7 and 6.5 s.
- **Big-title beats** at 6.3, 8.8, 49.1, 54.6 and 82.4 s break the video into sections.
- **Ending**: either/or buttons (3.0 s), then a Valiant crest with "WHEN DID IT BECOME YOURS?" → "WELCOME TO THE VALIANT" (5.5 s), then the logo (2.0 s). The CTA run is 11.0 s.
