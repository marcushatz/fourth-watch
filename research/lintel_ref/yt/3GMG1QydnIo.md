## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Direct-address hook with a command**: "Are you looking for a job? Don't scroll past this." The first shot shows the viewer's situation (a jobseeker at night) for 5.4 s with no cut.
- **Literal props for each worry**: envelopes flying away for "every job I applied to" (5.4 s), a man holding his phone waiting for "calls that never followed" (11.4 s).
- **A shot per item in the list**: "every application / every interview / every door" are 1.5, 1.3 and 1.3 s shots (29.3–33.4 s). Each one returns to a scene already set up, so it is a recap montage.
- **"Open the door" acted literally**, with the only strong camera move in the video (push through the opening door, 33.4 s).
- **Time-of-day change as the answer**: night → dawn in the same framing on "You are still working" (41.6 s) and on "I receive it today" (50.3 s).
- **Verse cards on a golden glow** (20.3 s for 9.0 s with an in-card gesture; 44.3 s for 6.0 s).
- **Ending**: "AMEN." beat (0.8 s), then a type-amen card (4.2 s), then the logo with a follow line for 7.2 s. That is 12.2 s after the prayer ends, with two asks (comment + follow).
