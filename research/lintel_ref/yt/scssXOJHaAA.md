## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook = the action named in the question, then the reveal.** At 0–2 s Eve reaches for the fruit while the voice says "If God knew Eve would eat the fruit". At 2.1–3.3 s the camera pulls back to the whole garden as the voice reaches "why didn't He remove the tree". It is one 8.5 s shot with no cut. The caption builds word by word and the question is fully asked by ~5.5 s. A light beam at 7 s stands for "He knew".
- **One shot per item in a list.** "Every war" (8.5 s, 1.7 s long) and "every sickness" (10.2 s, 1.5 s) each get their own scene and a black label pill naming the item. These are the two shortest shots in the video.
- **"If" lines are acted out literally.** "If God had taken the tree away" shows a beam taking the tree away (16.5–18.5 s), and Eve is left standing in the empty spot.
- **Abstract argument turned into objects and actions** (the core of this video):
  - "sin would still be waiting at the very next command": a snake coiled at the next stone (20.1 s)
  - "take away everything you could be tempted to take": the stall goods vanish as a man walks by (26.0 s)
  - "hide every good thing your neighbour owns": the neighbour's animals vanish (32.1 s)
  - "clear away every way to break it": a village empties object by object (35.5 s)
  - "people who obey because they can't do anything else": people turn into marionettes on strings (45.3 s)
- **Two-beat rhythm for parallel lines.** Commandment tablet (2.5 s / 2.1 s), then consequence scene (4.0 s / 3.4 s), done twice (23.5–35.5 s). The tablet framing is identical both times, and the words carve in as they are spoken.
- **The verse card sits on a live scene and the next line pays it off.** The Deut 30:19 card covers the top half for 5.8 s over a man walking to a fork (52.4 s). Then the card fades, a LIFE signpost appears, and he takes that path (58.2 s), so "choose life" becomes a literal choice of road.
- **Callbacks and a match-dissolve.** The opening garden wide comes back at 63.3 s and 73.6 s, the second time with the title question repeated over it. "Sin came through a tree" (67.0 s) dissolves straight into "a second tree, the cross" (69.8 s).
- **Foreshadow inside a callback.** A tiny cross appears in the garden sky at 63.3 s on "ready … before we ever made it".
- **The answer gets a group action.** The resolution line is a lit doorway that one man and then a crowd walk into (76.2 s), not a single figure standing still.
- **Ending timing.** The answer line ends at 82.0 s. A 0.2 s dip to dark leads into an either/or question card with two icons that appear on their words (gift/burden), plus a mock comment that types "A gift, because…" (82.3–87.4 s, 5.1 s). The logo build follows (87.4–89.4 s, 2.0 s). The CTA starts 7.1 s before the end.
