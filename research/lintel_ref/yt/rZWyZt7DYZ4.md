## Techniques (with time stamps)

- **Hook as a contrast pair with big title text.** Family in warm light under "IF GOD IS LOVE…" (0.3 s, 2.2 s), then a flash to a fire pit at night under "WHY IS THERE A HELL?" (2.7 s, 1.5 s). The question is finished by 4.2 s. This is the only video in the set that puts the hook in big centred titles rather than the usual caption line.
- **Numbered chapter cards.** These are full-screen dark cards (12.0 s, ~26.3 s, 67.5 s), unlike the bottom counter pill in "Never heard". The first card stays up 3.2 s with no picture, then they shrink to under 1 s.
- **Abstract claim → separation or tug-of-war.** "Not made for you" is shown as people kept in a light beam apart from the fire (20.7 s). "The enemy wants you lost and Jesus came to do the opposite" is a literal tug of war (38.9 s).
- **Verse acted inside the card's scene.** The sheepfold + thief under the John 10:10 card, with Jesus appearing on "I am come" (31.4 s). Jesus knocking at a closed door under the Rev 3:20 card (50.4 s), then a closer shot of him waiting (57.1 s).
- **Stamps.** "PAID" on the cross (68.2 s).
- **One recurring object carries the ending.** The door appears at 50.4 s (knocking), 57.1 s (never forced), 83.4 s (light path out of the door), and 88.2 s (the end card is a closed door).
- **Callback to the hook title** at 77.4 s, where the same "WHY IS THERE A HELL?" text and fire return to frame the answer.
- **Quote cards:** 5 cards (16.7, 31.4, 50.4, 63.4, 70.8 s), up 3.5, 6.9, 6.7, 3.6, 2.6 s (mean 4.7 s). All sit at the top over a live scene, not full-screen.
- **Ending.** The answer lands at 83.4 s, then a 7.0 s end card with question + mock comment ("I think it's…"), then a 2.0 s logo. The CTA starts 9.0 s before the end.
