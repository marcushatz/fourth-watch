## "What happens to people who never heard of Jesus?" (1.6M), 137 s, full VO as pasted by Marcus (Oct 29)
What happens to people who never heard of Jesus? If Jesus is the only way to the Father, what about a man born in a remote village who died without ever hearing his name? People ask this in our comments all the time so let's allow the Bible to answer it.
In Cesarea there lived a Roman officer named Cornelius, a Gentile who had never heard the gospel preached. Yet the Bible says he feared God with all his house, gave much to the poor, and prayed to God continually. One afternoon an angel told him to send for a man called Peter, who was lodging by the sea in Japa. When Peter walked into that house, he said, "God is no respecter of persons but in every nation he that feareth him is accepted with him."
1. God saw a man reaching for him with the little light he had and he sent the gospel straight to his door.
2. No one is born without any light at all. Paul writes that God's eternal power is clearly seen in creation and that his law is written on the hearts of people who never had the scriptures. When Paul walked through Athens he found an altar carved with the words "to the unknown God," built by people reaching for a God they couldn't name. God will judge every person fairly by what they were given. Jesus said, "Unto whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required." Centuries earlier Abraham pleaded with God for the city of Sodom and asked, "Shall not the judge of all the earth do right?" God agreed to spare the whole city if even ten righteous people could be found inside it. Christians still disagree on exactly how God deals with those who never heard. Some believe a person must hear of Jesus and trust him in this life while others believe Christ can save people who never learn his name but every one of those views agrees that no one is saved except through Jesus.
3. Paul asks a question that puts the weight back on our shoulders: "How shall they hear without a preacher?" The question isn't only what happens to people who never heard of Jesus, it's also who is going to tell them. Think of the person who first told you about Jesus and say thank you to them.

## "If God is love, why is there a hell?" (174K at 2 days), ~97 s, full VO as pasted by Marcus (Oct 29)
If God is love why is there a hell and why would a loving God send anyone there forever? Some Christians also wonder about this question sometimes.
1. Ask who hell was made for. Jesus called it "everlasting fire prepared for the devil and his angels." It was never made for you or for me. Why does anyone end up there?
2. Look at who is working to take people there. Jesus said, "The thief cometh not but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy. I am come that they might have life." The enemy wants you lost and Jesus came to do the opposite. Does God force anyone either way? Look at how the Bible ends. In Revelation Jesus says, "Behold I stand at the door and knock. If any man hear my voice and open the door, I will come in." He knocks but he never kicks the door in. The Bible's final invitation reads, "Whosoever will, let him take the water of life freely."
3. The price has already been paid. God is not willing that any should perish so Jesus carried the judgment for us on the cross. Why is there a hell then? Because evil will finally be judged, and the devil with. But God has opened a way out for every one of us and He is still knocking.
Tell us, if Jesus is knocking, what keeps people from opening the door? Let us know in the comments.
