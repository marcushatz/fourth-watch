# Jesus knew Judas would betray Him. So why did He keep him close?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 115
answer: He gave the reason at that table, "the Scripture will be fulfilled," and He walked into it with His eyes open, treating Judas like the rest so evenly that when He said "one of you," they couldn't tell who it was. Love isn't blind: Jesus saw everything, and loved anyway. So when someone close turns on you: tell the truth plainly, let them go if they choose to go, and don't let it make you cold.

## Hook
VO: Jesus knew Judas would betray Him. So why did He keep him close?
ON-SCREEN: JESUS KNEW JUDAS WOULD BETRAY HIM. SO WHY DID HE KEEP HIM CLOSE?

## Stakes
VO: John says Jesus knew "from the beginning... who it was who would betray him." [John 6:64]
VO: Most of us would cut that person off. Jesus did something else.

## Story
VO: He knew, and He still sent him out with the twelve to heal the sick and cast out demons. [Matthew 10:1-8]
VO: He knew, and He still let him keep the moneybag, though John says "he was a thief." [John 12:6]
VO: He knew, and He still washed his feet at the last supper. [John 13:2-5, 13:11]
VO: Then He said it out loud: "One of you will betray me." And here's what most people miss: the disciples "looked at one another." One after another, they asked, "Is it I, Lord?" [John 13:21-22; Matthew 26:21-22]
VO: Even when Jesus told Judas, "What you are going to do, do quickly," "no one at the table knew why he said this to him." [John 13:27-28]
VO: That night, Judas came with a kiss. Jesus named it: "Judas, would you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?" And He still called him "Friend." [Luke 22:47-48; Matthew 26:49-50]
VERSE: "Friend, do what you came to do." — Matthew 26:50 (ESV)

## Turn
VO: So why did Jesus keep Judas close? At that table He gave the reason: "the Scripture will be fulfilled." [John 13:18; John 17:12]
VO: But here's the part that hits: after about three years together, when He said "one of you," they couldn't tell who it was. [John 13:21-22]
VO: He treated Judas like the rest, right up to "Friend." [John 13:22, 13:28; Matthew 26:50]
VO: He had taught, "Love your enemies." He lived it. [Matthew 5:44]

## Landing
VO: Love isn't blind. Jesus saw everything, and loved anyway. [John 6:64; Matthew 26:50]
VO: So if someone close turns on you, do what He did. [Luke 22:48; John 13:27-30; Matthew 26:50]
VO: Tell the truth about it, plainly. Let them go, if they choose to go. And don't let it make you cold. [Luke 22:48; John 13:27-30; Matthew 26:50]
VO: Jesus knows exactly how that feels. [John 13:18; Hebrews 4:15]
VO: Which hits you harder: that Jesus washed his feet knowing what was coming, or that He still called him "Friend"? Tell us in the comments. [John 13:5, 13:11; Matthew 26:50]

## Value
- Jesus knew from the beginning who would betray Him [John 6:64]
- Judas was one of the twelve sent out to heal the sick and cast out demons [Matthew 10:1-8]
- Judas kept the moneybag and stole from it [John 12:6]
- At the Last Supper the disciples couldn't tell who it was: they "looked at one another," asked "Is it I, Lord?", and "no one at the table knew why" Jesus told Judas to go [John 13:22, 13:28-29; Matthew 26:22]
- Jesus gave the reason at that same table, quoting Psalm 41:9: "the Scripture will be fulfilled" [John 13:18; John 17:12]
- At the betrayal, Jesus called him "Friend" [Matthew 26:50]

## Caption
Jesus knew. He kept him close anyway. Read it yourself: John 6:64; 12:6; 13:1-31; 17:12; Matthew 26:20-50; Luke 22:48.
