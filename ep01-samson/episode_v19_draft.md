# The scariest thing in Samson's story isn't Delilah
blueprint: HOOK / STORY / TURN / LESSON / APPLY
answer: His strength working didn't mean he was living the way God wanted. A gift from God is not a verdict from God.

## Hook
VO: The scariest thing in Samson's story isn't Delilah. It's that his strength kept working, right up until it didn't. [Judges 16:3, 16:20]
VISUAL: Delilah's silhouette in a lit doorway fades; Samson steps forward; the gold strength mark lights, then flickers out on "didn't".

## Story
VO: "The Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him" three times, more than on any other judge. A lion. Thirty men. A thousand. [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
VISUAL: Montage, about 2 s per beat: the lion; thirty Philistine figures topple at Ashkelon (no gore); the jawbone raised at Lehi. Gold rushes in each time.
VO: The first choice we see him make? A wife, because "she is right in my eyes." [Judges 14:2-3]
VISUAL: Timnah: Samson points at a Philistine woman; his parents shake their heads. Card: RIGHT IN MY EYES · JUDGES 14:3.
VO: And even in Gaza, his strength kept working. He went in to a prostitute, and that same night he pulled up the city gates, posts and all, and carried them up a hill. [Judges 16:1-3]
VISUAL: Midnight in Gaza: a lit doorway (nothing inside shown), then Samson carrying the gate doors and posts up the hill; the gold mark holds.
VO: Four times Delilah said the exact same words: "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" Three times, he broke free. [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
VISUAL: The line stamps on screen; bowstrings, ropes, the loom pin; the gold mark flares each time.
VO: Then "he told her all his heart," and his hair, the sign he was set apart to God, was shaved. [Judges 13:5, 16:17, 16:19; cf. Numbers 6:7]
VISUAL: He leans in to tell her; the seven locks fall; the gold mark goes dark.

## Turn
VO: He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: He rises easy, unaware, as soldiers fill the doorway. Locked hold on the verse card.

## Lesson
VO: His strength working didn't mean he was living the way God wanted. A gift from God is not a verdict from God.
VISUAL: Back to the opening frame; the dark gold mark.

## Apply
VO: So when your gift is working, don't just ask, "Is it working?" Ask, "Is this right in God's eyes, or just in mine?" [Judges 14:3; Deuteronomy 12:25; cf. Judges 13:1]
VISUAL: Same frame as the first shot; the line as the end card.
