Eyes thread: 14:1 saw; 14:3 & 14:7 "right in my/Samson's eyes"; 16:1 saw a prostitute; 16:21 eyes gouged; 16:27 3,000 "looked on"; 16:28 "for my two eyes"; 17:6/21:25 "Everyone did what was right in his own eyes" (same Hebrew yashar + 'ayin). Mishnah Sotah 1:8: "Samson followed his eyes… Philistines gouged out his eyes." SOLID 5
2. Nobody cried out: 3:9, 3:15, 4:3, 6:6, 10:10 "cried out to the LORD"; 13:1 none; 40 yrs = longest oppression (others 8,18,20,7,18); cry returns 1 Sam 7:8, 9:16 ("their cry has come to me"). SOLID 5

4. "He did not know that the LORD had left him" (16:20), ESV cross-ref 1 Sam 16:14 (Spirit departed from Saul). Hair grew back (16:22), last feat by prayer (16:28). SOLID
5. Judah binds its deliverer: 15:11–13 "Do you not know that the Philistines are rulers over us?" (Judah told to "go up first" 1:2). SOLID 4
6. Spirit on Samson 4x (13:25; 14:6; 14:19; 15:14), most of any judge; "rushed upon" (tsalach) of only Samson, Saul (1 Sam 10:6; 11:6), David (16:13); also 18:10 harmful spirit rushed on Saul. SOLID 4
7. "Begin to save" (13:5) → 1 Sam 7:8, 9:16 → 2 Sam 3:18; 8:1 David subdued them. SOLID 4
8. Burned anyway: 14:15 "lest we burn you" → 15:6 "burned her and her father with fire". SOLID 4
