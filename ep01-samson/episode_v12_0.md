# Why did Samson tell Delilah his secret, after she tried to hand him over three times?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 70
answer: He was worn down, and every rule he had broken before cost him nothing, so he expected this one wouldn't either. Nothing happens, until it does.

## Landing

VO: That's the danger of compromise. Nothing happens. Until it does. [Judges 16:20–21]

## Value
- Delilah asked him openly how he "might be bound, that one could subdue you," and Philistines were hiding in the room each time [Judges 16:6, 16:9, 16:12]
- He told her because she wore him down: "she pressed him hard with her words day after day... his soul was vexed to death" [Judges 16:16]
- A Nazirite couldn't go near a dead body, yet Samson scraped honey from a dead lion and stripped thirty dead men, and his strength never left [Numbers 6:6; Judges 14:8–9, 14:19]
- He woke expecting to "go out as at other times," "but he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- What we learn: compromise that costs nothing teaches you to expect it never will; nothing happens, until it does [Judges 16:20–21]
