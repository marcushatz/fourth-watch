# What's the lesson of Samson's story?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 85
answer: Being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God: Samson's strength never failed, so when the LORD left him, he didn't even notice.

## Turn
VO: Now read that line again: "he did not know." Through the lion, the foxes, the prostitute and the gates, his strength never failed. So when the LORD left, Samson didn't even notice. [Judges 14:6, 15:4–5, 16:1–3, 16:20]
VISUAL: Locked hold on the 16:20 card; behind it, quick ghosts of the lion, the foxes and the gates, each lit gold, then the dark room at Delilah's.

## Landing
VO: So what's the lesson of Samson? Being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God. Samson learned that in a prison. [Judges 16:20–21]
VISUAL: Back to the opening: Samson at the mill in Gaza; the first gold light touches the hair beginning to grow again. End card.

## Value
- The angel said Samson would "begin to save Israel" [Judges 13:5]
- The Spirit of the LORD rushed on him for the lion, the thirty men and the thousand men [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- In Gaza he slept with a prostitute, then pulled up the city gates and carried them up a hill, and his strength did not fail [Judges 16:1–3]
- He woke up expecting to "go out as at other times," but "he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- What we learn: being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God [Judges 16:20–21]
