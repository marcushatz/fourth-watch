# If God is still using you, does that mean He's okay with how you're living?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 125
answer: A working gift doesn't mean God approves of how you're living, but when you're at your lowest, you can still call on God.

## Hook
VO: If God is still using you, does that mean He's okay with how you're living?
ON-SCREEN: IF GOD STILL USES YOU, IS HE OKAY WITH HOW YOU LIVE?

## Stakes
VO: Let's look at Samson, known as the strongest man in the Bible. He slept with a prostitute in Gaza, and that same night he pulled the city gates out of the ground. [Judges 16:1-3]

## Story
VO: But that strength came from God. Three times in the book of Judges, it is recorded that "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him." [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14, 16:20]
VO: Later, Samson fell in love with a woman named Delilah, and the leaders of the Philistines, Israel's enemies, paid her to find the secret of his strength. [Judges 16:4-5, 16:18]
VO: Four times, she called out the exact same words: "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
VO: Three times she tried to trap him, and three times he broke free. [Judges 16:8-9, 16:12, 16:13-14]
VO: But when Samson finally told her his secret, she had his uncut hair shaved, the sign he was set apart to God. [Judges 13:5, 16:6, 16:17-19; Numbers 6:5-7]
VO: Then she called a fourth time. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." [Judges 16:20]
VO: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)

## Turn
VO: So what does this mean for us? Three things. [Judges 13:5, 16:28]
VO: One: God gives gifts to be used for His glory and to help other people, not because we earned them. [Judges 13:5; 1 Peter 4:10-11]
VO: Before Samson was born, the angel of the LORD told his mother he would "begin to save Israel." God used his strength to rescue his people. [Judges 13:3-5, 15:14-18]
VO: Two: a working gift doesn't mean God approves of how you're living. [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20]
VO: Samson's strength worked the same night he slept with a prostitute in Gaza. [Judges 16:1-3]
VO: Jesus also warned that mighty works aren't proof of a right relationship with Him. On judgment day, many will say to Him, "Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name... and do many mighty works in your name?" [Matthew 7:22]
VO: And He will declare to them, "I never knew you; depart from me, you workers of lawlessness." [Matthew 7:23]

## Landing
VO: Three: when you're at your lowest, you can still call on God. [Judges 16:21-30]
VO: The Philistines blinded him and put him in prison. [Judges 16:21]
VO: Later on, at a celebration for their god Dagon, he prayed, "O Lord GOD, please remember me and please strengthen me only this once..." [Judges 16:22-28]
VO: Then he pushed against the two middle pillars with all his strength, and the building fell on the Philistine rulers and everyone inside. Samson died with them. [Judges 16:29-30]
VO: Yet centuries later, Hebrews 11 still names Samson among its examples of faith. [Hebrews 11:32-34]
VO: Samson's failures weren't the last word on his life, and yours don't have to be either. [Judges 16:28-30; Hebrews 11:32-34]

## Value
- His strength came from the LORD; the hair was the sign of his consecration [Judges 14:6, 16:17, 16:20, 16:28]
- Three times "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him"; four times Delilah said the exact same words, and the fourth time the LORD had left him [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14; 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
- Gifts are for God's glory and for others: before he was born, Samson would "begin to save Israel"; Peter says gifts are to "serve one another" so that "in everything God may be glorified" [Judges 13:5; 1 Peter 4:10-11]
- His strength worked the same night he slept with a prostitute in Gaza, and when it was gone he "did not know" [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20]
- At his lowest, blind and a prisoner, he called on God, and God answered [Judges 16:21, 16:28-30]
- Hebrews 11 names Samson among those who "through faith" "were made strong out of weakness" [Hebrews 11:32-34]
