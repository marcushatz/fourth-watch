# Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 75
answer: God raised Samson to begin the rescue, and read beside the Gospels, every place his story breaks shows the Savior he couldn't be. Samson could only begin. Jesus said, "It is finished."

## Hook
VO: Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
ON-SCREEN: WHY IS SAMSON IN THE BIBLE?
VISUAL: High three-quarter wide of the ridge at Lehi at dawn: Samson standing alone with a jawbone, long braids, Philistine camp soft in the distance; a slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: He chased a Philistine wife. He told Delilah his secret after she'd tried to hand him over three times. [Judges 14:1–3, 16:4–17]
VISUAL: Two fast cuts: Samson pointing toward a Philistine woman in the vineyards of Timnah; Delilah's lamp-lit room with a 1-2-3 counter flicking past.
VO: Put him next to Jesus, and he matches. Until he doesn't. And the places he doesn't are the point.
VISUAL: The frame splits into two stacked panels joined by a thin gold seam: top panel JUDGES (Samson's world, warm clay and ember), bottom panel THE GOSPELS (Jerusalem, night blue and stone). The two panels mirror each other in composition.

## Story
VO: Samson's own people tied him up to hand him over to the Philistines. The leaders of Jesus' own people "bound him" and handed him over to Pilate. [Judges 15:11–13; Matthew 27:1–2]
VISUAL: Top: men of Judah at the rock of Etam binding Samson with two new ropes. Bottom: the chief priests and elders leading Jesus, hands bound, to Pilate's door. Tags: JUDGES 15:13 · MATTHEW 27:2.
VO: Blinded, Samson was brought out to entertain his enemies. Jesus was blindfolded and mocked: "Prophesy! Who is it that struck you?" [Judges 16:21, 16:25; Luke 22:63–64]
VISUAL: Top: blind Samson led out by a young man holding his hand, between laughing Philistines in the house of Dagon. Bottom: Jesus seated, a cloth over his eyes, guards mocking around him. Neither face shown in detail. Tags: JUDGES 16:25 · LUKE 22:64.

## Turn
VO: Now watch where it breaks. [Judges 16:20; Matthew 26:53]
VISUAL: The gold seam between the panels cracks; the camera holds still.
VO: Samson said, "I will… shake myself free," and couldn't. Jesus could have appealed to his Father for "more than twelve legions of angels," and didn't. [Judges 16:20–21; Matthew 26:53]
VISUAL: Top: Samson straining as the Philistines seize him. Bottom: in the garden, just after Peter's strike: the servant clutching his ear, Jesus still, telling Peter to put his sword back; faint light high above him.
VO: Before Samson was born, the angel said he would "begin to save Israel." Before Jesus was born: "he will save his people from their sins." [Judges 13:5; Matthew 1:21]
VISUAL: Top: ZORAH: the angel of the LORD appearing to Samson's mother, BEGIN lighting gold. Bottom: an angel before Joseph in a dream, WILL SAVE lighting gold. Tags: JUDGES 13:5 · MATTHEW 1:21.
VO: Samson's last prayer was revenge "for my two eyes," and his death killed his enemies. Jesus prayed, "Father, forgive them," and then died for his enemies. Including us. [Judges 16:28–30; Luke 23:34; Romans 5:8–10]
VERSE: "…that I may be avenged on the Philistines for my two eyes." — Judges 16:28 (ESV) / "Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do." — Luke 23:34 (ESV)
VISUAL: The verse card holds alone first; then top: the pillars give way, the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies); bottom: a dark hill at noon with three crosses small against the sky. Tag: "WHILE WE WERE ENEMIES" · ROMANS 5:10.

## Landing
VO: So why is Samson in the Bible? God raised him to begin the rescue. And read beside the Gospels, every place his story breaks shows the Savior he couldn't be. [Judges 13:5; Matthew 1:21, 26:53; Luke 23:34]
VISUAL: The panels slide back together into one frame: the ridge at Lehi from the opening, first light breaking gold.
VO: Samson could only begin. Jesus said, "It is finished." [Judges 13:5; John 19:30]
VISUAL: Hold on the opening frame; the sky turns fully gold. End card.

## Value
- Samson's own people tied him up to hand him over to the Philistines; the leaders of Jesus' own people "bound him" and handed him over to Pilate [Judges 15:11–13; Matthew 27:1–2]
- Blind Samson was brought out to entertain the Philistines; Jesus was blindfolded and mocked, "Prophesy! Who is it that struck you?" [Judges 16:21, 16:25; Luke 22:63–64]
- Samson said he would "shake myself free" and couldn't; Jesus could have appealed for "more than twelve legions of angels" and didn't [Judges 16:20–21; Matthew 26:53]
- The angel said Samson would "begin to save Israel"; the angel said Jesus "will save his people from their sins" [Judges 13:5; Matthew 1:21]
- Samson's last prayer was to be "avenged… for my two eyes"; Jesus prayed, "Father, forgive them," and died for his enemies, "while we were enemies" [Judges 16:28–30; Luke 23:34; Romans 5:8–10]
