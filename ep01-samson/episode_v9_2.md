# If God knew Samson would throw it all away, why did He give him so much strength?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 95
answer: God didn't give Samson strength because he deserved it. He gave it because Israel needed saving.

## Hook
VO: If God knew Samson would throw it all away, why did He give him so much strength?
ON-SCREEN: WHY GIVE SAMSON THAT STRENGTH?
VISUAL: Samson alone on the dark stage in one warm spotlight, seven long locks, hands open. Series tag top left, episode name top right, both for the whole reel.

VISUAL: At home in Zorah, Samson points the way to Timnah (the woman appears only as a memory bubble); his parents shake their heads. Tags: SAMSON · HIS PARENTS · JUDGES 14:3.
VO: He knew Samson would tell Delilah how to take his strength away. [Judges 16:15–17; Isaiah 46:10]
VISUAL: Delilah listens in lamplight as Samson leans in to tell her. Tag: DELILAH · JUDGES 16:17.

VISUAL: Gold light rushes onto him. Quick cuts: a young lion charges and he stops it barehanded (JUDGES 14:6); the jawbone in his hand as a counter ticks to 1,000 (JUDGES 15:15).

## Turn

VISUAL: The timeline fills in: SAMUEL · 1 SAMUEL 7:13, then DAVID · 2 SAMUEL 8:1 on the right marker.

## Landing
VO: And the New Testament still lists Samson among the faithful. [Hebrews 11:32, 11:39]
VISUAL: A parchment list: GIDEON · BARAK · SAMSON · JEPHTHAH · DAVID · SAMUEL · THE PROPHETS, tagged HEBREWS 11:32, with SAMSON lighting gold.
VO: So why give Samson so much strength? Because it wasn't about Samson. It was about the people God was saving. [Judges 13:5]
VISUAL: Back to the opening composition: Samson in the spotlight.
VO: So the next time you think you've messed up too much for God to use you… remember Samson. [Judges 16:28–30; Hebrews 11:32, 11:39]
VISUAL: The sky behind Samson breaks from pre-dawn blue into gold. End card.

## Value
- Before he was born, God said Samson would "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [Judges 13:5]
- His own people had accepted Philistine rule ("The Philistines are rulers over us") and tied him up to hand him over [Judges 15:11–13]
- The marriage his parents opposed "was from the LORD, for he was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines" [Judges 14:4]
- Three times "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him": the strength came from God [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- He killed more Philistines at his death than in his whole life, and King David later "defeated the Philistines and subdued them" [Judges 16:30; 2 Samuel 8:1]
- What we learn: the strength was for the people God was saving, not a reward; Samson's choices still cost him his eyes; and the New Testament still lists him among the faithful [Judges 13:5, 16:17–21; Hebrews 11:32, 11:39]
