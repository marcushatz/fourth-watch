# Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 85
answer: God raised Samson to begin to save Israel, but the Philistines never beat Samson; Samson did. Israel's rescuer needed rescuing himself. Jesus came to save his people from their sins, and on the cross he said, "It is finished."

## Hook
VO: Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
ON-SCREEN: WHY IS SAMSON IN THE BIBLE?
VISUAL: High three-quarter wide of the ridge at Lehi at dawn: Samson standing alone with a jawbone, long braids, the Philistine camp soft in the distance; slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: Before he was born, an angel told his mother her son would "begin to save Israel" from the Philistines. Begin. Hold on to that word. [Judges 13:3–5]
VISUAL: ZORAH: the angel of the LORD appearing to Samson's mother; the word BEGIN lights gold in the narration and stays as a small gold word under the header until the last shot.

## Story

VISUAL: The cleft of the rock of Etam: men of Judah binding Samson with two new ropes, then leading him down toward Lehi as the Philistines come shouting. Tag: 3,000 MEN OF JUDAH · JUDGES 15:11.
VO: The ropes melted off his hands, and he struck down a thousand men with a donkey's jawbone. And the Philistines still ruled Israel. [Judges 15:14–15, 15:20]
VISUAL: Back to the opening ridge at Lehi: gold rushes in, the ropes flare like flax that has caught fire and melt off his hands, the jawbone raised; a counter ticks to 1,000 (no gore); then wide, the Philistine banners still standing on the far hills. Tag: JUDGES 15:15.

VO: She had his head shaved, "but he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:19–20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, a man with a razor, seven locks on the floor; the faint gold that has stayed on Samson goes out as the last lock falls. He wakes and stands, easy, unaware, as the Philistines fill the doorway.

## Turn
VO: More than a thousand years later, an angel announced another birth: "he will save his people from their sins." Not begin. Save. [Matthew 1:20–21]
VISUAL: Light sweep (time jump). Joseph asleep; an angel; the words FROM THEIR SINS light gold in the narration. Tag: MATTHEW 1:21.
VO: He was blindfolded and mocked. His own people's leaders bound him and handed him over. [Luke 22:63–64; Matthew 27:1–2]
VISUAL: Jesus seated with a cloth over his eyes, guards mocking (the same composition as blind Samson at Dagon); then bound and led by the chief priests and elders to Pilate's door (the same composition as Etam). Tags: LUKE 22:64 · MATTHEW 27:2.

VISUAL: Split for one beat: top, Samson's bonds melting off his hands at Lehi; bottom, Jesus' bound hands, still.
VO: Samson prayed for revenge. On the cross, Jesus prayed, "Father, forgive them." [Judges 16:28; Luke 23:34]
VERSE: "Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do." — Luke 23:34 (ESV)
VISUAL: A dark hill at noon, three crosses small against the sky; the verse card holds.

## Landing
VO: So why is Samson in the Bible? Israel's rescuer needed rescuing himself. [Judges 13:5, 15:18–19, 16:20–21, 16:28–30]
VISUAL: Back to the opening ridge at Lehi; Samson small against the dawn; BEGIN under the header.
VO: Jesus came to save his people from their sins. And on the cross he said, "It is finished." [Matthew 1:21; John 19:30]
VISUAL: First light breaks gold across the ridge; BEGIN becomes FINISHED under the header. Tag: JOHN 19:30. End card.

## Value
- The angel said Samson would "begin to save Israel" from the Philistines [Judges 13:5]
- The people he was born to save tied him up to hand him over, because "the Philistines are rulers over us" [Judges 15:11–13]
- After a thousand men at Lehi, the Philistines still ruled; he judged Israel "in the days of the Philistines" [Judges 15:15, 15:20]
- After his head was shaved, "he did not know that the LORD had left him," and his last prayer was for revenge [Judges 16:19–20, 16:28]
- Samson's bonds melted off his hands; Jesus let his bonds hold, and prayed "Father, forgive them" [Judges 15:14; Matthew 26:53–54; Luke 23:34]
- What we learn: Israel's rescuer needed rescuing himself; Jesus came to save his people from their sins [Judges 13:5, 15:18–19, 16:28; Matthew 1:21; John 19:30]

## Caption
Why is Samson's story even in the Bible? Look at one word in Judges 13:5: "begin."
And yes, Hebrews 11:32 still lists him among the faithful. Faith is what cries out to be rescued.
