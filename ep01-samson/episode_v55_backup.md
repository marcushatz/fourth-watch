# If God is still using you, does that mean He's okay with how you're living?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 100
answer: God still using you doesn't mean He approves of how you're living.

## Hook
VO: If God is still using you, does that mean He's okay with how you're living?
ON-SCREEN: IF GOD STILL USES YOU, IS HE OKAY WITH HOW YOU LIVE?

## Stakes
VO: Let's look at Samson, known as the strongest man in the Bible. His strength came from God. Yet he slept with a prostitute in Gaza. That same night, while men lay in wait to kill him, he pulled up the city gate, posts and all. [Judges 14:6, 16:1-3, 16:17, 16:20]

## Story
VO: Later, he fell in love with Delilah, and the Philistine lords, Israel's enemies, offered her silver to find the secret of his strength. [Judges 16:4-5, 16:18]
VO: Three times she said, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" And three times he broke free. [Judges 16:8-14]
VO: Then he told her his secret. While he slept, she had a man shave off his uncut hair, the sign he was set apart to God. [Judges 13:5, 16:17-19; Numbers 6:5-7]
VO: She called a fourth time. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." [Judges 16:20]
VO: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)

## Turn
VO: Notice what he said: "as at other times." It had always worked before, so he assumed it would work again. [Judges 15:14, 16:1-3, 16:9-14, 16:20]
VO: You can be gifted, lead, and help people. But God still using you doesn't mean He approves of how you're living. [Judges 13:5, 14:4, 15:20, 16:1-3, 16:20; Matthew 7:22-23]
VO: Jesus warned that on judgment day, many will point to the mighty works they did in His name, and He will declare, "I never knew you." [Matthew 7:22-23]

## Landing
VO: But Samson's story doesn't end there. Blinded and brought out to entertain his captors, he prayed, "O Lord GOD, please remember me and please strengthen me only this once..." Even then, he asked for revenge. [Judges 16:21, 16:25, 16:28]
VERSE: "Then Samson called to the LORD and said, 'O Lord GOD, please remember me and please strengthen me only this once, O God, that I may be avenged on the Philistines for my two eyes.'" — Judges 16:28 (ESV)
VO: His choices had cost him his eyes and his freedom. But this time, he didn't assume it would work. He asked. [Judges 16:17-21, 16:20, 16:28]
VO: Then he pushed against the pillars with all his strength, and the building fell on the Philistine lords and everyone inside. Samson died with them. [Judges 16:29-30]
VO: More than a thousand years later, Hebrews 11 still names him among its examples of faith. [Hebrews 11:32]

## Question
VO: What would you ask God for, only this once? [Judges 16:28]

## Value
- His strength was the LORD's, and it still worked the same night he slept with a prostitute in Gaza [Judges 14:6, 16:1-3, 16:20]
- Delilah was paid by the Philistine leaders, and she used the exact same words every time [Judges 16:5, 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:18, 16:20]
- His uncut hair was the sign he was set apart to God [Judges 13:5, 16:17; Numbers 6:5-7]
- "He did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- At his lowest he prayed "remember me," and Hebrews 11 still names him among its examples of faith [Judges 16:28; Hebrews 11:32]

## Caption
If God is still using you, is He okay with how you're living? Samson's strength kept working right up until the day it didn't. Is something that's "working" in your life making it easy to ignore something you already know needs to change? Read it yourself: Judges 16, Matthew 7:21-23, Hebrews 11:32.
