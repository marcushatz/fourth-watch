# If God knew Samson would throw it all away, why did He give him so much strength?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 88
answer: God didn't give Samson strength as a reward. Israel needed someone to start the fight.

## Value
- Before he was born, God said Samson would only "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [Judges 13:5]
- His strength came when "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him", not from himself [Judges 14:6, 15:14]
- Even the marriage his parents opposed "was from the LORD", and it started the fight with the Philistines [Judges 14:3–4, 14:19–15:8]
- Three thousand men of his own people accepted Philistine rule and tied him up to hand him over [Judges 15:11–13]
- He killed more Philistines at his death than in his whole life [Judges 16:30]
- David, who killed the Philistine Goliath, is the one God promised would "save my people Israel from the hand of the Philistines": almost word for word Samson's "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [1 Samuel 17; 2 Samuel 3:18; Judges 13:5]
- What we learn: the strength wasn't a reward but a beginning for the people God was saving; Samson's failures didn't stop God's plan, but they still cost him his eyes; God finished through David what Samson began [Judges 13:5, 16:17–21; 2 Samuel 3:18, 8:1]
