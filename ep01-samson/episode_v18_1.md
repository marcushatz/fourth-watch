# What was Samson's real weakness?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 82
answer: He let his own eyes lead, and his strength never warned him. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work.

## Hook
VO: What was Samson's real weakness?
ON-SCREEN: WHAT WAS SAMSON'S REAL WEAKNESS?
VISUAL: Samson blind at the mill in Gaza, bronze shackles, hair beginning to grow back; slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: It wasn't strength. Three times, "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him." No other judge has the Spirit come on him that often. [Judges 13:25, 14:6, 14:19, 15:14; cf. 3:10, 6:34, 11:29]
VISUAL: Fast montage over the line: the lion torn apart barehanded; thirty garments at Ashkelon; the ropes melting at Lehi and the jawbone raised. Gold rushes in on each. A small gold strength mark appears under the header and stays lit. No gore.
VO: And the Spirit "rushed upon" only three men in the whole Bible: Samson, Saul and David. Hold on to that. [Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 10:10, 16:13]
VISUAL: Three silhouettes side by side, each with a gold rush: SAMSON · SAUL · DAVID. On screen: HEBREW TSALACH: USED OF THE SPIRIT ONLY FOR THESE THREE.

## Story
VO: But watch Samson's eyes. "He saw one of the daughters of the Philistines." "Get her for me, for she is right in my eyes." [Judges 14:1–3]
VISUAL: At home in Zorah: Samson pointing down the road toward Timnah while his parents shake their heads. On screen: HEBREW YASHRAH B'EINAI, "RIGHT IN MY EYES." Tag: JUDGES 14:3.
VO: God used even that. But his eyes were choosing. [Judges 14:4]
VISUAL: Hold on Samson's face turned toward Timnah; the gold mark still lit. Tag: JUDGES 14:4.
VO: In Gaza, "he saw a prostitute." That same night, his strength still worked. He carried off the city gates. [Judges 16:1–3]
VISUAL: Midnight in Gaza: Samson carrying the gate doors and posts up a hill; the gold mark holds. Tag: JUDGES 16:3.
VO: Then one day, "he did not know that the LORD had left him." The very next verse: the Philistines "gouged out his eyes." In a way, he was blind before they ever touched him. [Judges 16:19–21]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, seven locks on the floor; the gold mark goes out as the last lock falls. He wakes and stands, easy, unaware, as the Philistines fill the doorway; cut to black on "gouged".
VO: Even his last prayer was about his eyes: "that I may be avenged on the Philistines for my two eyes." [Judges 16:28]
VISUAL: The house of Dagon: blind Samson at the two middle pillars, a young man at his side; the gold mark relights as he prays; the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 16:28.

## Turn
VO: Now the other two men. "The Spirit of the LORD departed from Saul." And when the prophet Nathan confronted David, he prayed: "take not your Holy Spirit from me." [1 Samuel 16:14; 2 Samuel 12:7; Psalm 51 title, 51:11]
VISUAL: The three silhouettes return: Saul's gold drains away; David kneels, the gold holding. Tags: 1 SAMUEL 16:14 · PSALM 51:11.
VO: Saul lost it. Samson didn't know it was gone. Only David is heard begging God not to take it away. [1 Samuel 16:14; Judges 16:20; Psalm 51:11]
VISUAL: Locked hold on the three silhouettes: SAUL (dark) · SAMSON (dark, eyes closed, small caption 16:20) · DAVID (lit).

## Landing
VO: And Judges ends with one line about the whole nation: "Everyone did what was right in his own eyes." [Judges 17:6, 21:25]
VISUAL: Wide over Israel's hill towns at dusk; the line on screen, RIGHT IN HIS OWN EYES, beside the earlier RIGHT IN MY EYES. Tag: JUDGES 21:25.
VO: So what was Samson's real weakness? He let his own eyes lead, and his strength never warned him. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work. [Judges 14:3, 16:1–3, 16:20; 21:25]
VISUAL: Back to the opening frame: the mill in Gaza, now empty, in cold light; a few cut hairs on the floor. End card.

## Value
- The Spirit of the LORD "rushed upon" Samson three times, more often than the Spirit came on any other judge [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14; 3:10, 6:34, 11:29]
- The Bible says the Spirit "rushed upon" only three men: Samson, Saul and David [Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 10:10, 16:13]
- "She is right in my eyes," and the same idea closes the book: "Everyone did what was right in his own eyes" [Judges 14:3; 17:6; 21:25]
- The night of the Gaza prostitute, his strength still carried off the city gates [Judges 16:1–3]
- "He did not know that the LORD had left him"; the next verse, they "gouged out his eyes"; his last prayer was "for my two eyes" [Judges 16:20–21, 16:28]
- The Spirit "departed from Saul"; David prayed "take not your Holy Spirit from me" [1 Samuel 16:14; Psalm 51:11]
- What we learn: don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work [Judges 16:20]
