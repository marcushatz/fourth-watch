# Delilah tried to hand Samson over three times. So why did he tell her his secret?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 78
answer: She wore him down, and three times, even with his hair woven into the loom, nothing had happened, so he expected to go out as at other times. Getting away with it before doesn't mean God is with you now.

## Value
- Each Philistine lord offered Delilah 1,100 pieces of silver [Judges 16:5]
- His lies moved toward the truth: bowstrings, new ropes, then his own seven locks woven into the loom, and he still broke free [Judges 16:7, 16:11, 16:13–14]
- She asked for his heart ("your heart is not with me"), and the moment "he had told her all his heart," she sent for the lords, who came "with the money in their hands" [Judges 16:15, 16:17–18]
- She shouted "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" four times; the first three times he simply broke free [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
- He said himself, "If my head is shaved, then my strength will leave me," yet woke saying, "I will go out as at other times" [Judges 16:17, 16:20]
- "He did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- What we learn: getting away with it before doesn't mean God is with you now [Judges 16:20]
