# Was Samson a hero or a villain?
series: HERO OR VILLAIN?
target_seconds: 48

## Hook
VO: Was Samson a hero or a villain?
ON-SCREEN: WAS SAMSON A HERO OR A VILLAIN?
VISUAL: Samson stands alone between two huge stone pillars under one spotlight; dust drifts.

## Stakes
VO: Most people remember the strong man. Few remember his last prayer. [Judges 16:3, 16:28]
VISUAL: Samson carries the doors of the city gate of Gaza on his shoulders; then darkness, his clasped hands in a thin beam.

## Story
VO: He was set apart to God before he was born. [Judges 13:5]
VISUAL: His mother holds a newborn in a column of gold light. A gold scale appears at the top of the frame; a gold weight drops on the left pan.
VO: He struck down a thousand men with a jawbone. [Judges 15:15]
VISUAL: Low angle, Samson raises a jawbone; a line of Philistine silhouettes falls away. A gold jawbone drops on the left pan.
VO: But he went to a prostitute in Gaza. [Judges 16:1]
VISUAL: Night in Gaza; Samson walks toward a lamp-lit doorway; the door closes. An ember weight drops on the right pan.
VO: He told Delilah what would take his strength. [Judges 16:17]
VISUAL: Lamp-lit room; Delilah in the foreground, Samson speaking. A razor drops on the right pan.
VO: And when his head was shaved, the Lord had left him. [Judges 16:19–20]
VISUAL: Seven locks of hair fall; the spotlight above Samson fades to dark. The scale tips hard to the right.
VO: The Philistines gouged out his eyes. [Judges 16:21]
VISUAL: Philistine silhouettes close in; cut to black; Samson, eyes closed, grinds at a mill in prison.

## Turn
VO: Then, blind, in his enemies' temple, he prayed. [Judges 16:23–28]
VISUAL: A huge house full of people, crowds on the roof; a boy leads Samson by the hand to the two middle pillars.
VO: He asked to be avenged for his two eyes. [Judges 16:28]
VISUAL: Close on Samson's face lifted upward, hands on the pillars; the camera holds completely still.
VO: "Let me die with the Philistines." [Judges 16:30]
VERSE: "Let me die with the Philistines." — Judges 16:30 (ESV)
VISUAL: Verse card over his hands pressing the pillars.
VO: He pushed the pillars, and the house fell. [Judges 16:29–30]
VISUAL: The pillars crack, the roof comes down, dust fills the frame.
VO: And Hebrews still lists him among people of faith. [Hebrews 11:32]
VISUAL: First light: the dust becomes dawn; a row of six figures on a ridge, Samson among them, lit gold.

## Landing
VO: Hero or villain? The text shows both. And God still answered him.
VISUAL: The scale settles level. Back to the opening frame: Samson between the two pillars.
