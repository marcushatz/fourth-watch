# Samson: building-block library, round 1

MESSAGE the whole reel must prove (Samson only, no other characters):
Samson's God-given strength kept working while he lived by what was right in his own eyes, so its working told him nothing about where he stood with God. A gift from God is not a verdict from God.

Rules: ESV only; state what the text says in its order; never chain facts into causes the text doesn't give (e.g. never "his eyes led him, so the LORD left"). Bold claim at the start, bold claim at the end. Visuals carry the famous story; narration carries value.

## STEP 2: SLOT CANDIDATES

### OPENING: bold claim (pick 1)
- O1. What was Samson's real weakness? His strength.
- O2. The most dangerous thing in Samson's life wasn't Delilah. It was that his strength kept working.
- O3. God's Spirit came on Samson more than on any judge in the Bible, and it never once told him he was drifting.
- O4. God's power worked through Samson right up until the day it didn't, and he didn't even notice.
- O5. Samson's strength was a gift from God. It was also the thing that fooled him.
- O6. Samson is the strongest warning in the Bible about God-given gifts.

### SETUP: the gift (pick 1; montage carries the feats)
- S1. "The Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him" three times, more than on any other judge. A lion. Thirty men. A thousand. [14:6, 14:19, 15:14; cf. 3:10, 6:34, 11:29]
- S2. His strength came from God. Three times, "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him." No other judge has the Spirit come on him that often. [same]
- S3. Before he was born, an angel said he would "begin to save Israel." And God's Spirit came on him like on no other judge. [13:5; 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]

### MIDDLE VALUE PIECES (pick 3–4, in story order)
- M1. He picked a wife because "she is right in my eyes." And the text says God still used it: "it was from the LORD." [14:3–4]
- M2. Look when the Spirit came the second time: in the middle of a wedding bet. The guests cheated on his riddle, he struck down thirty men to pay it, and "in hot anger he went back to his father's house." [14:12–19]
- M3. In Gaza, he went to a prostitute. That same night, he pulled up the city gates, posts and all, and carried them up a hill. The strength didn't flinch. [16:1–3]
- M4. Three times Delilah set a trap and shouted, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" Three times the strength bailed him out. [16:6–14]
- M5. He scraped honey out of a lion's carcass and didn't tell his parents where it came from. [14:8–9]
- M6. The hair was never where the strength came from. It was the sign he was set apart to God from birth. [13:5; 16:17; Num 6:7; 16:20]

### TURN: the detail most people miss (pick 1)
- T1. Then "he told her all his heart." She had his head shaved. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times…" "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [16:17–20]
- T2. He woke up expecting it to work like always: "I will go out as at other times." Every time before, it had. "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [16:20]
- T3. The scariest line in his story isn't the haircut. It's the next one: "he did not know that the LORD had left him." [16:19–20]

### AFTERMATH (optional, pick 0–1)
- A1. Blind and in chains, that's the first time we see him ask God for strength: "strengthen me only this once." [16:21, 16:28]
- A2. Blind, he finally asked: "strengthen me only this once." And God answered. [16:28–30]

### CLOSING: bold claim + lesson (pick 1)
- C1. A gift from God is not a verdict from God.
- C2. Your gifts can keep working while your life drifts. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work.
- C3. Being used by God is not the same as walking with God.
- C4. God's power on your life is not God's approval of your life.
- C5. Samson's strength never told him the truth about where he stood with God. Your gifts won't either.
- C6. Check your heart, not your gift.
- C7. (C1 + practical) A gift from God is not a verdict from God. Don't judge where you stand with Him by how well your gifts still work.
