# If God knew Samson would throw it all away, why did He give him so much strength?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 88
answer: Israel needed someone to start the fight. Samson only had to start it; David would finish it.

## Hook
VO: If God knew Samson would throw it all away, why did He give him so much strength?
ON-SCREEN: WHY GIVE SAMSON THAT STRENGTH?
VISUAL: High three-quarter wide on a low-poly ridge at Lehi at dawn: Samson bound with two new ropes, two men of Judah behind him, Philistines coming up the path from their camp, shouting. The two men of Judah step back as the Philistines arrive. Cut closer as he strains; on "give" gold light breaks from the upper left, the ropes on his arms flare like flax catching fire and melt off his hands, and he stands free. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

VISUAL: Gold light from the upper left on "Spirit". Quick cuts: alone in the vineyards of Timnah, a young lion comes at him roaring and he stops it barehanded (JUDGES 14:6); on the ridge at Lehi, a donkey's jawbone in his hand as a counter ticks to 1,000 (JUDGES 15:15).

VISUAL: He leans on the two middle pillars; the gold light flares and the temple falls in stone and dust (no bodies shown). Small tag: JUDGES 16:30.

## Landing

VO: God promised: "By the hand of my servant David I will save my people Israel from the hand of the Philistines." [2 Samuel 3:18]
VERSE: "By the hand of my servant David I will save my people Israel from the hand of the Philistines…" — 2 Samuel 3:18 (ESV)
VISUAL: On the card, WILL SAVE lights gold; the timeline reads SAMSON · BEGIN on the left, DAVID · WILL SAVE on the right; behind the card, King David above Israel's towns as the Philistine banners come down.
VO: So why give Samson so much strength, knowing he'd fail? He only had to start the fight. David would finish it. [Judges 13:1, 13:5, 14:4, 16:17–21; Isaiah 46:10; 2 Samuel 3:18, 8:1, 19:9]
VISUAL: Back to the opening composition on the ridge at Lehi, Samson standing free; the sky breaks from pre-dawn blue into gold. End card.

## Value
- Before he was born, the angel said Samson would only "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [Judges 13:5]
- His strength came when "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him", not from himself [Judges 14:6, 15:14]
- Israel had accepted Philistine rule, and three thousand men of his own people tied him up to hand him over [Judges 14:4, 15:11–13]
- He killed more Philistines at his death than in his whole life [Judges 16:30]
- David, who killed the Philistine Goliath, is the one God promised would "save my people Israel from the hand of the Philistines", almost word for word Samson's "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [1 Samuel 17; 2 Samuel 3:18; Judges 13:5]
- What we learn: God gave the strength knowing Samson would fail, because Samson only had to start the fight Israel wouldn't start; his failures still cost him his eyes; David would finish it [Judges 13:5, 14:4, 16:19–21; 2 Samuel 3:18, 8:1]
