# What's the real lesson of Samson? It isn't "don't cut your hair."
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 58
answer: The strength was God's, and when God left, Samson didn't even notice. Say it before the fight.

## Landing

VO: So what's the real lesson of Samson? The strength was God's. Say it before the fight. [Judges 16:20, 16:28; 1 Samuel 17:47]
VISUAL: Back to the opening: Samson at Lehi in gold, same framing as the first frame, the sky breaking into gold. End card.

## Value
- Delilah tried three times before the haircut; each time she shouted "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" and each time he broke free [Judges 16:6–14]
- The hair was the sign of his vow: "A razor has never come upon my head, for I have been a Nazirite to God" [Judges 13:5, 16:17]
- When he woke he expected to "go out as at other times", but "he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- His strength was the LORD's: the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him at the lion, Ashkelon and Lehi, and his strength left when the LORD left [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14, 16:19–20]
- His only recorded prayer for strength came at the very end, blind, and God answered [Judges 16:28–30]
- What we learn: Samson asked for God's strength after he had lost everything; David said "the battle is the LORD's" before the fight. Say it before the fight [Judges 16:28; 1 Samuel 17:47]
