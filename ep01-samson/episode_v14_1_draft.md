# Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 70
answer: His story matches Jesus' until it breaks, and the places Samson failed are the places Jesus didn't: Samson could only begin to save; Jesus finished it.

## Hook
VO: Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
ON-SCREEN: WHY IS SAMSON IN THE BIBLE?
VISUAL: High three-quarter wide of the ridge at Lehi at dawn: Samson standing alone with a jawbone, long braids, Philistine camp soft in the distance. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: He chased a Philistine wife, and told Delilah his secret after she tried to hand him over three times. [Judges 14:1–3, 16:4–17]
VISUAL: Two fast cuts: Samson pointing toward a Philistine woman in the vineyards of Timnah; Delilah's lamp-lit room with a 1-2-3 counter flicking past.
VO: Put him next to Jesus, and he matches. Until he doesn't. And the places he doesn't are why he's here.
VISUAL: The frame splits into two stacked panels joined by a thin gold seam: top panel JUDGES (Samson's world, warm clay and ember), bottom panel THE GOSPELS (Jerusalem, night blue and stone). The two panels mirror each other in composition.

## Turn
VO: Now watch where it breaks. [Judges 16:20; Matthew 26:53]
VISUAL: The gold seam between the panels cracks; the camera holds still.
VO: Samson said, "I will… shake myself free," and couldn't. Jesus could have asked for "more than twelve legions of angels," and didn't. [Judges 16:20–21; Matthew 26:53]
VISUAL: Top: Samson straining as the Philistines seize him. Bottom: Jesus in the garden, still, telling Peter to put away his sword; faint light high above him.

## Landing
VO: Before Samson was born, the angel said he would "begin to save Israel." Before Jesus was born: "he will save his people from their sins." [Judges 13:5; Matthew 1:21]
VISUAL: Top: the angel of the LORD before Samson's mother in the field, BEGIN lighting gold. Bottom: an angel before Joseph in a dream, WILL SAVE lighting gold. Tags: JUDGES 13:5 · MATTHEW 1:21.
VO: So why is Samson in the Bible? The places he failed are the places Jesus didn't. [Judges 13:5, 16:20, 16:28; Matthew 1:21, 26:53; Luke 23:34]
VISUAL: The panels slide back together into one frame: the ridge at Lehi from the opening, first light breaking gold.
VO: Samson could only begin. Jesus said, "It is finished." [Judges 13:5; John 19:30]
VISUAL: Hold on the opening frame; the sky turns fully gold. End card.

## Value
- Samson's own people, 3,000 men of Judah, bound him and handed him to the Philistines; Pilate told Jesus, "Your own nation… delivered you over to me" [Judges 15:11–13; John 18:35]
- Blind Samson was brought out to entertain the Philistines; Jesus was blindfolded and mocked, "Prophesy! Who is it that struck you?" [Judges 16:21, 16:25; Luke 22:63–64]
- Samson killed more in his death than in his life; Jesus "through death" destroyed "the one who has the power of death" [Judges 16:30; Hebrews 2:14]
- Samson said he would "shake myself free" and couldn't; Jesus could have appealed for "more than twelve legions of angels" and didn't [Judges 16:20–21; Matthew 26:53]
- Samson's last prayer was to be "avenged… for my two eyes"; Jesus prayed, "Father, forgive them" [Judges 16:28; Luke 23:34]
- The angel said Samson would "begin to save Israel"; the angel said Jesus "will save his people from their sins" [Judges 13:5; Matthew 1:21]
