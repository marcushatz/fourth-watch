# Delilah tried to hand Samson over three times. Why did he still tell her his secret?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 82
answer: She framed guarding his secret as not loving her and pressed him day after day, and three times before playing along had cost him nothing, so he woke expecting to go out as at other times. Three free escapes don't promise a fourth.

## Hook
VO: Delilah tried to hand Samson over three times. Why did he still tell her his secret?
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID SAMSON TELL DELILAH?
VISUAL: Delilah's lamp-lit room, high three-quarter: Samson asleep on a mat, Delilah beside the lamp; through a doorway, an inner room with Philistine men crouched in ambush. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: Each Philistine lord had offered her eleven hundred pieces of silver. [Judges 16:5]
VISUAL: Philistine lords in feathered crowns at Delilah's door making their offer, each pointing to himself as a big number ticks to 1,100. Tag: JUDGES 16:5.
VO: Three times he lied: bowstrings, new ropes, his hair in a loom. Three times she shouted, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" Three times he broke free. [Judges 16:7–14]
VISUAL: A 1-2-3 counter under the header. Three fast escapes: seven fresh bowstrings snap like flax touched by fire, men in the inner room (16:9); new ropes snap like thread, men in the inner room (16:12); his seven locks woven into the web, and he wakes and pulls away the pin, the loom and the web (16:14).

## Story
VO: Then she made it about love: "How can you say, 'I love you,' when your heart is not with me? You have mocked me these three times…" [Judges 16:15]
VERSE: "How can you say, 'I love you,' when your heart is not with me? You have mocked me these three times, and you have not told me where your great strength lies." — Judges 16:15 (ESV)
VISUAL: Delilah turned away from Samson by the lamp, arms folded, wounded; Samson reaching toward her.
VO: She was the one promised silver to hand him over. Yet she accused him of mocking her. [Judges 16:5, 16:15]
VISUAL: The same shot, wider: behind Delilah, out of Samson's sight, a ghosted "1,100 × EACH LORD" hangs in the air like a memory of the offer, and the lords' silhouettes wait at the window. Tag: JUDGES 16:5 · 16:15.
VO: And she kept at it "day after day," until "his soul was vexed to death." [Judges 16:16]
VISUAL: The same room over many days: lamp lit, lamp out, lamp lit; Delilah talking, Samson slumped with his head in his hands.
VO: Then "he told her all his heart": "If my head is shaved, then my strength will leave me…" [Judges 16:17]
VERSE: "A razor has never come upon my head, for I have been a Nazirite to God from my mother's womb. If my head is shaved, then my strength will leave me…" — Judges 16:17 (ESV)
VO: Bowstrings couldn't hold Samson. New ropes couldn't. Her words got through. [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:16–17]
VISUAL: The snapped bowstrings and the snapped ropes drop onto the floor between them, then the pin and the web, pulled loose.

## Turn
VO: She sent for the lords, and they "brought the money in their hands." [Judges 16:18]
VISUAL: A messenger slips out; the lords climbing the stairs at night, bags of silver in hand. Tag: "ALL HIS HEART" · JUDGES 16:18.
VO: She had him shaved in his sleep and shouted it a fourth time. He woke and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." [Judges 16:19–20]
VISUAL: Samson asleep on Delilah's knees; a man with a razor; seven dark locks on the floor; the counter adds a 4. Then Samson stands, easy, rolling his shoulders, as the lords and their men crowd the doorway.
VO: She'd known the moment he told her all his heart. But he "did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:18, 16:20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Split frame, locked: Delilah by the lamp, sure; Samson in the doorway among the Philistines, unaware, the faint gold that has stayed on him all reel now gone.

## Landing
VO: So why did Samson tell Delilah? She framed guarding his secret as not loving her, and pressed him day after day. And three times before, even with his hair in a loom, playing along had cost him nothing. [Judges 16:9–17, 16:20]
VISUAL: The Philistines lead him away; then the prison mill at Gaza in cold light, Samson already blind, in bronze shackles (the blinding itself is not shown). Tag: BLINDED · GAZA · JUDGES 16:21.
VO: Three free escapes don't promise a fourth. [Judges 16:20–21]
VISUAL: Back to the opening view of Delilah's room, now empty: snapped bowstrings, snapped ropes, the pin and the web on the floor beside seven locks; the counter reads 1 2 3 4. End card.

## Value
- Each Philistine lord offered Delilah 1,100 pieces of silver [Judges 16:5]
- While promised silver to hand him over, she accused him: "You have mocked me these three times" and "your heart is not with me" [Judges 16:5, 16:15]
- The text names the cause: she pressed him "day after day," "his soul was vexed to death," and "he told her all his heart" [Judges 16:16–17]
- By his third lie he was already talking about his hair, and he still broke free [Judges 16:13–14]
- "When Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart," she sent for the lords; but he woke saying "I will go out as at other times," and "he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:18, 16:20]
- What we learn: three free escapes don't promise a fourth [Judges 16:20–21]
