# Samson v21 (lesson-heavy; fact-checked; viewer test v20 = 7/10, fixes applied)
HOOK: Your gift can keep working while you're doing what's right in your own eyes. Samson is the proof. [14:3; 16:1-3]
STORY: His gift was real. "The Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him" three times, more than on any other judge. [14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
STORY: The first choice we see him make? A wife, because "she is right in my eyes." [14:2-3]
TAKEAWAY: That's how drift can start. Not rejecting God. Just letting what looks right to you decide. [14:3; 21:25]
STORY: In Gaza, he went in to a prostitute, and that same night he carried off the city gates. [16:1-3]
TAKEAWAY: That's the trap. When your gift still works, it's easy to assume God is fine with how you're living.
TAKEAWAY: Why did it keep working? The text ties it to God's purpose, to "begin to save Israel," never to a reward for how he lived. [13:5; 14:4]
TURN: He knew the secret of his strength and told Delilah all of it. His hair was shaved, and he woke up expecting it to work "as at other times." "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [16:17, 16:19-20]
LESSON: A gift from God is not a verdict from God.
APPLY: Maybe your post blew up the same week you justified something you know isn't right. That's your Gaza. Name one choice you made because it looked right to you, and ask: right in God's eyes, or just in mine? [14:3; Deut 12:25]
