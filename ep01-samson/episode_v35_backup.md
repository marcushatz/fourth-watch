# If God is still using you, does that mean He's okay with how you're living?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 125
answer: If God is still using you, that shows what He can do through you, not where you stand with Him.

## Hook
VO: If God is still using you, does that mean He's okay with how you're living?
ON-SCREEN: IF GOD STILL USES YOU, IS HE OKAY WITH HOW YOU LIVE?

## Stakes
VO: Look at Samson, known as the strongest man in the Bible. He slept with a prostitute in Gaza, and that same night he pulled the city gates out of the ground. [Judges 16:1-3]

## Story
VO: In fact, the book of Judges records that the Spirit of the LORD moved on him four times, more than on any other judge. [Judges 13:25, 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
VO: Now look at how many times Delilah called him. [Judges 16:4-20]
VO: Four times, the exact same words: "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
VO: Three times, he broke free. [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:14]
VO: But when Samson finally told her the secret of his strength, she had his hair shaved, the sign he was set apart to God. [Judges 13:5, 16:6, 16:17-19]
VO: The fourth time, he woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." [Judges 16:20]
VO: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)

## Turn
VO: So what does this mean for us? Three things. [Judges 13:5, 16:28]
VO: One: God gives gifts for His glory and for others, not because we earned them. [Judges 13:5, 14:4; 1 Peter 4:10-11]
VO: Before Samson was born, the angel said he would "begin to save Israel." His strength was for God's plan and for his people, not for him. [Judges 13:5, 14:4]
VO: Two: a working gift doesn't mean God approves of how you're living. [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20]
VO: Samson's strength worked the same night he slept with a prostitute in Gaza. And when it was gone, he didn't even know. [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20]

## Landing
VO: Three: when you're at your lowest, you can still call on God. [Judges 16:21-30]
VO: Blind and a prisoner, he prayed, "O Lord GOD, please remember me and please strengthen me only this once." [Judges 16:21, 16:28]
VO: God answered, and the house of Dagon fell. [Judges 16:28-30]
VO: And Hebrews 11 names him among those who "through faith" "were made strong out of weakness." [Hebrews 11:32-34]
VO: So if God is still using you, that shows what He can do through you, not where you stand with Him. [Judges 13:5, 14:4, 16:1-3, 16:20]
VO: Samson's last prayer was "remember me." When has God answered you at your lowest? Tell us in the comments. [Judges 16:28-30]

## Value
- His strength came from the LORD; the hair was the sign of his consecration [Judges 14:6, 16:17, 16:20, 16:28]
- Four times the Spirit of the LORD moved on him ("began to stir him" once, "rushed upon him" three times), more than on any other judge; and four times Delilah said the exact same words. The fourth time, the LORD had left him [Judges 13:25, 14:6, 14:19, 15:14; 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
- Gifts are for God's glory and for others: before he was born, Samson would "begin to save Israel"; Peter says gifts are to "serve one another" so that "in everything God may be glorified" [Judges 13:5; 1 Peter 4:10-11]
- His strength worked the same night he slept with a prostitute in Gaza, and when it was gone he "did not know" [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20]
- At his lowest, blind and a prisoner, he called on God, and God answered [Judges 16:21, 16:28-30]
- Hebrews 11 names Samson among those who "through faith" "were made strong out of weakness" [Hebrews 11:32-34]
