# Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 80
answer: Even the strongest man could only begin to save. Jesus came to finish it, and on the cross he said, "It is finished."

## Hook
VO: Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
ON-SCREEN: WHY IS SAMSON IN THE BIBLE?
VISUAL: High three-quarter wide of the ridge at Lehi at dawn: Samson standing alone with a jawbone, long braids, the Philistine camp soft in the distance; slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: Before he was born, an angel told his mother that her son would "begin to save Israel" from the Philistines. Begin. Hold on to that word. [Judges 13:3–5]
VISUAL: ZORAH: the angel of the LORD appearing to Samson's mother; the word BEGIN lights gold in the narration and stays as a small gold word under the header for the rest of the story.

## Story
VO: Samson grew up strong. But he lived for what he wanted: "Get her for me, for she is right in my eyes." [Judges 13:24, 14:3]
VISUAL: Samson as a young man in the vineyards of Timnah, pointing at a Philistine woman while his father and mother shake their heads. Tag: JUDGES 14:3.
VO: His own people tied him up to hand him over to the Philistines. [Judges 15:11–13]
VISUAL: The cleft of the rock of Etam: men of Judah binding Samson with two new ropes. Tag: 3,000 MEN OF JUDAH · JUDGES 15:11.
VO: Delilah tried to hand him over three times, and he still told her his secret. [Judges 16:4–17]
VISUAL: Delilah's lamp-lit room; a 1-2-3 counter flicks past; Samson leaning in to tell her.
VO: They shaved his head. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times." "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:19–20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, a man with a razor, seven locks on the floor; he stands, easy, as Philistines fill the doorway; the faint gold that has stayed on him goes out.
VO: Blinded, he was brought out to entertain his enemies. His last prayer was for revenge. He pulled the house down and died with them. [Judges 16:21–30]
VISUAL: The house of Dagon: blind Samson led by a young man between laughing Philistines; his hands on the two middle pillars; the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 16:28–30.
VO: He began to save Israel. He couldn't finish. [Judges 13:5, 16:30–31]
VISUAL: Dust settling over the ruins; the small gold BEGIN under the header, alone.

## Turn
VO: More than a thousand years later, an angel announced another birth: "he will save his people from their sins." Not begin. Save. [Matthew 1:20–21]
VISUAL: Light sweep (time jump). Joseph asleep; an angel; WILL SAVE lights gold beside BEGIN under the header. Tag: MATTHEW 1:21.
VO: And his story echoes Samson's. His own people's leaders bound him and handed him over. He was blindfolded and mocked. [Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 22:63–64]
VISUAL: Jesus, bound, led by the chief priests and elders to Pilate's door; then seated with a cloth over his eyes, guards mocking. The compositions match the Etam and Dagon shots. Tags: MATTHEW 27:2 · LUKE 22:64.
VO: Samson couldn't break free. Jesus could have appealed to his Father for "more than twelve legions of angels," and didn't. [Judges 16:21; Matthew 26:53]
VISUAL: The garden just after Peter's strike: Jesus still, telling Peter to put his sword back; faint light high above.
VO: Samson prayed for revenge. Jesus prayed, "Father, forgive them." [Judges 16:28; Luke 23:34]
VERSE: "Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do." — Luke 23:34 (ESV)

## Landing
VO: So why is Samson in the Bible? He shows that even the strongest man could only begin. [Judges 13:5, 16:30]
VISUAL: Back to the opening ridge at Lehi; Samson small against the dawn; BEGIN under the header.
VO: Jesus came to finish. On the cross he said, "It is finished." [Matthew 1:21; John 19:30]
VISUAL: First light breaks gold across the ridge; BEGIN becomes FINISHED under the header. End card.

## Value
- The angel said Samson would only "begin to save Israel" from the Philistines [Judges 13:5]
- His own people, the men of Judah, tied him up to hand him over [Judges 15:11–13]
- He woke up saying "I will go out as at other times," "but he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- His last prayer was for revenge "for my two eyes" [Judges 16:28]
- The angel said Jesus "will save his people from their sins"; he could have appealed for "more than twelve legions of angels" and didn't, and prayed "Father, forgive them" [Matthew 1:21, 26:53; Luke 23:34]
- What we learn: even the strongest man could only begin; Jesus came to finish [Judges 13:5; John 19:30]
