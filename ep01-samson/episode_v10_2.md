# If God knew Samson would throw it all away, why did He give him so much strength?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 88
answer: God didn't give Samson strength because he deserved it. He gave it because Israel needed saving.

## Hook
VO: If God knew Samson would throw it all away, why did He give him so much strength?
ON-SCREEN: WHY GIVE SAMSON THAT STRENGTH?

The two men of Judah step back as the Philistines arrive. Cut closer as he strains; on "give" gold light breaks from the upper left, the ropes on his arms flare like flax catching fire and melt off his hands, and he stands free. ## Turn
VO: So God didn't give Samson strength because he deserved it. He gave it because Israel needed saving. [Judges 13:1, 13:5]

## Landing

VISUAL: Back to the opening composition on the ridge at Lehi, Samson standing free; the sky breaks from pre-dawn blue into gold. End card.

## Value
- Before he was born, God said Samson would only "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [Judges 13:5]
- His strength came when "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him", not from himself [Judges 14:6, 15:14]
- The marriage his parents opposed "was from the LORD", part of God's opportunity against the Philistines [Judges 14:4]
- Three thousand men of his own people accepted Philistine rule and tied him up to hand him over [Judges 15:11–13]
- He killed more Philistines at his death than in his whole life [Judges 16:30]
- Decades later God promised to "save my people Israel from the hand of the Philistines" by David: the same words as Samson's "begin to save" [2 Samuel 3:18; Judges 13:5]
- What we learn: the strength wasn't a reward but a beginning for the people God was saving; Samson's choices still cost him his eyes; God finished through David what Samson began [Judges 13:5, 16:17–21; 2 Samuel 3:18, 8:1]
