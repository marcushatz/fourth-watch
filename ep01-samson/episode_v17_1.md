# What's the lesson of Samson's story?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 72
answer: Being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God. Samson's strength kept working while he lived by what was right in his own eyes, so when the LORD left him, he didn't even know. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work.

VISUAL: Fast montage, one feat per beat (0.8 s each): the lion torn apart barehanded in the vineyards of Timnah; 300 foxes with torches racing through grain; the jawbone raised at Lehi with a counter to 1,000; the gates of Gaza on his shoulders going up a hill. ## Story
VO: But here's what most people miss. His strength kept working no matter how he lived. [Judges 14:1–16:17]
VISUAL: Hold on Samson on the ridge, the gold mark lit; the camera eases in.
VO: He wanted a Philistine wife because "she is right in my eyes." It's the same phrase the book of Judges ends on: "Everyone did what was right in his own eyes." [Judges 14:3; 21:25]
VISUAL: At home in Zorah: Samson pointing down the road toward Timnah while his parents shake their heads; the two quotes stack on screen, RIGHT IN MY EYES / RIGHT IN HIS OWN EYES. Tags: JUDGES 14:3 · 21:25.
VO: He scooped honey out of a dead lion's carcass, and didn't tell his parents where it came from. [Judges 14:8–9]
VISUAL: Bees around the carcass; Samson scooping honey, then handing some to his parents without a word. Tag: JUDGES 14:9.
VO: He slept with a prostitute in Gaza, then walked off with the city gates. The strength stayed. [Judges 16:1–3]
VISUAL: Midnight in Gaza: Samson carrying the gate doors and posts up a hill; the gold mark holds. Tag: JUDGES 16:3.
VO: Delilah tried to hand him over three times, and he kept playing along. The strength stayed. [Judges 16:6–15]
VISUAL: Delilah's lamp-lit room; a 1-2-3 counter: bowstrings snap, new ropes snap, he pulls away the pin and the loom; the gold mark holds.
VO: Then he told her everything. She had his head shaved. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times…" "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:17–20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, a man with a razor, seven locks on the floor; the gold mark under the header goes out as the last lock falls. He wakes and stands, easy, unaware, as the Philistines fill the doorway.

## Turn
VO: Every time before, the strength had come. So he assumed it always would. [Judges 16:20]
VISUAL: Locked hold on Samson in the doorway; quick gold ghosts of the gates and the three traps flicker behind him and die.
VO: The Philistines took his eyes. And only then, blind, do we see him ask God for strength: "strengthen me only this once." [Judges 16:21, 16:28]
VISUAL: The prison mill, then the house of Dagon: blind Samson at the two middle pillars; the gold mark under the header relights as he prays; the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 16:28.

## Landing
VO: So what's the lesson of Samson? Being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work. [Judges 14:3, 16:20; 21:25]
VISUAL: Back to the opening frame: the mill in Gaza, now empty; first light falls across the floor where a few cut hairs lie. End card.

## Value
- "The Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him" before his feats [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- He wanted a wife because "she is right in my eyes," the same phrase the book ends on: "Everyone did what was right in his own eyes" [Judges 14:3; 21:25]
- He scooped honey out of a lion's carcass and hid where it came from; after a prostitute in Gaza he still carried off the city gates [Judges 14:8–9, 16:1–3]
- He woke expecting to "go out as at other times," but "he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- Only at the end, blind, do we see him ask God for strength: "strengthen me only this once" [Judges 16:28]
- What we learn: being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God [Judges 14:3, 16:20; 21:25]
