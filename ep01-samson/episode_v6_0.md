# Three things about Samson most people miss
series: MOST PEOPLE MISS
target_seconds: 38
answer: Samson chased what he wanted. God used it to begin saving Israel.

## Stakes
VO: One: he lost a riddle bet at his own wedding. He paid it with the clothes of thirty men he killed. [Judges 14:10–19]
ON-SCREEN: 1. THE WEDDING BET

## Story

ON-SCREEN: 2. HIS OWN PEOPLE

## Turn
VO: Three: his parents objected to him marrying a Philistine. But the Bible says the marriage "was from the LORD." [Judges 14:1–4]
ON-SCREEN: 3. "FROM THE LORD"
VERSE: "His father and mother did not know that it was from the LORD, for he was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines." — Judges 14:4 (ESV)
VO: God was "seeking an opportunity against the Philistines," who ruled Israel. [Judges 14:4]

## Landing
VO: Samson chased what he wanted. God used it to begin saving Israel. [Judges 13:5, 14:3–4]

## Value
- He lost a riddle bet at his own wedding and paid it with the clothes of thirty men he killed [Judges 14:10–19]
- 3,000 of his own people tied him up for the Philistines, saying "the Philistines are rulers over us" [Judges 15:9–13]
- His parents objected to the Philistine marriage, but the Bible says it "was from the LORD", who was "seeking an opportunity against the Philistines" [Judges 14:3–4]
- Before his birth, his mission was announced: he would "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [Judges 13:5]
