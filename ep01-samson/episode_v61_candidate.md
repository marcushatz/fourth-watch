# Why did Samson's strength keep working, even the night he slept with a prostitute?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 100
answer: His strength was never a reward or proof that God approved; it was God's strength, given for God's purpose.

## Hook
VO: Why did Samson's strength keep working, even the night he slept with a prostitute?
ON-SCREEN: WHY DID SAMSON'S STRENGTH KEEP WORKING, EVEN THE NIGHT HE SLEPT WITH A PROSTITUTE?

## Stakes
VO: In Gaza, that same night, men waited to kill him, and he pulled up the city gate, posts and all. [Judges 16:1-3]

## Story
VO: It looks like God didn't care how he lived. But Judges tells us whose strength it was. [Judges 14:6]

## Turn
VO: But notice: every time, the text says the same thing: "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him." [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
VO: Even when he demanded a Philistine wife, his parents "did not know that it was from the LORD, for he was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines." [Judges 14:1-4]
VO: So his strength was never a reward. It was for a job: he would "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines." [Judges 13:5]

## Landing
VO: And working was never proof that God approved. Notice what he said when Delilah called the fourth time: "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." [Judges 16:20]
VO: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VO: Blinded and grinding in a prison, Samson prayed, "O Lord GOD, please remember me." [Judges 16:21, 16:28]
VO: And Hebrews 11 still names him among those who, through faith, "were made strong out of weakness." [Hebrews 11:32-34]
VO: So why did Samson's strength keep working? Because it was God's, given for God's purpose, not a sign of God's approval. And even at his lowest, he could still call on God. [Judges 13:5, 16:20, 16:28]

## Question
VO: What stands out more to you about Samson: his failures, or his prayer at the end? [Judges 16:28]

## Value
- Each time his strength came, the text says "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him": it was never his own [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- Even his Philistine marriage was "from the LORD, for he was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines" [Judges 14:4]
- His strength had a job: to "begin to save Israel" [Judges 13:5]
- He "did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- Hebrews 11 still names him among those "made strong out of weakness" [Hebrews 11:32-34]

## Caption
Why did Samson's strength keep working? Read Judges 13:5, 14:4 and 16:20, then Hebrews 11:32-34.
