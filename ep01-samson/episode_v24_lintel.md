# Samson v24: Lintel blueprint (hook question -> story as evidence -> 3 points with rehooks -> CTA)

HOOK: Why did God keep giving Samson his strength while he kept sinning? He slept with a prostitute in Gaza, and that same night he carried off the city gates. Was God fine with how he lived? Let's let the Bible answer it. [Judges 16:1-3]

STORY: Three times, "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him," more than on any other judge. But he chose his wife because "she is right in my eyes." Then came Delilah. Four times she said the exact same words: "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" Three times, he broke free. Then he told her all his heart, and his hair, the sign he was set apart to God, was shaved. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [14:6, 14:19, 15:14; 14:3; 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:17, 16:19-20]

1. Ask what the strength was for. Before Samson was born, the angel said he would "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines." Even when he chose by his own eyes, the text says the LORD "was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines." The strength was for God's plan, not a reward for Samson's life. So can a gift keep working while God isn't pleased? [13:5; 14:4]

2. Listen to Jesus. He said many will say on that day, "Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name, and cast out demons in your name, and do many mighty works in your name?" And He will declare, "I never knew you." Mighty works and knowing God are not the same thing. Samson's strength kept working, right up until the morning the LORD had left him. So was that the end of Samson? [Matthew 7:22-23; Judges 16:20]

3. Look at how his story ends. Blind and a prisoner, he prayed, "O Lord GOD, please remember me and please strengthen me only this once." And Hebrews 11 doesn't praise his muscles. It names him among those who "through faith" "were made strong out of weakness." So why did God keep giving Samson strength? Because it was for God's purpose, never a verdict on his life. So don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts work. A gift from God is not a verdict from God. [Judges 16:21, 16:28; Hebrews 11:32-34]

CTA: Be honest: have you ever taken something going well as proof God was pleased with you? Tell us in the comments.
