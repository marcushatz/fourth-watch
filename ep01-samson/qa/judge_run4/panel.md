## Our scripts: what the judges said

### ep01-samson/episode_v64_candidate.md: GOOD ENOUGH. Note: question pull 3 is below every Lintel hit's (4)

Weakest line, per judge:
- "Even when he demanded a Philistine wife, his parents "did not know that it was from the LORD, for he was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines.""
- "So why did Samson's strength keep working?"
- "And even at his lowest, he could still call on God."

The one fix, per judge:
- Cut the Philistine-wife detour and state the 'oh' plainly: his strength was equipment for a job, not a grade on his life. Then build the reveal around 'he did not know the LORD had left him.'
- Land the final answer on the single sharpest idea, that power still working is not proof of God's approval, tied to 'he did not know that the LORD had left him', instead of the generic 'it came from God, for God's purpose' plus a second idea.
- Land the answer in one sharp line (his strength proved God's purpose, not God's approval) and drop the second tacked-on moral so there is a single takeaway.

AI judges score scripts, not audiences. They flag weak hooks, thin payoffs, little new value and dead lines; they can't measure retention or sharing (Round 17).
