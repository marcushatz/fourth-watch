## Our scripts: what the judges said

### ep02-jonah/episode_v5_draft.md: GOOD ENOUGH

Weakest line, per judge:
- "Historically, Nineveh was one of Assyria's great cities."
- "Historically, Nineveh was one of Assyria's great cities."
- "Historically, Nineveh was one of Assyria's great cities."

The one fix, per judge:
- Replace the history line with why it matters (Nineveh was the capital of Assyria, Israel's most brutal enemy) so the viewer feels why Jonah would rather die than watch God spare it.
- Say why Nineveh mattered (the capital-class city of Israel's brutal enemy Assyria) so the reveal that Jonah ran because God might spare them hits much harder.
- Give the stakes up front: Assyria was Israel's brutal enemy and would later wipe out the northern kingdom. Then Jonah's 'I knew you are gracious' lands as a real shock instead of a mild surprise.

### ep01-samson/episode_v61_candidate.md: GOOD ENOUGH

- "But Judges tells us whose strength it was."
- "But Judges tells us whose strength it was."
- "But Judges tells us whose strength it was."

The one fix, per judge:
- Add one line of setup before the Delilah quote (she had his hair cut while he slept) so 'as at other times' lands on one listen, and soften 'every time' since the Gaza gate verse never mentions the Spirit.
