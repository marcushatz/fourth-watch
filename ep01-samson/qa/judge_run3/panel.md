## Our scripts: what the judges said

### ep01-samson/episode_v63_candidate.md: GOOD ENOUGH. Note: question pull 3 is below every Lintel hit's (4)

Weakest line, per judge:
- "And the text never treats his strength working as a sign that God approved."
- "So why did Samson's strength keep working?"
- "Because it came from God, for God's purpose, and the text never presents it as a reward or a sign of approval."

The one fix, per judge:
- Replace the closing recap with one crisp, carryable line (for example: God using you is not God approving of you), placed before the final question.
- Replace the recap and the generic failures-or-prayer question with one sharp closing idea (God's power working through someone is not proof God approves of them) and a question tied directly to that idea.
- Cut the closing recap, which repeats a point already made twice. Pick one takeaway (power is not approval) and end on a sharper question tied to it, not the generic failures-or-prayer choice.

AI judges score scripts, not audiences. They flag weak hooks, thin payoffs, little new value and dead lines; they can't measure retention or sharing (Round 17).
