**1. Pillar line: VERIFIED**
- 16:29: "grasped the two middle pillars… and he leaned his weight against them."
- 16:30: "Then he bowed with all his strength, and the house fell upon the lords and upon all the people who were in it."
- The verbs "leaned" and "bowed" match, and so does the order. The line leaves out "grasped" and "Let me die with the Philistines" (said between leaning and bowing), but nothing is put out of order.
- "Philistine lords" is supported by 16:27 ("All the lords of the Philistines were there").
- "Everyone inside" fairly renders "all the people who were in it."
- "Samson died with them" is supported by 16:30: "the dead whom he killed at his death."
- "Building" for "house" follows the house rule.

**2. The join: VERIFIED as an application, with one condition**
- 16:20 "as at other times" is exact.
