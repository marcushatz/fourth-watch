## Our scripts: what the judges said

### ep02-jonah/episode_v5_draft.md: GOOD ENOUGH. Note: question pull 3 is below every Lintel hit's (4)

Weakest line, per judge:
- "Historically, Nineveh was one of Assyria's great cities."
- "So the reason Jonah gives isn't fear."
- "Historically, Nineveh was one of Assyria's great cities."

The one fix, per judge:
- Say plainly up front that Assyria was Israel's brutal enemy, so Jonah's anger at God's mercy lands with full force when his real reason is revealed.
- Say early that Nineveh was the capital-city power of Assyria, Israel's brutal enemy, so the reveal that Jonah ran because God is merciful hits with full force.
- Say early that Nineveh was the capital of Israel's most brutal enemy, so the reveal that Jonah ran because God might forgive them hits with full force.

### ep01-samson/episode_v62_candidate.md: GOOD ENOUGH. Note: question pull 2 is below every Lintel hit's (4)

Weakest line, per judge:
- "And when it worked, that never meant God approved."
- "And when it worked, that never meant God approved."
- "And when it worked, that never meant God approved."

The one fix, per judge:
- Sharpen the closing question so it ties back to the hook (what it means when God still works through someone living badly) instead of a generic failures-or-prayer either/or.
- Make the ending question test the takeaway (can someone be powerfully used by God without God approving of how they live?) instead of the generic failures-or-prayer choice, and consider framing the hook around the more widely asked 'why did God keep using Samson?'
- Replace the generic closing question with one that tests the payoff (e.g. 'Have you ever taken something working as proof God was fine with how you were living?') so the ending lands the 'gift is not approval' idea instead of drifting to failures vs prayer.
