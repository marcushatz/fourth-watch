# Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 85
answer: Even a man that strong could only begin to save. Jesus came to save his people from their sins, and on the cross he said, "It is finished."

## Hook
VO: Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
ON-SCREEN: WHY IS SAMSON IN THE BIBLE?
VISUAL: High three-quarter wide of the ridge at Lehi at dawn: Samson standing alone with a jawbone, long braids, the Philistine camp soft in the distance; slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: Before he was born, an angel told his mother her son would "begin to save Israel" from the Philistines. Begin. Hold on to that word. [Judges 13:3–5]
VISUAL: ZORAH: the angel of the LORD appearing to Samson's mother; the word BEGIN lights gold in the narration and stays as a small gold word under the header for the rest of the story.

## Story
VO: But the man born to fight the Philistines wanted to marry one: "Get her for me, for she is right in my eyes." [Judges 14:1–3]
VISUAL: At home in Zorah: Samson, a young man, pointing down the road toward Timnah while his father and mother shake their heads. Tag: JUDGES 14:3.
VO: And the people he was born to save tied him up and brought him to the Philistines. [Judges 15:11–14]
VISUAL: The cleft of the rock of Etam: men of Judah binding Samson with two new ropes, then leading him down toward Lehi as the Philistines come shouting. Tag: 3,000 MEN OF JUDAH · JUDGES 15:11.
VO: The ropes melted off his hands, and he struck down a thousand men with a donkey's jawbone. That was the beginning. [Judges 15:14–15]
VISUAL: Back to the opening ridge at Lehi: gold rushes in, the ropes flare like flax and fall away, the jawbone raised; a counter ticks to 1,000 (no gore). Tag: JUDGES 15:15.
VO: Then came Delilah. She tried to hand him over three times. He told her his secret anyway. [Judges 16:4–17]
VISUAL: Delilah's lamp-lit room; a 1-2-3 counter flicks past; Samson leaning in to tell her.
VO: She had his head shaved. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times…" "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:19–20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, a man with a razor, seven locks on the floor; the faint gold that has stayed on Samson goes out as the last lock falls. He wakes and stands, easy, unaware, as the Philistines fill the doorway.
VO: Blinded, he was brought out to entertain the crowd. His last prayer was for revenge, and the house came down on them all. [Judges 16:21–30]
VISUAL: The house of Dagon: blind Samson led by a young man between laughing Philistines; his hands on the two middle pillars; the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 16:28–30.
VO: He began to save Israel. He couldn't finish. [Judges 13:5, 16:30–31; 1 Samuel 7:13; 2 Samuel 3:18]
VISUAL: Dust settling over the ruins; the small gold BEGIN under the header, alone.

## Turn
VO: More than a thousand years later, an angel announced another birth: "he will save his people from their sins." Not begin. Save. [Matthew 1:20–21]
VISUAL: Light sweep (time jump). Joseph asleep; an angel; the gold BEGIN under the header becomes SAVE. Tag: MATTHEW 1:21.
VO: And his story will sound familiar. He was blindfolded and mocked. His own people's leaders bound him and handed him over. [Luke 22:63–64; Matthew 27:1–2]
VISUAL: Jesus seated with a cloth over his eyes, guards mocking (the same composition as blind Samson at Dagon); then bound and led by the chief priests and elders to Pilate's door (the same composition as Etam). Tags: LUKE 22:64 · MATTHEW 27:2.
VO: Samson's ropes melted off his hands. Jesus let his hold. [Judges 15:14; Matthew 26:53, 27:2]
VISUAL: Split for one beat: top, Samson's ropes flaring and falling at Lehi; bottom, Jesus' bound hands, still. Tag: "MORE THAN TWELVE LEGIONS OF ANGELS" · MATTHEW 26:53.
VO: Samson prayed for revenge. On the cross, Jesus prayed, "Father, forgive them." [Judges 16:28; Luke 23:34]
VERSE: "Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do." — Luke 23:34 (ESV)
VISUAL: A dark hill at noon, three crosses small against the sky; the verse card holds.

## Landing
VO: So why is Samson in the Bible? God raised him to "begin to save Israel." And even a man that strong could only begin. [Judges 13:5, 16:30]
VISUAL: Back to the opening ridge at Lehi; Samson small against the dawn; BEGIN under the header.
VO: Jesus came to save his people from their sins. And on the cross he said, "It is finished." [Matthew 1:21; John 19:30]
VISUAL: First light breaks gold across the ridge; BEGIN becomes FINISHED under the header. End card.

## Value
- The angel said Samson would "begin to save Israel" from the Philistines [Judges 13:5]
- The man born to fight the Philistines wanted to marry one, and the people he was born to save tied him up and brought him to the Philistines [Judges 13:5, 14:1–3, 15:11–14]
- He woke up saying "I will go out as at other times," "but he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- His last prayer was for revenge "for my two eyes" [Judges 16:28]
- Samson's ropes melted off his hands; Jesus, who could have appealed for "more than twelve legions of angels," let his hold, and prayed "Father, forgive them" [Judges 15:14; Matthew 26:53; Luke 23:34]
- What we learn: even a man that strong could only begin; Jesus came to save his people from their sins, and said "It is finished" [Judges 13:5; Matthew 1:21; John 19:30]
