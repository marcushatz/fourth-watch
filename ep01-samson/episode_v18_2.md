# What was Samson's real weakness?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 82
answer: He let his eyes lead and judged where he stood with God by whether his strength still worked. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work.

## Hook
VO: What was Samson's real weakness?
ON-SCREEN: WHAT WAS SAMSON'S REAL WEAKNESS?
VISUAL: Samson blind at the mill in Gaza, bronze shackles, hair beginning to grow back; slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: Only three men in the Bible had the Spirit of the LORD "rush upon" them: Samson, Saul and David. And one of them never noticed when it left. [Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 10:10, 16:13; Judges 16:20]
VISUAL: Three silhouettes side by side, each with a gold rush: SAMSON · SAUL · DAVID. On screen: HEBREW TSALACH: USED OF THE SPIRIT ONLY FOR THESE THREE.
VO: Samson had it three times, more than any other judge. [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14; cf. 3:10, 6:34, 11:29]
VISUAL: Fast montage: the lion torn apart barehanded; thirty garments at Ashkelon; the ropes melting at Lehi and the jawbone raised. Gold rushes in on each; a small gold strength mark appears under the header and stays lit. No gore.

## Story
VO: But watch his eyes. "He saw one of the daughters of the Philistines": "Get her for me, for she is right in my eyes." [Judges 14:1–3]
VISUAL: At home in Zorah: Samson pointing down the road toward Timnah while his parents shake their heads. On screen: HEBREW YASHRAH B'EINAI, "RIGHT IN MY EYES"; beneath it, smaller: THE LAST LINE OF JUDGES: "EVERYONE DID WHAT WAS RIGHT IN HIS OWN EYES" (21:25). Tag: JUDGES 14:3.
VO: God used him anyway. That's the trap: being used by God can feel like being approved by God. [Judges 14:4]
VISUAL: Hold on Samson's face turned toward Timnah; the gold mark still lit. Tag: JUDGES 14:4.
VO: In Gaza, "he saw a prostitute." That same night, his strength still worked. He carried off the city gates. [Judges 16:1–3]
VISUAL: Midnight in Gaza: Samson carrying the gate doors and posts up a hill; the gold mark holds. Tag: JUDGES 16:3.
VO: Then Delilah had his head shaved, the sign he was set apart to God. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times…" "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." The next verse: the Philistines "gouged out his eyes." [Judges 13:5, 16:19–21]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, seven locks on the floor; the gold mark goes out as the last lock falls. He wakes and stands, easy, unaware, as the Philistines fill the doorway; cut to black on "gouged".
VO: Even his last prayer was about his eyes: "that I may be avenged on the Philistines for my two eyes." [Judges 16:28]
VISUAL: The house of Dagon: blind Samson at the two middle pillars, a young man at his side; the gold mark relights as he prays; the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 16:28.

## Turn
VO: Now David. When the prophet Nathan confronted him, it was over a woman David "saw from the roof." [2 Samuel 11:2, 12:7]
VISUAL: The three silhouettes return; David on a palace roof at evening, looking down. Tag: 2 SAMUEL 11:2.
VO: Same Spirit. Same eyes. But David prayed: "take not your Holy Spirit from me." [Psalm 51 title, 51:11]
VISUAL: David kneeling, the gold holding. Tag: PSALM 51:11.
VO: Saul lost it. Samson never noticed. Only David is heard begging God not to take it away. [1 Samuel 16:14; Judges 16:20; Psalm 51:11]
VISUAL: Locked hold on the three silhouettes: SAUL (gold drained) · SAMSON (dark, eyes closed, small caption 16:20) · DAVID (lit).

## Landing
VO: So what was Samson's real weakness? He let his eyes lead, and judged where he stood with God by whether his strength still worked. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work. [Judges 14:3, 16:1–3, 16:20]
VISUAL: Back to the opening frame: the mill in Gaza, now empty, in cold light. End card.

## Value
- The Spirit of the LORD "rushed upon" Samson three times, more often than the Spirit came on any other judge [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14; 3:10, 6:34, 11:29]
- The Bible says the Spirit "rushed upon" only three men: Samson, Saul and David [Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 10:10, 16:13]
- "She is right in my eyes," and the same idea closes the book: "Everyone did what was right in his own eyes" [Judges 14:3; 17:6; 21:25]
- The night of the Gaza prostitute, his strength still carried off the city gates [Judges 16:1–3]
- "He did not know that the LORD had left him"; the next verse, they "gouged out his eyes"; his last prayer was "for my two eyes" [Judges 16:20–21, 16:28]
- David's fall began with his eyes too: he "saw from the roof a woman bathing"; but he prayed "take not your Holy Spirit from me" [2 Samuel 11:2; Psalm 51:11]
- What we learn: don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work [Judges 16:20]
