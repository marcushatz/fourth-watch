Device notes: gold light only for what God did (the Spirit at Lehi, the vineyards, the angel, the prayer and the house falling); ember for Samson's own choices (Zorah, Delilah, the prison). The BEGIN … ? timeline appears at shot 11 and stays under the header through shot 13, then returns at shot 20, where the ? becomes DAVID. Captions are off: the narration at the top is the only text, and it hides while a verse card is up.

Camera rubric: every move starts on its word, one main move per shot within the limits, no two neighbours share size and angle, God's light stays upper left and the Philistines stay right, faces sit in the 22–60% band, the turn (shot 11) is a locked hold, shake only on the three impacts (give, apart/thousand, answered), and the last shot returns to frame one. Pacing: cuts every 1.1–5.3 s, fastest at the hook and the lion and jawbone, stillest at the turn.
