# What's the lesson of Samson's story?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 70
answer: Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work.

## Hook
VO: What's the lesson of Samson's story?
ON-SCREEN: WHAT'S THE LESSON OF SAMSON?
VISUAL: Samson blind at the mill in Gaza, bronze shackles, hair beginning to grow back; slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: Samson is known as the strongest man in the Bible. [Judges 14:6, 15:4–5, 15:15, 16:3]
VISUAL: Fast montage, one feat per beat (0.8 s each): the lion torn apart barehanded in the vineyards of Timnah; 300 foxes with torches racing through grain; the jawbone raised at Lehi with a counter to 1,000; the gates of Gaza on his shoulders going up a hill. No gore. Tags flick past: JUDGES 14:6 · 15:4 · 15:15 · 16:3.
VO: And it came from God: "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him." [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
VISUAL: Gold rushes down from the upper left onto Samson on the ridge at Lehi; a small gold strength mark appears under the header and stays lit.

## Story
VO: Here's what most people miss: his strength kept working while he lived by what was right in his own eyes. [Judges 14:3, 16:1–3, 16:6–17]
VISUAL: The camera pushes past Samson on the ridge toward Timnah below; the gold mark stays lit.
VO: He chose a wife because "she is right in my eyes." [Judges 14:3]
VISUAL: At home in Zorah: Samson pointing down the road toward Timnah while his parents shake their heads. On-screen card beneath: RIGHT IN MY EYES (14:3) · "EVERYONE DID WHAT WAS RIGHT IN HIS OWN EYES" — THE LAST LINE OF JUDGES (21:25).
VO: He scooped honey out of a lion's carcass, which made a person unclean, and hid where it came from. [Judges 14:8–9; Leviticus 11:27]
VISUAL: Bees around the carcass; Samson scooping honey, then handing some to his parents without a word. Tag: JUDGES 14:9.
VO: He slept with a prostitute in Gaza, then walked off with the city gates. The strength stayed. [Judges 16:1–3]
VISUAL: Midnight in Gaza: Samson carrying the gate doors and posts up a hill; the gold mark holds. Tag: JUDGES 16:3.
VO: Three times Delilah showed him exactly what she was doing. Three times the strength bailed him out. [Judges 16:6–15]
VISUAL: Delilah's lamp-lit room; a 1-2-3 counter: bowstrings snap, new ropes snap, he pulls away the pin and the loom; each time she shouts and the Philistines wait in the inner room; the gold mark holds.
VO: So he told her the sign God set on him before he was born: no razor on his head. [Judges 13:5, 16:17]
VISUAL: Samson leaning in to tell her; a flash back to Zorah, the angel and his mother, the words NO RAZOR in gold. Tag: JUDGES 13:5 · 16:17.
VO: She had his head shaved. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times…" "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:19–20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, a man with a razor, seven locks on the floor; the gold mark under the header goes out as the last lock falls. He wakes and stands, easy, unaware, as the Philistines fill the doorway.

## Turn
VO: Every time before, the strength had come. So he assumed it would again. [Judges 16:9–14, 16:20]
VISUAL: Locked hold on Samson in the doorway; quick gold ghosts of the gates and the three traps flicker behind him and die.
VO: Blind, in a Philistine prison, only then do we see him ask God for strength: "strengthen me only this once." [Judges 16:21, 16:28]
VISUAL: The prison mill, then the house of Dagon: blind Samson at the two middle pillars; the gold mark relights as he prays; the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 16:28.

## Landing
VO: So what's the lesson of Samson? Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work. [Judges 14:3, 16:20; 21:25]
VISUAL: Back to the opening frame: the mill in Gaza, now empty, in cold light; a few cut hairs on the floor. End card.

## Value
- "The Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him" before his feats [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- He chose a wife because "she is right in my eyes," which echoes the last line of Judges: "Everyone did what was right in his own eyes" [Judges 14:3; 17:6; 21:25]
- He scooped honey out of a lion's carcass, which made a person unclean, and hid where it came from [Judges 14:8–9; Leviticus 11:27]
- After a prostitute in Gaza he still carried off the city gates [Judges 16:1–3]
- He told Delilah the sign God set on him before birth, no razor; "he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 13:5, 16:17–20]
- What we learn: don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work [Judges 14:3, 16:20; 21:25]

And yes, Hebrews 11:32 still lists him among the faithful. When he cried out to God, God answered (Judges 15:18–19; 16:28–30).
