### What the viewer learns

1. Before his birth, God gave him a mission, to "begin to save Israel" from the Philistines, and he married one of them (Judges 13:3–5, 14:1–4).
2. He killed thirty men of Ashkelon for their clothes to pay off a riddle bet he lost at his own wedding (Judges 14:12–19).
3. His wife was given to another man, so he burned Philistine fields with 300 foxes (Judges 14:20, 15:1–5).
4. The Bible says the marriage that started it all "was from the LORD", who was "seeking an opportunity against the Philistines" (Judges 14:4).
5. His own people had accepted Philistine rule ("Do you not know that the Philistines are rulers over us?"), so 3,000 of them tied him up for the Philistines (Judges 15:9–13).
6. Blinded, he prayed "please remember me" in the same breath as revenge "for my two eyes"; God answered, and the building fell "upon the lords and upon all the people" (Judges 16:21–30).
