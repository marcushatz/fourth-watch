# If God is still using you, does that mean He's okay with how you're living?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 140
answer: No. Gifts are for others, and a gift is never proof He approves. A gift from God is not a verdict from God.

## Hook
VO: If God is still using you, does that mean He's okay with how you're living?
ON-SCREEN: IF GOD STILL USES YOU, IS HE OKAY WITH HOW YOU LIVE?

## Stakes
VO: Samson slept with a prostitute in Gaza, and that same night he pulled the city gates out of the ground, posts and all. [Judges 16:1-3]
VO: Let's let the Bible answer it.

## Story
VO: The Spirit of the LORD moved on him four times, more than on any other judge. [Judges 13:25, 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
VO: Then came Delilah. [Judges 16:4]
VO: Four times, she said the exact same words: "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
VO: Three times, he broke free. [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:14]
VO: Then he told her all his heart, and his hair, the sign he was set apart to God, was shaved. [Judges 13:5, 16:17, 16:19]
VO: The fourth time, he woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." [Judges 16:20]
VO: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)

## Turn
VO: So what does that mean for you? Three things. [Judges 13:5, 16:28]
VO: One: God gives gifts to help others, not because we earned them. [Judges 13:5]
VO: Before Samson was born, the angel said he would "begin to save Israel." His strength was for his people, not for him. [Judges 13:5]
VO: Two: a working gift doesn't prove you're right with God. [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20]
VO: Samson's strength worked the same night he sinned in Gaza. And when it was gone, he didn't even know. [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20]

## Landing
VO: Three: when the gift is gone, you can still call on God. [Judges 16:21-30]
VO: Blind and a prisoner, he prayed, "O Lord GOD, please remember me and please strengthen me only this once." [Judges 16:21, 16:28]
VO: The LORD had left him, but when Samson called, God still answered: the house of Dagon fell. [Judges 16:20, 16:28-30]
VO: And Hebrews 11 names him among those who "through faith" "were made strong out of weakness." [Hebrews 11:32-34]
VO: So does God using you mean He's okay with how you live? No. Gifts are for others, and a gift is never proof He approves. [Judges 13:5; Matthew 7:22-23]
VO: A gift from God is not a verdict from God.
VO: Which hit you harder: that Hebrews 11 names him among the faithful, or that God still answered him? Tell us in the comments. [Hebrews 11:32; Judges 16:28-30]

## Value
- His strength came from the LORD; the hair was the sign of his consecration [Judges 14:6, 16:17, 16:20, 16:28]
- Four times the Spirit of the LORD moved on him ("began to stir him" once, "rushed upon him" three times), more than on any other judge; and four times Delilah said the exact same words. The fourth time, the LORD had left him [Judges 13:25, 14:6, 14:19, 15:14; 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
- His strength was given for others: before he was born, he would "begin to save Israel" [Judges 13:5]
- His strength worked the same night he sinned in Gaza, and when it was gone he "did not know"; yet when he called, God still answered [Judges 16:1-3, 16:20, 16:28-30]
- Hebrews 11 names Samson among those who "through faith" "were made strong out of weakness" [Hebrews 11:32-34]
