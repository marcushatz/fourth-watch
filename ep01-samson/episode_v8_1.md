# If God knew Samson would fail, why give him such great strength?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 110
answer: The strength wasn't for Samson's glory. It was God's strength, for God's rescue.

## Value
- Three times the Bible says "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him": his strength came from God [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- After his head was shaved, he "did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:19–20]
- Before his birth, God said he would "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [Judges 13:5]
- His own people weren't fighting back: "the Philistines are rulers over us" [Judges 15:11]
- Even the marriage his parents objected to "was from the LORD" [Judges 14:4]
- He killed more Philistines at his death than in his whole life, and King David later "defeated the Philistines and subdued them" [Judges 16:30; 2 Samuel 8:1]
- What we learn: God can work through our failures, but they still cost (he lost his eyes and his freedom); and failure isn't the last word, since the New Testament lists him among those "commended through their faith" [Judges 14:3–4, 16:21, 16:28–30; Hebrews 11:32, 11:39]
