# What's the lesson of Samson's story?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 105
answer: Being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God: Samson's strength stayed every time, so when the LORD left him, he didn't even notice. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work.

## Hook
VO: What's the lesson of Samson's story?
ON-SCREEN: WHAT'S THE LESSON OF SAMSON?
VISUAL: Samson blind at the mill in Gaza, bronze shackles, hair beginning to grow back; slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: Before he was born, an angel said he'd be set apart to God: no razor on his head, and he would "begin to save Israel" from the Philistines. [Judges 13:3–5]
VISUAL: ZORAH: the angel of the LORD appearing to Samson's mother; a small gold glow settles on the child.
VO: Keep an eye on his strength.
VISUAL: A small gold strength mark appears under the header and stays lit through every scene that follows.

## Story
VO: The Spirit of the LORD rushed on him, and he tore a lion apart with his bare hands. [Judges 14:5–6]
VISUAL: The vineyards of Timnah: a young lion springs; gold rushes in; Samson catches it barehanded (no gore). Tag: JUDGES 14:6.
VO: Later he scooped honey out of its carcass, and hid where it came from. His strength stayed. [Judges 14:8–9]
VISUAL: Bees around the lion's carcass; Samson scooping honey into his hands, then handing some to his parents without a word; the gold mark holds. Tag: JUDGES 14:9.
VO: He married a Philistine. When the wedding guests cheated to solve his riddle about that honey, he struck down thirty Philistines to pay the bet. [Judges 14:1–19]
VISUAL: The wedding feast; the riddle on screen, "OUT OF THE STRONG CAME SOMETHING SWEET"; then thirty garments dropped at the guests' feet (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 14:14, 14:19.
VO: They gave his wife away, so he burned their fields with three hundred foxes. They burned her and her father, and he struck back. [Judges 15:1–8]
VISUAL: Foxes paired tail to tail with torches racing through standing grain; the fields catch. Tag: 300 FOXES · JUDGES 15:4.
VO: His own people tied him up to hand him over. The ropes melted off his hands, and he struck down a thousand men with a donkey's jawbone. [Judges 15:9–15]
VISUAL: Etam, men of Judah binding him; then Lehi: the bonds melt like flax in fire, jawbone raised, a counter to 1,000 (no gore). Tag: JUDGES 15:15.
VO: Near death from thirst, he cried out to God, and God split open a spring. [Judges 15:18–19]
VISUAL: Samson collapsed on the dry hillside, calling up; water bursts from a hollow in the rock. Tag: JUDGES 15:19.
VO: In Gaza he slept with a prostitute, then pulled up the city gates and carried them up a hill. His strength stayed. [Judges 16:1–3]
VISUAL: Midnight in Gaza: Samson walking out of the city with the gate doors and posts on his shoulders, climbing a hill; the gold mark holds. Tag: JUDGES 16:3.
VO: Then came Delilah. Three times she set a trap and shouted, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" Three times his strength stayed. Then he told her everything. [Judges 16:4–17]
VISUAL: Delilah's lamp-lit room; a 1-2-3 counter: bowstrings snap, new ropes snap, he pulls away the pin and the loom; the gold mark holds; then Samson leaning in to tell her.
VO: She had his head shaved. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times…" "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:19–20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, a man with a razor, seven locks on the floor; the gold mark under the header goes out as the last lock falls. He wakes and stands, easy, unaware, as the Philistines fill the doorway.
VO: The Philistines gouged out his eyes and set him grinding at a mill. Blind, he prayed, "strengthen me only this once," and brought the house down. He died with them. [Judges 16:21–30]
VISUAL: The prison mill (no gore); then the house of Dagon: blind Samson led by a young man, laughing Philistines, a faint gold returns on his hands at the two middle pillars as he prays; the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 16:30.

## Turn
VO: Now read that line again: "he did not know." Every time before, his strength had stayed. So when the LORD left, Samson didn't even notice. [Judges 14:9, 16:3, 16:9–14, 16:20]
VISUAL: Locked hold on the 16:20 card; behind it, quick ghosts of the honey, the gates and the three traps, each with the gold mark lit, then the dark room at Delilah's.

## Landing
VO: So what's the lesson of Samson? Being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God. Don't judge where you stand with God by how well your gifts still work. [Judges 14:3, 16:20–21; 21:25]
VISUAL: Back to the opening frame: the mill in Gaza, now empty; first light falls across the floor where a few cut hairs lie. End card.

## Value
- The angel said Samson would "begin to save Israel" [Judges 13:5]
- He scooped honey out of the lion's carcass and hid where it came from; his riddle came from it [Judges 14:8–9, 14:14]
- His own people tied him up to hand him over [Judges 15:11–13]
- In Gaza he slept with a prostitute, then carried the city gates up a hill, and his strength stayed [Judges 16:1–3]
- He woke up expecting to "go out as at other times," but "he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- What we learn: being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God; don't judge where you stand by how well your gifts still work [Judges 14:3, 16:20–21; 21:25]
