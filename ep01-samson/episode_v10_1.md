# If God knew Samson would throw it all away, why did He give him so much strength?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 88
answer: God didn't give Samson strength because he deserved it. He gave it because Israel needed saving.

## Landing

## Value
- Before he was born, God said Samson would only "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [Judges 13:5]
- His strength came when "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him", not from himself [Judges 14:6, 15:14]
- Three thousand men of his own people accepted Philistine rule ("the Philistines are rulers over us") and tied him up to hand him over [Judges 15:11–13]
- The marriage his parents opposed "was from the LORD, for he was seeking an opportunity against the Philistines" [Judges 14:4]
- He killed more Philistines at his death than in his whole life, and decades later King David "defeated the Philistines and subdued them": Samson only began what God finished [Judges 16:30; 2 Samuel 8:1]
- What we learn: the strength was for the people God was saving, not a reward; Samson's choices still cost him his eyes; and the New Testament still lists him among the faithful [Judges 13:5, 16:17–21; Hebrews 11:32, 11:39]
