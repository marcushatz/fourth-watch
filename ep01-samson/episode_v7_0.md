# If God knew Samson would fail, why make him the strongest man in the Bible?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 95
answer: The strength wasn't for Samson's glory. It was God's strength, for God's rescue.

## Value
- Three times the Bible says "the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him": his strength came from God [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- When he gave away his secret, "the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- Before his birth, God said he would only "begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines" [Judges 13:5]
- His own people had stopped resisting: "the Philistines are rulers over us" [Judges 15:11]
- Even the marriage his parents objected to "was from the LORD" [Judges 14:4]
- His death killed more Philistines than his whole life, and King David later "defeated the Philistines and subdued them" [Judges 16:30; 2 Samuel 8:1]
