# Was Samson a hero or a villain?
series: HERO OR VILLAIN?
target_seconds: 48

## Hook
VO: Was Samson a hero or a villain?
ON-SCREEN: WAS SAMSON A HERO OR A VILLAIN?
VISUAL: Blind Samson between two huge stone pillars, a hand on each; a crack climbs one pillar; crowds on the roof above.

## Stakes
VO: Most remember his strength. Few remember his last prayer: revenge. [Judges 16:3, 16:28]
VISUAL: At midnight, Samson carries the city gate of Gaza on his shoulders: both doors, both posts and the bar. Then darkness and a thin beam on his lifted face.

## Story
VO: Set apart to God from the womb: no razor on his head. [Judges 13:5]
VISUAL: His mother holds a newborn in a column of gold light; long uncut hair is the sign. The scale appears at the top of the frame; a gold lock of hair drops on the left pan.
VO: But he went to a prostitute, [Judges 16:1]
VISUAL: Night in Gaza; Samson walks toward a lamp-lit doorway; the door closes. An ember weight drops on the right pan.
VO: then told Delilah a razor would take his strength. [Judges 16:17]
VISUAL: Lamp-lit room; Delilah in the foreground, Samson speaking to her. A razor drops on the right pan.
VO: His head was shaved, and the Lord had left him. [Judges 16:19–20]
VISUAL: He sleeps on Delilah's knees; a man shaves off seven locks; the spotlight above him fades to dark. The scale tips hard right.
VO: They gouged out his eyes. But his hair began to grow again. [Judges 16:21–22]
VISUAL: Cut to black on "eyes". Then Samson, eyes closed, in bronze shackles, grinds at a mill; a few short locks catch the light.

## Turn
VO: Blind, surrounded by enemies, he prayed to be avenged for his two eyes. [Judges 16:25–28]
VISUAL: A huge house full of men and women, about 3,000 on the roof; a young man leads Samson by the hand to the two middle pillars. Then close on his face lifted upward, one hand on each pillar; the camera holds completely still.
VO: "Let me die with the Philistines." [Judges 16:30]
VERSE: "Let me die with the Philistines." — Judges 16:30 (ESV)
VISUAL: The verse card over his hands on the pillars.
VO: He bowed with all his strength, and the house fell. [Judges 16:30]
VISUAL: He leans and bows; the pillars give; the roof comes down; dust fills the frame.
VO: The New Testament still lists him among people of faith. [Hebrews 11:32]
VISUAL: First light: the dust settles into dawn over the ruins; the camera rises into the warm sky.

## Landing
VO: Hero or villain? The text shows faith and failure. Which do you see?
VISUAL: The scale settles level. Back to the opening frame: Samson between the two pillars.
