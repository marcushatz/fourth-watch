# What's the lesson of Samson's story?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 85
answer: Being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God: Samson's strength never left him, so when the LORD left him, he didn't even notice.

## Hook
VO: What's the lesson of Samson's story?
ON-SCREEN: WHAT'S THE LESSON OF SAMSON?
VISUAL: Samson blind at the mill in Gaza, bronze shackles, hair beginning to grow back; slow push-in. Header (FOURTH WATCH · ASK THE TEXT · SAMSON) stays for the whole reel; narration builds word by word at the top.

## Stakes
VO: The Philistines were ruling Israel when an angel told a woman who had no children: your son will "begin to save Israel." No razor on his head. [Judges 13:1–5]
VISUAL: ZORAH: the angel of the LORD appearing to Samson's mother; a small gold glow comes to rest on the baby she later holds.

## Story
VO: He grew up and demanded a Philistine wife. On the way, the Spirit of the LORD rushed on him, and he tore a lion apart with his bare hands. [Judges 14:1–6]
VISUAL: The vineyards of Timnah: a young lion springs; gold rushes in; Samson catches it barehanded (no gore). Tag: JUDGES 14:6.
VO: When the wedding guests cheated on his riddle, he struck down thirty Philistines to pay the bet. [Judges 14:12–19]
VISUAL: Thirty festal garments dropped at the guests' feet (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 14:19.
VO: When they gave his wife to another man, he burned their fields with three hundred foxes. So they burned her and her father, and he struck back. [Judges 15:1–8]
VISUAL: Foxes paired tail to tail with torches racing through standing grain; the fields catch. Tag: 300 FOXES · JUDGES 15:4.
VO: The Philistines came for him, and his own people tied him up to hand him over. The ropes melted off his hands, and he struck down a thousand men with a donkey's jawbone. [Judges 15:11–15]
VISUAL: Etam, men of Judah binding him; then Lehi: the bonds melt like flax in fire, jawbone raised, a counter to 1,000 (no gore). Tag: JUDGES 15:15.
VO: Near death from thirst, he cried out to God, and God split open a spring. [Judges 15:18–19]
VISUAL: Samson collapsed on the dry hillside, calling up; water bursts from a hollow in the rock. Tag: JUDGES 15:19.
VO: In Gaza he slept with a prostitute, then pulled up the city gates, posts and all, and carried them up a hill. [Judges 16:1–3]
VISUAL: Midnight in Gaza: Samson walking out of the city with the gate doors and posts on his shoulders, climbing a hill. Tag: JUDGES 16:3.
VO: Then came Delilah. Each Philistine lord offered her eleven hundred pieces of silver. Three times she tried. Then he told her everything. [Judges 16:4–17]
VISUAL: Delilah's lamp-lit room; a 1-2-3 counter flicks past; Samson leaning in to tell her.
VO: She had his head shaved. He woke up and said, "I will go out as at other times…" "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." [Judges 16:19–20]
VERSE: "But he did not know that the LORD had left him." — Judges 16:20 (ESV)
VISUAL: Asleep on Delilah's knees, a man with a razor, seven locks on the floor; the gold on Samson goes out as the last lock falls. He wakes and stands, easy, unaware, as the Philistines fill the doorway.
VO: The Philistines seized him, gouged out his eyes, and set him grinding at a mill in Gaza. [Judges 16:21]
VISUAL: The prison mill from the opening shot, cold light (no gore).
VO: At their feast he prayed, "strengthen me only this once," and the house came down on them all. [Judges 16:23–30]
VISUAL: The house of Dagon: blind Samson led by a young man between laughing Philistines; a faint gold returns on his hands at the two middle pillars as he prays; the house falls in stone and dust (no bodies). Tag: JUDGES 16:28–30.

## Turn
VO: Now read that line again: "he did not know." Through the lion, the jawbone, the prostitute and the gates, his strength never left him. So when the LORD left, Samson didn't even notice. [Judges 14:6, 15:14–15, 16:1–3, 16:19–20]
VISUAL: Locked hold on the 16:20 card; behind it, quick ghosts of the lion, the jawbone and the gates, each lit gold, then the dark room at Delilah's.

## Landing
VO: So what's the lesson of Samson? Being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God. Samson found out too late. [Judges 14:3, 16:20–21; 21:25]
VISUAL: Back to the opening frame: the mill in Gaza, now empty; first light falls across the floor where a few cut hairs lie. End card.

## Value
- The angel said Samson would "begin to save Israel" [Judges 13:5]
- The Spirit of the LORD rushed on him for the lion, the thirty men and the thousand men [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14]
- In Gaza he slept with a prostitute, then pulled up the city gates and carried them up a hill, and his strength did not fail [Judges 16:1–3]
- He woke up expecting to "go out as at other times," but "he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- What we learn: being gifted by God isn't the same as walking with God [Judges 14:3, 16:20–21; 21:25]
