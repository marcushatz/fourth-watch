## Value
- The text gives the reason itself: she "pressed him hard with her words day after day," "his soul was vexed to death," and "he told her all his heart" [Judges 16:16–17]
- His third lie was already about his hair: weave the seven locks into the web [Judges 16:13]
- She shouted "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" four times; the first three times he simply broke free [Judges 16:9, 16:12, 16:14, 16:20]
- He said himself, "If my head is shaved, then my strength will leave me," yet woke saying, "I will go out as at other times" [Judges 16:17, 16:20]
- "He did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- What we learn: when a risk keeps costing nothing, you stop believing it ever will; nothing happens, until it does [Judges 16:20–21]
