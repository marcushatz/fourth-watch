## Value
- Delilah tried three times before the haircut; each time she shouted "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" and each time he broke free [Judges 16:6–14]
- The fourth time he expected to "go out as at other times", but "he did not know that the LORD had left him" [Judges 16:20]
- His strength was the LORD's: the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon him at the lion, Ashkelon and Lehi, and his strength left when the LORD left [Judges 14:6, 14:19, 15:14, 16:19–20]
- His only recorded prayer for strength came at the very end, blind, and God answered [Judges 16:28–30]
- Facing a Philistine, David said it before the fight: "the battle is the LORD's" [1 Samuel 17:47]
- What we learn: you can get so used to God's strength that you don't notice when it's gone; Samson only saw it when he was blind [Judges 16:20–21, 16:28]
