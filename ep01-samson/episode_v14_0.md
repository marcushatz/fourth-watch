# Why is Samson's story even in the Bible?
series: ASK THE TEXT
target_seconds: 70
answer: Samson's life sketches Jesus, and every place the sketch breaks shows the Savior Samson couldn't be. Samson could only begin to save; Jesus finished it.

## Value
- Samson's own people, 3,000 men of Judah, bound him and handed him to the Philistines; Pilate told Jesus, "Your own nation… delivered you over to me" [Judges 15:11–13; John 18:35]
- Blind Samson was brought out to entertain the Philistines; Jesus was blindfolded and mocked, "Prophesy! Who is it that struck you?" [Judges 16:21, 16:25; Luke 22:63–64]
- Samson killed more in his death than in his life; Jesus "through death" destroyed "the one who has the power of death" [Judges 16:30; Hebrews 2:14]
- Samson said he would "shake myself free" and couldn't; Jesus could have appealed for "more than twelve legions of angels" and didn't [Judges 16:20–21; Matthew 26:53]
- Samson's last prayer was to be "avenged… for my two eyes"; Jesus prayed, "Father, forgive them" [Judges 16:28; Luke 23:34]
- The angel said Samson would "begin to save Israel"; the angel said Jesus "will save his people from their sins" [Judges 13:5; Matthew 1:21]
