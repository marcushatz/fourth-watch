## Hook
VO: Who gave God a name in Genesis? [Genesis 16:13]
ON-SCREEN: WHO GAVE GOD A NAME IN GENESIS?

## Stakes
VO: Not Abraham. Not Jacob. A pregnant slave, running through the wilderness. [Genesis 16:4] [Genesis 16:6]

## Story
VO: Her name was Hagar. Abraham and Sarah never say it. She's "my servant," "your servant," "this slave woman." [Genesis 16:2] [Genesis 16:6] [Genesis 21:10]
VO: Sarah gave her to Abraham to bear a child. Then Sarah "dealt harshly with her," and she ran. [Genesis 16:2] [Genesis 16:3] [Genesis 16:6]

## Turn
VO: In the wilderness, the angel of the LORD finds her, and his first word is "Hagar." [Genesis 16:7] [Genesis 16:8]
VO: Then comes the hardest line in the story: "Return to your mistress and submit to her." [Genesis 16:9]
VO: It doesn't get explained, and it isn't a rescue. [Genesis 16:9]
VO: But he tells her "the LORD has listened to your affliction." [Genesis 16:11]
VO: And she answers with a name for God: "You are a God of seeing." [Genesis 16:13]
VERSE: "You are a God of seeing." — Genesis 16:13 (ESV)
VO: The woman nobody would name had named God. [Genesis 16:13]

## Landing
VO: Years later, Abraham sends her away with her son, some bread and a skin of water. [Genesis 21:14]
VO: The water runs out. She leaves the boy under a bush and walks a bowshot away. [Genesis 21:15] [Genesis 21:16]
VO: "Let me not look on the death of the child." [Genesis 21:16]
VO: The woman who named the God of seeing can't bear to look. [Genesis 16:13] [Genesis 21:16]
VO: She weeps. And the voice comes again: "What troubles you, Hagar?" [Genesis 21:16] [Genesis 21:17]
VO: "Then God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water." [Genesis 21:19]


## Value
- Abraham and Sarah never say Hagar's name in their recorded words [Genesis 16:2] [Genesis 21:10]
- Hagar gives God a name after being told to go back [Genesis 16:9] [Genesis 16:13]
- A mother walks away so she won't watch her son die [Genesis 21:16]
